# COST3 R7, 28 Nov 2026: the refusal prices from the blocks into the model

Claude in the chat, in its sandbox, snax-forge-experiments on `ry/cost3` from R6c's `e858198`. R6 and R6c measured a refused request on the streamer and interconnect blocks alone (X116). This round puts their prices into the tables and the fits that snax-forge imports, and checks them on the two refusal pairs they were never fitted on.

**Result.** A refused request costs a reader its own `r_refused` (0.072–0.262 of a lane's grant, falling with depth and channels) and the interconnect 0.0018 of an access. With the readers' mean, the ablation's last step is 0.91–1.08 of the netlist on all 17 designs. The refusal pairs move −9.9 % against −10.1 % (mvt) and −0.5 % against +1.3 % (bicg int64). The top-1 is the netlist's in every kernel. R1's fit on the mvt pair gave −8.7 and −0.9.

## What changed

- `report/strm_table.py`: `streamer.csv` gains `r_refused`, each reader's $r$ from `refusal.json`, clamped at 0. `xbar.csv` is unchanged.
- `report/model_fit.py`: `refusal` comes from `refusal.json` (`source: blocks`).
  - `r_strm` is the readers' mean, 0.1505; snax-forge prices a mover at its own reader's `r_refused` and keeps the mean for a table without the column.
  - `r_xbar` is the interconnect's refused-against-apart $r_{more}$, 0.0018 ($r_{first}$ −0.0020).
  - The mvt pair's own fit stays beside it as a check (streamers 0.0847, crossbar −0.0262), and both pairs are modelled with the blocks' $r$.
- `report/ablation.py`: a mover's refusals at its own $r$ where the rerun's prices carry one (snax-forge's prices after this round; R8's runs), at the mean otherwise. So the table below uses the mean.
- Docs: X116's result, `cost_tables.md`, "A refused request".
