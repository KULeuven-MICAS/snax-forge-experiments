# CSYN2 R3, 27 Nov 2026: tier A at three levels

Claude in the chat, on R2's committed reports (`ry/csyn2`, after R3a), snax-forge `3c4d4bc`'s `paper/data/` as the estimate. `cluster-breakdown` and `cluster-energy` were run again for the 14 new points only; the dot points' entries are kept as committed (their inputs changed since, which is not this task's). `cluster-energy` now keeps leakage per group and window beside the dynamic energy (X106); `tier-check` (new) lays the three levels side by side over the program window. Nothing is tuned; this note is for the morning read.

## What the numbers say

- **Cycles.** SNAX-GVSoC's program window is 0.3–3.6 % short of the RTL's (the netlist's equal it, R2) in every design, and τ between the two is 1.00 in every kernel and pooled. The estimate counts the accelerator runs only (E4S1 R3): against the netlist's run windows it is 6–12 % short where nothing conflicts; `mvt_contiguous_w8` is 73 % short (bank conflicts the graph does not see, as `cluster_limits` shows); the two DMA-copy forms' estimate holds the copy, which the netlist's run windows leave out.
- **The picks with leakage agree at every level.** With the design's leakage (view C) and with the fixed cluster (view D), all three levels pick the read-once form in every kernel; τ estimate–netlist 0.90–1.00 per kernel, 0.86–0.90 pooled.
- **Dynamic alone, two picks move at the netlist.** The model (estimate and SNAX-GVSoC alike) picks `mvt_column` over `mvt_read_once` (26.2 against 26.9 nJ, 2.6 % apart) and `bicg_two_pass` over `bicg_read_once` (25.6 against 26.3 nJ); the netlist picks the read-once forms (40.5 against 44.5 nJ, 36.6 against 40.8 nJ), also within the run windows alone (29.5 against 33.0, 27.4 against 31.4 nJ). The longer form pays its clock tree and idle logic for twice the cycles, which the event-priced model does not charge. So at gate level the faster form wins even before leakage.
- **Dynamic energy per part, netlist against the model.** The model is low on the int8 forms (the netlist 1.26–1.77× the estimate over the window): estimate over netlist 0.45–0.65 on the streamers and on the crossbar, 0.61–0.77 on the banks, and high on the datapath (1.30–1.46, but 0.83 and 0.93 in the two forms with the most units). Outside the run windows (DMA in and out, configuration) the netlist spends 8–20 nJ that the model prices at nothing; the DMA moving inputs and outputs is 7–49 nJ at the netlist and 0 in the model, which prices only a copy's bursts. On the DMA copies the model is high (0.83× at the netlist), mostly the DMA (1.22×) and the banks (1.31×).
- **Leakage.** The estimate's six parts are 5–15 % above PT's (4 231 against 3 864 µW for `mvt_column`): the streamers and the crossbar by area density. The fixed parts hold across all 14 points, as expected for the same RTL: the core 845–850 µW (`cluster_fixed.json` 847), the rest 4 067–4 122 (4 017), the DMA 1 351–1 375 (1 407): within 1–3 %, so one point stands for all and `cluster_fixed.json` can stay.
- **Area.** The whole cluster within −0.4 to +2.0 % of the estimate in every design; by part, the streamers 6–35 % above the estimate, the crossbar 5–11 % below.
- **Cycle gaps over 20 %** between SNAX-GVSoC and the RTL are only in short windows (DMA transfers of 24–90 cycles, streamer configuration, two short axpby runs of 25–45 cycles): the DMA's transfers 20–40 % longer on the RTL, the streamer configuration 30–40 % shorter. Listed below for the morning.
