## Against the plan's criteria

- **±10 % on every design:** at step `copy` five designs are over (1.10–1.14: `bicg_read_once`, `atax_read_once`, `gesummv_x_read_once`, both copies); the range is 0.94–1.14. With the DMA's bank accesses at $e_{bank,dma}$ = 4.17 pJ (step `dma_banks`, a candidate, not agreed) every design is within 0.92–1.09.
- **The refusal pairs within 3 points:** mvt contiguous → padded −8.7 % against −10.4 %, bicg int64 sequential → pipeline −0.9 % against +1.9 % (2.8 points). Pass. The int8 pipelines: −11.4 against −12.3, −10.0 against −10.7.
- **Top-1 and τ ≥ 0.9:** top-1 is the netlist's in every kernel (bicg's netlist ties `bicg_read_once` and `bicg_two_pass_pipeline` within 1 %, the model picks the latter). bicg's τ is 0.80 with no pair ordered oppositely: the netlist's tie and one tie in the model count as neither, so 0.90 is bicg's best possible. The criterion should read "no pair ordered oppositely".
- **What is left** is per part, not a missing event: the datapath is priced 1.28–1.46 of the netlist on eleven of the thirteen int8 forms (0.83 and 0.93 on `atax_column_shared_movers` and `gesummv_literal`; char's int8 blocks), the streamers 0.69–0.92 (their busy beats), CACTI's banks high on the DMA's accesses (1.31 on the copies before `dma_banks`).
