# PWR1 R2 run note (2026-11-12, micas-lapserv1)

PWR1 measures the one-core cluster's dynamic energy on `dot3_w16` (dot_3, W = 16, N = 2048) per program section. R1 (`2026-11-12_pwr1_r1.md`) built part 1 of `pixi run cluster pwr <point>`: six windows from `obs_o`'s marks and one checked SAIF each. This round adds part 2: one PrimePower session on the whole cluster's netlist, window after window, into `results/cluster/nangate45/power_pt.csv` and `power_reports/dot3_w16.zip`. The breakdown into X72's groups and the comparison with HWCOST are R3's.

**Result:** `cluster pwr dot3_w16: PASS, 6 windows, span 901 cycles, E_span 491.1 nJ (macros 7 %), run window 124.0 nJ, annotated 100.0–100.0 % of driven nets`. Part 1 was skipped (its `pwr/check.json` PASS for the same netlist, quiet ELF and snax_cluster commit). Every window passes every check: the wire load 0 (PT1), 732,296 of 732,298 nets annotated from the SAIF and the 2 left at Z for the whole window in it (PT6), every row of `power.rpt` parsed with the seven groups only, the hierarchy's top equal to `power.rpt`'s total, the SAIF's 1,437,059 entries as part 1 counted them. One thing looks off and is open for R3: the flop energy is lower in the run window than in the quiet ones (below).

## For R3

- The flop energy is lower in the run window than in the quiet ones: e_flop 402.8 pJ per cycle against 439.6 to 454.4, the clock_network group 38.9 mW against 42.4 to 44.5 while register rises by about 1 mW only (PT3's shift from clock to register would keep their sum). By instance, the streamers (`i_snax_forge_dot_3_dot_streamer_wrapper`) take 3.4 mW less internal power in the run window than in acc_config (12.0 against 15.4 mW). Not traced; a likely cause is the flops' clock-pin internal power under `when` conditions on their D and Q, which averaged mode takes from each net's static probability (PT7's mechanism). Worth a look before the groups are read.
- The hierarchy's names: rebuilt from the indentation as `pwr.pt_hierarchy`, they are DC's `area_hierarchy.rpt` names, so `groups.json` applies unchanged; rows with `N/A` are the 133 empty instances.
- The macros are PT's black_box group (memory 0), in averaged mode (PITFALLS PT7's bias on `when`-conditioned read and write energy); `e_macro_pj` is that group. The TCDM banks hold almost all of it in the run window (`i_snitch_data_mem` 7.84 mW internal).
- `tx_nets` per window is in `power_pt.csv` (VS2): 104,919 and 96,751 in the two DMA windows that carry X; the energies there miss at most one toggle per net leaving X for most of them.
- Leakage is in the reports (95.5 mW in the run window, most of it the macros') and out of every column (X13).
