# Run note: GE1 R8, the dynamic energy beside the static estimate and the netlist, 17 Nov 2026

R3 and R7 ran dot_3 at N = 2048, W = 2 to 32 through snax-forge's energy run (`pixi run flow ... --energy`, its D177) at the crossbar's prices by input kind (its D178, X93), into `results/cluster/nangate45/ge/`; R7 priced the static estimate again (`hwcost/`). **Result:** below the bank bound the model's counts are the graph's (W refused requests, 3 fill cycles), so GE1 equals the static estimate within 0.15 % and both are 1.14 to 1.18 of the netlist's five parts, the datapath's random-data price (1.61 to 1.63) being most of it; the crossbar, priced by input kind, is 0.84 to 0.99 (it was 1.26 to 1.48). ## Reading

- **Below the bound (W ≤ 16)** the counts carry nothing new for dot_3: GE1 adds W refusals (reader B once across its lanes) and 3 fill cycles to the graph's counts. The remaining gap is the prices: the datapath at 1.61 to 1.63 (the tables' random 64-bit operands against dot_3's values in [−1000, 1000), accepted as a known limit), control 1.07 to 1.16 (0.1 % of the five parts).
- **At W = 32** GE1 sees the bank conflicts: 8161 requests for 4097 words, 131 busy cycles for 65. Its crossbar is 0.83 of the netlist's (the W = 16 ratio, 0.84: a refused request at a read's price fits the crossbar), its streamers 1.26 (0.95 at W = 16: a refused request seems to cost a streamer less than a granted beat), its datapath 1.06, as the static one (fires are fixed by the program; the netlist's datapath is 7.7 nJ above W = 16, which a count of fires cannot see).
- **The ranking** over W: the netlist's cheapest are W = 4 and 8 (49.47, 49.48 nJ), GE1's and the static's W = 16; the datapath's price, 1.6× at W ≤ 16 and 1.06 at W = 32, sets that order.
- **Not counted (D167):** the DMA's and the cores' traffic, the other windows (their counts are in the ge files).
