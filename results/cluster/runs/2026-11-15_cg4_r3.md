# CG4 R3, 15 Nov 2026: the gated cluster priced by snax-forge's own HWCOST

Claude in the chat, on `ry/cg4` after R1, with snax-forge's `ry/cg4` at its R2 (tag `cg4-r2`): snax-forge now reads the clock-gated tables this repository's R1 left (`import-costs`) and prices a unit by its fires only (snax-forge D154 · D172). The five gated cluster points are priced again by snax-forge itself, so `cg2-reprice`'s second pricing (retired in R1) is no longer needed.

## What changed

- `pixi run cluster-hwcost dot3_w{2,4,8,16,32}_cg` from a clean snax-forge checkout at R2's commit: the five `hwcost/*.json`, each naming that commit.
- `cluster-breakdown` and `cluster-energy` again: `breakdown.json` and `energy.json` change only in their HWCOST columns and the commit they name.
- `cluster-sweep`: `sweep.csv` and its four figures replace CG3's `sweep_gated.*`. The CSV equals CG3's value for value; only the source label changed (`hwcost_cg` to `hwcost`). The figures were drawn in the chat's sandbox with DejaVu Sans set through `MATPLOTLIBRC`.

## The check

snax-forge's HWCOST against `cg2-reprice`'s gated pricing at CG3 (`hwcost_cg.csv`, kept in snax-forge as `tests/estimate/fixtures/dot3_gated.json`), per W and part:

- Area: equal to within 4e-7 µm² at every W and part.
- Energy: the datapath is 4.19e-6 nJ lower at every W, the hold term snax-forge dropped (dot 3's tree register, 64 bits idle one cycle at 0.0655 fJ); control within 5e-10 nJ (rounding); streamers, crossbar and memory equal.

The five parts' energy at each W, before (snax-forge on the ungated tables) and now:

| W | before (nJ) | now (nJ) | of which streamers, before → now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 72.467 | 63.493 | 20.919 → 12.213 |
| 4 | 71.000 | 62.107 | 20.507 → 11.723 |
| 8 | 70.414 | 61.599 | 20.310 → 11.523 |
| 16 | 69.923 | 61.132 | 20.215 → 11.411 |
| 32 | 69.928 | 61.149 | 20.167 → 11.356 |

Gating takes about 43 % off the streamers, the reader's FIFO no longer clocked while it holds. The crossbar rises 0.5 % (11.223 to 11.277 nJ) and the datapath falls 0.1 to 1.3 %. The gated runs' part-by-part ratios are CG3's (`2026-11-14_cg3_r2.md`); this round changes the price, not the measurement.
