## Per W, ungated against gated

Each row is one point's run window `5_run` (the same cycles for both twins). "Five parts" are what HWCOST prices (datapath, control, streamers, crossbar, memory), measured in the netlist; "the rest" is the run window outside them (core, DMA, I-cache, the cluster's glue); "floor × cycles" is the idle floor (`4_acc_config`, every group) times the run window's cycles. HWCOST is snax-forge's on the ungated tables for an ungated point and the gated tables' (`hwcost_cg.csv`) for a gated one. "Error without datapath" is the sum of |HWCOST − netlist| over control, streamers, crossbar and memory, in nJ: the datapath is left out because its gap is the characterization stimulus (rand64, CG2 topic 6), the same in both sets.

Example, W = 8 gated: the run window is 62.23 nJ over 322 cycles; the five parts are 49.48 nJ (80 %); the rest, 12.74 nJ, is close to the idle floor's 31.5 pJ per cycle times 322 cycles (10.13 nJ); HWCOST on the gated tables gives 61.60 nJ, 1.245 times the five parts, nearly all of the excess the datapath's (24.17 against 14.83); without the datapath HWCOST misses by 3.79 nJ in all, against 9.26 nJ ungated.

## What the sweep shows

- **Area.** Gating takes 6.0 to 7.7 % off the cluster (more at larger W) and 98.55 to 98.58 % of the registers are gated at every W, each gate a `CLKGATE_X1`. The gated tables price the five parts' area within 2.3 % at every W (0.994 to 1.023), the ungated tables within 1.1 %; per part, the streamers move from 0.80–0.95 (ungated) to 0.83–1.02 (gated), the crossbar stays 5 to 12 % high in both (its table is a full crossbar), datapath and memory within 0.1 %.
- **Energy, W = 2 to 16.** The same picture at every W as CG2 found at W = 8. The gated streamers agree with the gated tables (0.93 to 0.97), the ungated ones did not (0.66 to 0.77: the idle clock HWCOST does not price). Memory is 0.99 in both, control is a fraction of a nJ in both. The datapath (1.61 to 1.63) and the crossbar (1.23 to 1.49) are high in both sets, by the same amount: the characterization stimulus, not the clock. Without the datapath, HWCOST's error falls from 9.3–14.8 nJ ungated to 3.1–4.8 nJ gated.
- **The five-part totals.** Ungated, HWCOST lands closer on the total (1.03 to 1.10) only because the streamers' shortfall cancels the datapath's excess; gated, the total is 1.21 to 1.26 high, and that excess is the datapath's (+9.0 to +9.5 nJ) and the crossbar's (+2.3 to +3.7 nJ).
- **What HWCOST covers.** The five parts are 64 to 91 % of the gated run window against 18 to 65 % ungated. The rest of the gated window follows the cluster's idle floor (30 to 40 pJ per cycle) times the run's cycles; ungated the floor is 323 to 594 pJ per cycle.
- **W = 32 is a case of its own, gated or not.** Its run takes 190 cycles for 64 fires (W = 16: 185 cycles for 128), so most of its cycles are stalls (2 × 32 read channels and one write channel on 32 banks); the accelerator counts 118 busy cycles (`mult_dot`). HWCOST prices beats, not stalls, and at W = 32 it is low on the streamers (0.63 gated, 0.41 ungated), the crossbar (0.62, 0.60) and the five parts (0.83, 0.66), while the datapath measures 22.6 nJ against about 15 at W ≤ 16. The streamers and the crossbar are also extrapolated there (X85). Gating halves the gap, but the missing term is the stalls'.

## R2's odd items

1. No `cg3-r1` tag on the server or origin: the tag was made in the chat's clone, local as every round's tag (project rule: a local tag per round, not pushed).
2. `dot3_w16` was bundled at snax-forge `299ef010`, not `b29bf0c0`: its ELFs, window cycles and HWCOST equal `dot3_w16_cg`'s, so the pair is the same program at the same price. Recorded; nothing to redo.
3. The top-level PNGs drawn in the chat's sandbox are not byte-equal on the server (fonts; known since CG1 R1): R2 restored them from git, correctly. Figures are made on the server only.
4. `dot3_w16_cg` and `dot3_w32_cg` share their slack to the last digit: every gated point's worst path is Snitch's `pc_q` to its register file, outside the accelerator (slack +1.61 to +1.68 ns at every gated W).
5. D123 NEAR at W = 32 (`mult_dot` 118 against SNAX-GVSoC's 131, 7.6 %), as ungated; reported under X84, and the rtl step's D123 verdict is PASS with a NEAR note as before.
