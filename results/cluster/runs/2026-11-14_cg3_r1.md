- X85, `report/cg2_reprice.py`: W = 32 priced, the extrapolated fields named; `vs_cluster` for every W with a `dot3_w<W>_cg` in `energy.json` and `breakdown.json`, beside snax-forge's HWCOST against the ungated twin, energy and area. `results/cluster/nangate45/hwcost_cg.csv` gains W = 32's ten rows; W = 2 to 16's 40 rows are byte-equal to CG2's.
- X85, `report/cluster_sweep.py`: `--tables gated` takes each point's HWCOST five parts from `hwcost_cg.csv`'s gated rows at its W; `sweep.csv`'s source is then `hwcost_cg`, the figures' title says "HWCOST, gated tables". Without the flag nothing changes: the recipe sweep and `cg1/` remade in the sandbox give byte-equal `sweep.csv` files.
- `cluster/batch.sh`: its folder from `BATCH_OUT` (default `tmp/overnight-1` as before).
- Docs: X84 and X85 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `cluster/README.md` (points, the `gls` row, the gated sweep's command), `README.md`'s command list.
