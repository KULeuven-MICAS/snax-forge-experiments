# PWR1 R3 run note (2026-11-12, Claude in the chat)

**Result: `dot3_w16`'s one-core cluster spends 491.1 nJ of dynamic energy over the measured span (901 cycles: DMA in, streamer and accelerator configuration, the run, DMA out), 124.0 nJ of it in the run window (185 cycles).** About 450 of the run window's 670 pJ per cycle is the idle floor: the clock of about 63k flops that are never gated, there whether anything moves or not. Above that floor the run adds 219.8 pJ per cycle, almost all in four groups: the TCDM banks (+79.2), the accelerator's datapath (+79.9), the TCDM sparse interconnect (+46.2) and the TCDM bank logic (+21.4). Against HWCOST's five energy parts the memory and the interconnect hold about the same shares (memory 20.7 % against 22.0 %, crossbar 16.1 % against 13.7 %), while HWCOST ranks the datapath first (34.2 % against 22.4 %) and the streamers second (28.9 % against 41.7 %). HWCOST prices the datapath on random 64-bit data (`rand64 seed 0`): the shell alone on that data (`done/dot/forge3_w16`, X23) takes 23.43 nJ, close to HWCOST's 23.92, against 15.15 nJ in the cluster on the program's data. The streamers in the netlist are almost all clock (154.2 pJ per cycle idle, 149.8 running). HWCOST is a first-order estimate with no bit-level switching, so the absolute numbers are not expected to match; X73–X76 are now final.

## Reading

- **The idle floor.** Every window costs at least about 445 pJ per cycle: the streamers (154), AXI (155), the DMA (59–61), the TCDM bank logic (21), the cores and the I-cache logic (about 20 each). It is the clock of flops that are never gated; averaged mode's clock in the idle window matches the library's per-flop clock-pin energy counted by hand (R2b, PITFALLS PT10).
- **The run phase above idle** is the banks (14.65 nJ, all of their energy), the datapath (14.79 nJ), the interconnect (8.56 nJ) and the bank logic (3.97 nJ). The streamers' part above idle is −0.81 nJ: their FIFOs are flops, clocked every cycle in every window, and a flop about to toggle books part of its clock-edge energy under register rather than clock (X11), so the streamers' run energy sits at about their idle floor. It is kept as measured (R2b).
- **The DMA windows** carry the AXI and DMA groups' traffic (+15 to +18 pJ per cycle on AXI, +23 to +26 on the DMA against the idle window) and the banks' writes (28–29 pJ per cycle). They have many nets with X time (VS2, `tx_nets` 104,919 and 96,751 in windows 1 and 6), a small low bias on their activity.
- **HWCOST against the run phase.** The five parts sum to 69.92 nJ against 66.44 nJ in the run window and 37.19 nJ above idle. Memory: 14.50 against 14.65 nJ (the same 4,096 reads, X75). Crossbar: 11.22 against 9.11 nJ (HWCOST +23 %, its area +10.6 %, X72). Datapath: 23.92 against 14.88 nJ; the alone row points at the stimulus (random 64-bit operands toggle the multipliers far more than the program's data). Streamers: 20.21 against 27.71 nJ, the netlist's figure almost all clock. Neither side's ranking is a check: HWCOST is an estimate (Ryan, 12 Nov).
