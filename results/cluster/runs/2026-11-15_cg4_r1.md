## The gated cost tables' numbers

The examples snax-forge's `tests/char/test_tables.py` pins, from `report/strm_table.py` on the gated files:

| Example | Query | Before (ungated) | Now (gated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | reader $D$ 8, $L$ 2, $C$ 3 | 15.3916 pJ, 15,424.9 µm² | 8.7904 pJ, 12,794.7 µm² |
| 2 | reader $D$ 6, $L$ 3, $C$ 3 | 13.5002 pJ | 8.2413 pJ |
| 3 | xbar $n$ 12, $b$ 32 | 3.4030 pJ, 61,450.9 µm² | 3.4495 pJ, 61,698.0 µm² |
| 4 | xbar $n$ 40, $b$ 32 | 2.7394 pJ, 210,443.8 µm² | 2.7526 pJ, 210,357.6 µm² |
| 5 | factor form, $n$ 36, $b$ 32, $n_{ro}$ 32, $n_{wo}$ 1 | 119,308 µm² | 119,390 µm² |
| 6 | factor form, $n$ 16, $b$ 16, all read-only | 25,278 µm² | 25,376 µm² |

Clock gating roughly halves the reader's energy per beat and leaves the interconnect within 2 % (its flops are few). The interconnect against the cluster: the one-core `dot3_w16_cg`'s `sparse_interconnect_i` is 103,257 µm², the factor form +10.3 % and the crosspoint form +7.2 % (ungated, the two-core cluster: +10.8 % and +7.5 %).

Leave-one-out, gated: reader area 0.56 % mean (worst −1.01 %), reader energy 5.09 % (worst −11.42 % at $D$ 4, $L$ 6, $C$ 6), interconnect area 1.05 % (+3.14 %), interconnect energy in $1/n$ 2.61 % (+11.68 %, the 64-bank dip). The fits table was recomputed with each exponent fitted over a grid; on the ungated files this reproduces the old table but for $\alpha + \beta\thinspace e^{-n/\tau}$ (3.8 % and +12.9 % against the 5.9 % and +21.9 % written before, a local minimum in the old fit). No fit beats the table on the mean.
