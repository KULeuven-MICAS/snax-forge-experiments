- **Dynamic alone the picks still split:** the estimate ties `mvt_column` and `bicg_two_pass` with their pipelined twins and SNAX-GVSoC picks the sequential forms (1.2 % apart), the netlist `mvt_column_pipeline` (39.0 against 44.5 nJ) and `bicg_read_once` tied with `bicg_two_pass_pipeline` (36.6, 36.4 nJ). The netlist's −11 to −12 % from pipelining is its idle floor over fewer cycles, which the event-priced model does not charge (R3).
- **Cycles.** SNAX-GVSoC's program window against the netlist's: 909 against 891 (+2.0 %), 853 against 850 (+0.4 %), 1 561 against 1 580 (−1.2 %); τ GVSoC–netlist 1.00 in every kernel, 0.97 pooled. The estimate counts the accelerator runs alone (514 cycles for each pipelined form).
- **The contiguous pipelined bicg** (`bicg_pipeline_contiguous_w8`) conflicts more than its sequential twin: SNAX-GVSoC +14 % dynamic, the netlist +2 % (with 20 % fewer cycles), the estimate 0 %. The refused-request price overshoots as on `mvt_contiguous_w8` (COST3).
- **Leakage and area** of the pipelined points equal their twins' per part within 1 % (same netlist, a window of another length).

## The quiet span against the default build

R4b found each pipelined point's quiet span 22–34 cycles (2–3 %) under the default build's `cycles`. R2's sequential points with the same waits in both builds show the same: `mvt_column` 1 601 against 1 646, `bicg_two_pass` 1 504 against 1 532, `bicg_sequential_contiguous_w8` 1 983 against 2 018, the copies 2 and 12 cycles. The default build's `cycles` runs from `t0 = snrt_mcycle()` in `main` to the read in `forge_check`; the quiet span from the first mark, inside the core's function after the call through `forge_cores[]` and its first instructions, to the check's mark after that read. The difference is the call into the core's function (and its first fetches) less the call into `forge_check`, a few tens of cycles, the same in every point; the quiet span is what every level is compared on. The read-once forms' quiet spans are longer than their default builds (the quiet build waits on every start, D135), which is why R2's table shows both signs. Nothing changes; recorded so the 2–3 % is not read as a model gap.
