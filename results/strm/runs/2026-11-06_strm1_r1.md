# STRM1 R1: the reader and interconnect grid (2026-11-06)

**All 195 points are `ok` through DC, and all 185 power points (the 168 readers and the 17 interconnects with inputs <= banks) are `ok` through power.** Every point's flops equal the bits its RTL declares (0 OPT-1207), every point meets 10 ns (worst slack 5.46 ns for a reader, 6.33 ns for the interconnect), and every power run passed its gates with 100 % of the nets annotated. The tables snax-forge's cost model reads are `results/strm/nangate45/streamer.csv` and `xbar.csv`, with the reference interpolation in `report/strm_table.py` (X53).
- **Readers:** 2,138 to 152,598 um2 and 2.69 to 129.41 pJ per beat.
- **Interconnects:** 10,362 to 669,961 um2 and 2.60 to 10.17 pJ per access.

R1 found that a reader's loop stride is 16 bits although its port has 17 (PITFALLS SC9): the stimulus puts the larger bounds outermost. It also found that a 1-channel reader has no spatial-stride port (PITFALLS SC2). Leave-one-out errors: area within 3.04 % everywhere, reader energy mean 4.0 % (worst -8.7 %), interconnect energy mean 9.0 % (worst +24.3 %, at few inputs, where the energy goes as 1 / inputs).

## Odd things (PITFALLS)

- **SC9, new:** a reader's loop stride is 16 bits, not the 17 its port has. `ProgrammableCounter`'s `step` is `width - 1` bits, so a stride of 65,536 B is 0. With the larger bounds innermost, the outermost stride at 16 channels and 6 or 7 loops is 8 x 16 x 512 = 65,536 B, and half of the requests went to the wrong address with correct data and beat counts. The fix: the larger bounds outermost (largest stride 32,768 B) and a guard in `sim/sources/strm.py` that refuses a stride over 65,535 B.
- **SC2, extended:** at 1 channel the reader has no `io_aguCfg_spatialStrides_0`; the stride multiplies only channel 0's index, so firtool drops it. The TB now connects what the netlist has, and the port check in both directions caught it before any simulation.
- **Q11, new:** `process::self().srandom()` in an `initial` block does not seed the `always` blocks' `$urandom`. R0's TB had it and R1's seed 0 gave the same energy as R0's seed 1 to every digit. The seed is now `vsim -sv_seed`.
- **SC1 to SC8** come from R0's candidates, written into `docs/PITFALLS.md` in this round.
- **vcd2saif dominates the interconnect's power runs** when 20 run at once: 803 s against 10.5 s alone for a smaller point in R0. It reads the large VCDs from the shared disk.
- **The 64-bank interconnect's dip at 32 inputs** (4.13 pJ against 4.61 at 24 and 4.59 at 40) is measured, not explained; the gates passed there as everywhere.
