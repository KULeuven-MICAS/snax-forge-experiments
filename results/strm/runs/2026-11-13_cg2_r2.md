## Anything odd, for Ryan

- **e_hold is an ICG's, not a cell's.** A gated block's idle energy is its ICGs' alone, 4.90 fJ per ICG per cycle at every point (idle e_dyn 0.0049 pJ with one ICG, mac, serial, tree, link d1). Per sequential cell it is 4.90 fJ / (cells per ICG): 0.0754 fJ with 64 bits behind the gate, 0.0096 with 512 (mac/acc_w8), 0.0024 with 2,048 (mac/acc_w32). X83's per-cell mean, 0.0655 fJ, therefore spreads 111 %, and the ctrl counter factor (1.06 → 115.67) keeps E_ctrl only because it is rescaled. A price per ICG (about 4.9 fJ per cycle) would fit these 14 points exactly; the per-cell constant over- or underprices the idle energy of a register by up to 30× (absolute: a few fJ per cycle per gate). X83 calls the per-cell rule a first assumption.
- **One ICG for 2,048 flops.** mac/acc_w8 and acc_w32 and link/d1_w8 and d1_w32 put every bit behind a single ICG (513 and 2,049 sequential cells). DC's own clock power for the two w32 blocks is about 620 pJ per cycle against 13 pJ ungated (one CLKGATE_X1 driving 2,048 clock pins with no CTS); DC reports no max-transition net, as the clock net is ideal to it. PT, which the tables use, does not show it: e_dyn gated / ungated 0.953 (link/d1_w32) and 1.003 (mac/acc_w32 busy). A real layout would buffer that net; the tables carry no clock-tree cost either way.
- **Busy energy goes up slightly behind a gate.** e_load per cell is 3.8 % higher gated (17.82 against 17.17 fJ) and the busy e_bit 1.00 to 1.06× ungated: a register loaded every cycle still clocks its ICG. Blocks that load every cycle gain nothing: lanes 1.000, mac busy 1.003 to 1.004, serial busy 0.97 to 1.01, link d1 (one stage, loaded every cycle) 0.95.
- **The crossbar gets slightly worse.** xbar area +0.2 to 0.4 %, e_access +0.5 to 1.3 %, and DC gates only 128 of 256, 160 of 384 and 384 of 896 registers: the probe's stimulus keeps every input busy every cycle, so there is nothing to gate away. The cluster's crossbar (busy far less) may differ; CG1's dot3_w8_cg run window had the crossbar at 8.04 nJ against HWCOST's gated 11.28.
- **The streamer saves most where it is deep.** e_beat gated / ungated falls with FIFO depth: 0.84 (d2_l1_c1), 0.76 (d2_l7_c16), 0.57 to 0.61 (depth 8), 0.46 to 0.49 (depth 16); area 0.81 to 0.83 at every point (comb 0.47 to 0.64: DC drops the enable muxes). The depth-2 points leave more registers ungated than the others (7 of 223 and 80 of 2,992, about 3 %, against 0.2 to 0.4 % elsewhere).
- **The datapath stays as priced.** HWCOST's dot_3 datapath is 24.17 nJ gated against the gated cluster's 14.83 nJ at W = 8 (1.629): the mul lanes are combinational (ratio 1.000) and the TreeAccumulate's registers load every busy cycle, so gating the blocks alone changes the datapath by under 1 %; the gap is the char model's, not the gating's.
- **One ungated char row moved.** Writing the gated rows, syn.py rewrote `char_synth.csv` from `syn/runs/char/nangate45/summary.csv`, which holds a local rerun of the ungated link/d4_w1 from 6 Nov that was never committed: that row's `date` is now 6 Nov (was 3 Nov) and it has the four slack columns the others leave empty; every other value is equal. The other ten CSVs' ungated rows are identical to main's; each gained only `clk_gate` and its gated rows.
- **char power's annotated column.** `char_power.csv` has no `annotated` column, so `cg2-check pairs` leaves it empty for char; the numbers above come from the run zips.
