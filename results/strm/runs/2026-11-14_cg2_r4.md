- **Streamers.** The part gating was for. Ungated, HWCOST's 20.31 nJ sat below the ungated cluster's 26.28 (HWCOST does not price the cycles the readers clock while idle); gated, the table gives 11.52 against the gated cluster's 11.88 (0.97) and the area 0.96. The per-beat saving grows with FIFO depth (e_beat × 0.75 at depth 2 to 0.46 at 16, R3), as the config and FIFO registers written once stop clocking.
- **Datapath.** 1.63 gated as ungated: the mul lanes are combinational and the TreeAccumulate loads every busy cycle, so gating moves it by under 1 %. The gap is rand64's full-range operands against the program's data, kept on purpose (X19, CG2 topic 6: pessimistic).
- **Crossbar.** 1.40: also not a clock question. The gated table is 0.5 % above the ungated (one gate per bank, every input busy every cycle in the stimulus, nothing to gate away); the cluster's crossbar is busy far less, and HWCOST prices every access at $e_{acc}(b, b)$, a full crossbar's. Its area is 1.10 (X71's factor form, +10.8 % since CSYN1).
- **Control and memory.** Control is 0.13 nJ either way (ctrl ungated, its factor rescaled, X83); memory is CACTI's macros, 0.99.

So with the gated tables the five parts come within 1.245 of the gated cluster's run window, all of the excess in the datapath (+9.34 nJ) and the crossbar (+3.24 nJ), both pessimistic by the stimulus, while the streamers now agree.

## e_hold on a gated block

R2 found that a gated block's idle energy is its ICGs' alone, about 4.90 fJ per ICG per cycle at every point. With $k$ bits behind one gate, a held bit costs per sequential cell

$$e_{hold,cg} = \frac{e_{icg}}{k + 1}$$

0.0754 fJ at $k$ = 64, 0.0096 at 512, 0.0024 at 2,048. `char_cg.json` keeps the mean over the 14 registered points, 0.0655 fJ (range 111 %), as agreed in topic 4; HWCOST's registers are at least 64 bits wide, so the 64-bit value would be the pessimistic choice. Either way it is small in dot_3: the TreeAccumulate's 64 held bits over 257 cycles cost 1.08 pJ with the mean and 1.24 pJ with the 64-bit value, against the datapath's 24 nJ.
