## The stimuli

- **Idle:** the same ports and configuration, nothing started or requested; the reader's window 1,024 cycles, the interconnect's 1,025 as grant's.
- **Refused, reader:** each channel's TCDM `ready` a new random bit every cycle; a refused request held until granted, its address checked against the pending one.
- **Refused, interconnect:** inputs 2j and 2j + 1 move in step on bank (j + k) mod banks, so each pair has exactly one refusal per request. A first form let every input move on alone (request k to bank (i // 2 + k) mod banks): once a pair collided its two inputs fell out of step and never met again, 16 refusals in 1,029 cycles on `i8_b16`. Pairs in step, as two movers feeding one datapath, give inputs / 2 × 1,024 refusals over 2,049 cycles.
