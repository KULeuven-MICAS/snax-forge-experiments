# COST3 R6b, 28 Nov 2026: the interconnect's refusals against runs with the same grants and none refused

Claude in the chat, in its sandbox (no EDA tools), snax-forge-experiments on `ry/cost3` from R6's `a6b6c06`. R6 measured the interconnect's refusal at 3.2–3.9 pJ a refusal on `xbar` ($r_{xbar}$ 0.853), but with that price the model puts mvt's contiguous → padded pair at −18.4 % against the netlist's −10.1 %, while the cluster's 23 637 extra refusals there cost about nothing above the floor. R6's runs subtract the extra cycles at the idle energy, so they cannot tell a refusal's own energy from a half-busy interconnect's; and every refusal there is a request's first refused cycle, where a stalled streamer in the cluster holds its request for many. This round adds the stimuli that separate the two; R6c runs them.

**Result.** `pwr run --source xbar|xbar_mix --mode apart`, and `--group 4` for `refused` and `apart`. Refused minus apart, at 2 and at 4 inputs a bank, gives a refusal's energy in its first cycle ($e_{first}$) and in each further one ($e_{more}$); `refusal-fit` prints them (`ab`). R6's refused TB at 2 inputs a bank is unchanged byte for byte, so R6's runs are the refused half at 2.

## The stimuli

- **Groups** (`--group G`): G inputs share a bank, group g = i // G with request k on bank (g + k) mod banks, 2 × 1,024 / G requests an input, the group's next requests once all G are granted. Per group and request: G cycles, G − 1 refused requests, G (G − 1) / 2 refused request-cycles, so at G = 4 a refused request waits 1, 2 or 3 cycles. The window stays 2,049 cycles at any G.
- **Apart** (`--mode apart`): the same stimulus, the same request fields presented at the same edges, but only one input of a group valid at a time, in index order, the next one valid once the current one is granted. The same grants, cycles and banks; no refusal.
- With $F$ the first refused cycles (the TB prints them as `first`) and $R$ all refused request-cycles:

$$E_{ref} - E_{apart} = e_{first}\thinspace F + e_{more}\thinspace(R - F)$$

At G = 2 every refused request waits one cycle ($F = R$), so $e_{first} = (E_{ref} - E_{apart}) / R$; G = 4 then gives $e_{more}$. Each also over the granted access ($r_{first}$, $r_{more}$).
