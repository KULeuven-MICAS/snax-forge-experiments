# COST3 R6, 28 Nov 2026: the streamer and the interconnect idle and refused, on the whole grant grid

R6 runs R5's idle and refused stimuli (X116) on the gated block netlists, every point of the streamer and interconnect grant grid in both modes (168 readers, 17 `xbar` and 18 `xbar_mix` points), then `refusal-fit`. Claude Code on micas-lapserv1, snax-forge-experiments on `ry/cost3` from R5's `eaba0797`, tag `cost3-r6` (local). These are the new TBs' first runs on snax_cluster's netlists: every one of the 406 runs (and the 8 of step 3 before them) is `ok`, and no TB needed a fix. Raw data for the chat.

**Result.** Every count is as the stimulus intends: the idle runs count nothing; every refused interconnect has inputs / 2 × 1,024 refusals over 2,049 cycles, as the model had; every refused reader stalls, with 0.97 to 1.04 refused request-cycles per channel request. The fit gives **`r_strm` = 0.1505** (X111: 0.085) and **`r_xbar` = 0.8535** (X111: 0, clamped); no set has every point within ±20 % of its mean (62 of 168 readers, 8 of 17 `xbar`, 8 of 18 `xbar_mix`). `model_fit.json`, X111 and X116 are not touched; every source's `power_pt.csv` is byte-equal.

## Anything odd, for the chat

- **The interconnect's r is near a grant.** `r_xbar` 0.85, against X111's cluster fit at 0. On the refused stimulus every grant of the grant run is still made, over 2,049 cycles in place of 1,025, with every pair's held request and the arbiters active on all of them; the extra 1,024 cycles are charged at the idle run's energy a cycle, so whatever those cycles cost above idle is in $r$. Its spread follows the banks (1.10 at 16, 0.69 at 64) and the input kind (read-only 1.3, write-only 0.4–0.6).
- **No set is within ±20 %.** By X116 that means a table beside the grant prices rather than one $r$ per block; the per-point values are in `refusal.json`.
- **Idle against the floor.** The idle runs come out at 0.77 of X112's streamer floor and 1.22 and 1.12 of its crossbar floor on average, from 0.33 to 1.77 by point: a check only (X116).
- **`pwr.py` drops the `TB:` lines.** `info["tb"]` is collected but neither printed nor kept, and `vsim.log` is not in the run zip; step 3 needed a wrapper to show them.
