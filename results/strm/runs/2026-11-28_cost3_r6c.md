## Anything odd, for the chat

- **The refusal is free; the half-busy cycles are not.** With the same grants and cycles, holding a refused request costs the interconnect −0.03 to +0.02 pJ a cycle, so R6's r_xbar 0.853 measured what the extra 1,024 cycles cost above the idle run's energy a cycle (the arbiters and the other half of each pair active), not the refusal.
- **e_first is slightly negative at many points.** −0.0054 to −0.0063 pJ on every 32-bank point, −0.014 on `i4_b64` and `i8_b64`, −0.032 on `i32_b64`, `i40_b64` and `i48_b64`; about 0 on the 16-bank points and the rest. One possible reading, not checked: in the refused run a losing input's valid stays high while in apart it toggles, so a little less switches. The same values at the same inputs and banks across kinds suggest the arbiter's side rather than the input's.
