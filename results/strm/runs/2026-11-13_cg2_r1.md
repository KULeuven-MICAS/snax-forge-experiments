# CG2 R1, 13 Nov 2026: the gated tables' writers and checks, in the chat

CG2 makes HWCOST's block tables (streamer, crossbar, char) again from clock-gated synthesis (X79's `--clk-gate`), beside the ungated ones, and prices dot_3 with them here, snax-forge read only. R1 is the code, with no tool run: Claude in the chat, on `ry/cg2` from main, tag `cg2-r1` (local). The plan is the project doc `claude/cg2-plan-2026-11-13.md`.

## What changed

- **X82, `syn/syn.py`.** The streamer and xbar flop check (`seq_cells` against `rtl_flop_bits`) and `xbar_mix`'s count no longer fail a row: `flop_note()` prints `flops <point> on <tech>: DC <n> sequential cells, RTL <m> bits, ...` and the row stays `ok`. A clock-gated row also takes DC's `black_box` power group out of the four groups' sum check, as the cluster and the SRAM macros do: Nangate's `CLKGATE` cells are black boxes to DC (CG1 R2). `syn/README.md` says so; new test `test_flop_note_reports_not_fails` in `syn/test_clk_gate.py`.
- **X83, `report/strm_table.py`.** `build()` reads only the rows of one `clk_gate` (an empty column reads as 0), so the ungated tables stay ungated once gated rows sit in the same CSVs. `--clk-gate` writes `streamer_cg.csv` and `xbar_cg.csv` from the gated rows, same columns (`flops` is `seq_cells`, ICGs included), whatever points have gated rows, then prints gated / ungated per point for area, energy and flops. `--loo` reads the set asked for. New test `TestGated` in `report/test_strm_table.py`.
- **X83, `report/char_export.py` and `char_table.py`.** `char_table.read_csv()` and `load()` take `gate`. `char-export --clk-gate` writes `results/char/nangate45/char_cg.json` in `char.json`'s format: `flop.area_um2` the mean of `seq_area / seq_cells`; e_load and e_hold as before, per sequential cell; a block's `seq_cells` against its bits printed, not checked; ctrl's factor `char.json`'s × `char.json`'s e_hold / its own (E_ctrl unchanged); `source` adds `clk_gate` and a ctrl note. The params are checked against `blocks.json` at the rows' snax-forge commit (`git show` in the sibling checkout), since snax-forge main has renamed `ElementwiseLanes` to `SimdLanes` (below). Then gated / ungated per point. New `report/test_char_export.py` (`pixi run char-export-test`): synthetic gated twins of the committed rows.
- **`report/cg2_reprice.py`** (`pixi run cg2-reprice`, test `cg2-reprice-test`): dot_3's five HWCOST parts at W = 2, 4, 8, 16 with either table set, as snax-forge's `estimate/cost.py` prices them, into `results/cluster/nangate45/hwcost_cg.csv`; at W = 8 the gated parts against `dot3_w8_cg`'s run window and area. A field the gated tables cannot give yet is left empty and named.
- **`report/cg2_check.py`** (`pixi run cg2-check inventory [--all] | pairs | mix`): the RTL zips against the ungated rows' commits, each gated row beside its ungated row (provenance, area, comb, `seq_cells`, `clock_gating.rpt`'s counts, the power windows, the energy ratio), and the `xbar_mix` factor check at `i36_b32`. Markdown for the run notes; nothing in it is a gate.
- Docs: X82 and X83 in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (X33, X52, X70 note the amendment), `docs/cost_tables.md`, `strm/README.md`, `results/char/README.md`, `README.md`; the tasks in `pixi.toml`.

## What was checked

- The ungated `streamer.csv` and `xbar.csv` written again by the new `strm-table`: byte for byte the committed ones.
- `char-export` and `char-table` without `--clk-gate` print what they printed on main and exit 1 the same way (`ctrl/ctrl in 78 synthesis and 7 power rows, 48 expected`, CG1 R1's finding, not CG2's).
- `cg2-reprice` with the ungated set gives back every part of the committed `results/cluster/nangate45/hwcost/dot3_w<W>.json`, area and energy, at W = 2, 4, 8 and 16 (worst |ratio − 1| 2.2e-16): HWCOST's dot_3 is two readers `d8_l1_cW` and a writer `d8_l1_c1`, a mul `ElementwiseLanes` and a `TreeAccumulate` at W lanes, a 67-bit control unit, $C_{acc} = N/W + 1$, the crossbar's energy at $e_{acc}(b, b)$ per access and its area in X71's factor form. With the ungated tables copied in as stand-ins for the gated ones, the W = 8 comparison reproduces CG1's: 70.41 nJ against the gated run window's 49.48 nJ.
- `cg2-check mix` on the committed rows: ungated area ratio 0.6414 at `i36_b32`, the factor form 0.6361 (−0.82 %).
- Dry runs of `syn.py run --clk-gate --dry-run` on one point of each source (zips faked in `rtl/`, deleted after): runs and zips tagged `_cg`, `SYN_CLK_GATE_ENABLE=1`, the three clock-gating reports kept. `pwr.py --clk-gate` (no dry run) reads `<point>_cg.zip` through `syn_zip()`, tested in `test_pwr_names_and_flag`; streamer, xbar and char use the same cell models as the cluster (`pwr.cell_model`).
- Tests run here (Python 3.11 venv with numpy and matplotlib, no pixi in the sandbox): `clk-gate-test`, `strm-table-test`, `char-export-test`, `cg2-reprice-test`, `xbar-kinds-test`, all pass. `arc check`: nothing left over.

## Found

- snax-forge main has renamed the catalogue's `ElementwiseLanes` to `SimdLanes` in `hw/chisel/blocks.json`; its `snax_forge/char/nangate45.json` still keys `ElementwiseLanes`. `char-export`'s catalogue check against the sibling checkout's main would refuse CHAR1's points, so the gated export checks against `blocks.json` at the rows' commit (`e8611d51`). `char_cg.json` keeps `ElementwiseLanes`, as `char.json`; the later snax-forge import deals with the name.
- snax-forge's crossbar energy is one price per access at $e_{acc}(b, b)$, a full crossbar's, whatever the inputs (its `estimate/cost.py`), which is why HWCOST's crossbar is 11.22 nJ at every W.
