# Run note: RAM1 R3, SRAM macros (.lib, .db, .v) from CACTI's numbers, 6 Nov 2026

R3 builds the generator that turns a CACTI point into a macro DC, Questa and PrimeTime can use (X49): a Liberty model compiled to `.db` by `lc_shell` and a behavioural Verilog model, generated on demand in `ram/runs/lib/` and never committed. It is checked on one TCDM bank (512 x 64, byte masks): the `.lib` against the table, DC linking and timing it next to Nangate 45 nm, and PrimePower charging the energy of counted accesses. Run on the MICAS server.

## Step 3: the generator

`pixi run ram lib <depth> <width> [--mask 8]` and `pixi run ram libs ram/macros.json -j N` in `ram/ram.py` (the `ram/README.md` section and X49 say what the `.lib` and `.v` hold). `libs` runs at most as many lc_shell at once as there are free Library-Compiler licences.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| sram_d128_w256 | d128_w256 | 19,245.5 | 9.55139 | 16.6784 | 0.234872 | 0.222222 | 2,212.13 | 2.3 s | ok |
| sram_d128_w39 | d128_w40 | 2,286.74 | 1.10094 | 2.21454 | 0.198298 | 0.15128 | 355.085 | 2.2 s | ok |

lc_shell's warnings, the same two on each macro: `Warning: Line 163, Cell 'sram_d512_w64_m8', pin 'rdata_o[63]', The 'rdata_o[63]' Pin/bus on the 'sram_d512_w64_m8' cell has no 'function' attribute.` and `Warning: Line 52, Cell 'sram_d512_w64_m8', Failed to recognize the functionality of cell 'sram_d512_w64_m8'. (LIBG-10)` (lines differ per macro). No errors, no Information lines.

**Without a `memory` group.** lc_shell does not need one. On scratch copies of the bank's `.lib`: without it (the generator's choice) the two warnings above; with `is_memory_cell : true` the same two; with a cell `memory () { type : ram; address_width : 9; word_width : 64; }` neither, but `Information: ... contains memory/memory_read/memory_write groups which are deprecated, use the is_memory_cell attribute instead. (LIBG-280)`. All three compile to a `.db` (PITFALLS LIB6).

**Library hygiene.** The first `.lib` also drew six LBDB-172 lines (`default_fanout_load`, `default_inout_pin_cap`, `default_input_pin_cap`, `default_output_pin_cap`, `default_cell_leakage_power`, `default_leakage_power_density` not specified) and `The 'internal_power_calculation' attribute in char_config group is required for NLPM library.` (LBDB-366, as R0). nangate45's `default_*` values (the same as lc_shell's defaults; now in `nangate45_pins.json`) and `char_config () { internal_power_calculation : exclude_switching_on_rise_and_fall; }` remove them. The chosen value says the `clk_i` energy excludes the switching of `rdata_o`'s load, which DC and PT compute themselves; the macro has no output-pin internal power, which is what the attribute governs. The slew and delay thresholds are nangate45's too, so DC and PT see one set of thresholds on both libraries.

**CACTI's cycle is shorter than its access time** on all three (0.210 against 0.269 ns on the bank): CACTI's cycle is the mat's own (inside the mat, wordline reset, bitline restore), and the access time adds the H-trees (`uca.cc`, PITFALLS C6). `min_period` and the min pulse widths use the cycle, the clk-to-`rdata_o` arc the access time; at 10 ns neither limits.

**The `.v` against `tc_sram`.** `tc_sram_impl` only wraps `tc_sram` (`impl_o = ImplOutSim`), so `tc_sram` defines the behaviour. At `NumPorts 1`, `Latency 1`: `rdata_q <= (req && !we) ? sram[addr_i] : sram[r_addr_q]` on every rising edge, `r_addr_q <= addr_i` on a read only (reset to 0), writes through `be_i`. So `rdata_o` shows the word read after a read; after a write the last read word as it was before the edge (a write to that word shows a cycle later); while deselected the same, so it holds. The generated `.v` uses the same equations. Checked side by side with `tc_sram` (512 x 64, `ByteWidth` 8) under random reads, full writes, masked writes and idle cycles on 16 addresses, 20,000 cycles, compared with `!==` at every falling edge: 0 mismatches with X contents (`SimInit "none"`), 0 with zero contents (`SimInit "zeros"` against `+define+SRAM_INIT_ZERO`); `RAMCOUNT` printed reads 5024 writes 9894. All three `.v` elaborate in `vsim` with no warning, with and without `SRAM_INIT_ZERO`.

## Step 4: the `.lib` against the table

`ram/probe/check_lib.py` read each `.lib`'s `area`, the `rise_power` of the read and write `when` groups (fJ to pJ), `cell_leakage_power` (nW to µW), `cell_rise` and `min_period`, and compared them with the table's row for the padded size ((512, 64), (128, 256), (128, 40)): every value equal, the largest relative difference 2.1e-16 (fJ to pJ rounding of 16.6784). Every `fall_power` is 0 and the `!req_i` group's `rise_power` is 0.

## Step 5: DC links one bank

`ram/probe/ram_probe_top.sv`: one `sram_d512_w64_m8` with its ports at the top and one register stage (no reset) on `rdata_o`. Probe Tcl, X1 to X4's settings, `compile`:

```tcl
set nangate  [file normalize $repo/../RTL_flow_Nangate15/tech/nangate45/NangateOpenCellLibrary_typical.db]
set macro    [file normalize $repo/ram/runs/lib/sram_d512_w64_m8/sram_d512_w64_m8.db]
set_app_var target_library $nangate
set_app_var link_library   [list * $nangate $macro]
set_app_var auto_wire_load_selection false
read_db [list $nangate $macro]
remove_attribute [get_libs NangateOpenCellLibrary] default_wire_load
analyze -format sverilog ../ram_probe_top.sv
elaborate ram_probe_top
link
remove_wire_load_model [current_design]
set_max_transition 0.25 [current_design]
set_max_fanout 32 [current_design]
create_clock -name clk -period 10 [get_ports clk_i]
set_clock_uncertainty 0.1  -setup [get_clocks clk]
set_clock_uncertainty 0.02 -hold  [get_clocks clk]
set data_inputs [remove_from_collection [all_inputs] [get_ports clk_i]]
set_driving_cell -lib_cell INV_X4 $data_inputs
set_load 25.0 [all_outputs]
set_input_delay  -max 0.01 -clock clk $data_inputs
set_output_delay -max 0.01 -clock clk [all_outputs]
compile
report_timing -nosplit -input_pins -to [get_pins {u_sram/addr_i[*] u_sram/wdata_i[*] u_sram/be_i[*] u_sram/req_i u_sram/we_i}]
report_timing -nosplit -input_pins -from [get_pins {u_sram/rdata_o[*]}]
report_power -nosplit -cell -only [get_cells u_sram]
write_file -format verilog -hierarchy -output out/ram_probe_top_mapped.v
write_sdc out/ram_probe_top.sdc
```

rc 0, no error. Messages: `Loading link library 'sram_d512_w64_m8'`, `No messages were issued during linking.` (LINK-1806), no unit or library message; warnings 84 x UID-401 (driving-cell design rules on the ports, as R0) and 2 x DDB-24 (`Overwriting design file` for both `.db`, the in-memory copy: the Nangate `.db`'s md5 is unchanged). `report_constraint -all_violators`: no violated constraints.

- **One cell with the `.lib`'s area.** `report_reference`: `sram_d512_w64_m8` from `sram_d512_w64_m8`, count 1, unit area 13,522.700195, attributes `b, d`; 64 `DFF_X1` (289.408). `report_area`: 1 macro/black box, macro area 13,522.700195, total cell area 13,812.108185 µm², combinational area 0.
- **No buffers on `rdata_o`.** `report_area` has 0 buf/inv: each `rdata_o` bit drives its `DFF_X1` directly. In DC `u_sram/rdata_o[0]` has `max_capacitance` 242.309998 (R0's 0.84 fF put 64 buffers there); `u_sram/clk_i` reads 0.949653 fF and `u_sram/addr_i[0]` 1.140290 fF.
- **Into the macro** (its setup arc): `addr_i[0]` (in, 0.01 f) to `u_sram/addr_i[0]`, endpoint `u_sram` as a rising-edge flop clocked by clk; required 10.00 - 0.10 uncertainty - 0.10 `library setup time` = 9.80; slack 9.79 ns.
- **Out of `rdata_o`** (its clock-to-output arc): `u_sram/clk_i` 0.00 r, `u_sram/rdata_o[63]` +0.27 = 0.27 f, `rdata_o_reg[63]/D`; required 10.00 - 0.10 - 0.04 setup = 9.86; slack 9.59 ns. A full-cycle path from the rising edge (R0's OpenRAM macro launched on the falling edge).
- **Hold** into the macro is unconstrained (`-max` input delays only, as `syn/scripts/constraints.sdc`).
- **The `when` groups in DC.** At DC's default activity (static probability 0.5 on `req_i` and `we_i`) `report_power` gives the macro 250.1472 µW internal, which is (3.53819 + 6.4677) / 4 pJ per 10 ns cycle: 250.147 µW. Leakage 2.333 mW (CACTI's itrs-hp leakage, in `cell_leakage_power`).

## Step 6: PrimeTime against the counted energy

**Simulation.** `ram/probe/tb.sv` drives the mapped netlist (Nangate's `NangateOpenCellLibrary.v`, the macro's `.v` with `+define+SRAM_INIT_ZERO`) at 10 ns, seed 1 (`$urandom(1)`): 1,000 reads, 1,000 full writes (`be_i` = ff), 500 masked writes (`be_i` random in 1..255) and 1,500 idle cycles (`req_i` and `we_i` low, the other inputs held), shuffled (Fisher-Yates on `$urandom_range`), random addresses and data, after 4 idle lead-in cycles and before 2 idle tail cycles. Inputs change at falling edges; `rdata_o` is compared with `!==` every cycle against a model of `tc_sram` (Latency 1) plus the register: 0 mismatches. Questa as `sim/` (docs/power.md): `vlog -timescale 1ns/1ps` on the netlist, `vopt +initreg+0 +acc=n`, `vsim -c +nospecify +notimingchecks` with `sim/scripts/run.do`, `$dumpvars(1, tb.dut)` (the netlist's nets, not the cells'). The window: from a quarter cycle before the first slot's rising edge to a quarter cycle before the rising edge after the last, 52.5 to 40,052.5 ns, 4,000 rising edges, 40,000 ns.

`RAMCOUNT tb.dut.u_sram sram_d512_w64_m8 reads 1000 writes 1500`: 1,000 reads and 1,000 + 500 writes, as scheduled (the lead-in and tail are idle). E = 1000 x 3.53819 + 1500 x 6.4677 = 13,239.74 pJ.

**PrimePower,** as `sim/scripts/pt_power.tcl` (in `ram/probe/pt.tcl`):

```tcl
set power_enable_analysis true
set power_analysis_mode $mode                      ;# averaged or time_based
set link_library [list * $nangate $macro]
read_verilog ../dc/out/ram_probe_top_mapped.v
current_design ram_probe_top
link_design
set auto_wire_load_selection false
remove_wire_load_model
set all_nets [get_nets -hierarchical *]
set_load 0 $all_nets
set_resistance 0 $all_nets
read_sdc ../dc/out/ram_probe_top.sdc
read_saif -strip_path tb/dut probe.saif                                         ;# (a) averaged
read_vcd -strip_path tb/dut -zero_delay -time [list 52.5 40052.5] probe.vcd     ;# (b) time_based, -time in ns
update_power
report_switching_activity -list_not_annotated > not_annotated.rpt
report_power -nosplit -significant_digits 6 -cell_power [get_cells u_sram] > power_macro.rpt
get_attribute [get_cells u_sram] internal_power
```

(a)'s SAIF: `vcd2saif -input probe.vcd -output probe.saif -instance tb/dut -time 52500 40052500` (the VCD is in ps, PITFALLS PT4); `(DURATION 40000000)`. Both PT runs rc 0, no warning or error; each held one PrimeTime and one PrimePower. The VCD (508 KB) was deleted after the runs.

| mode | macro internal power | x 40,000 ns | against E | macro pins annotated | design nets annotated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) averaged (SAIF) | 289.7568 µW | 11,590.27 pJ | -12.46 % | 148 of 148 | 212 of 212 (100 %) |
| (b) time-based (VCD) | 330.9935 µW | 13,239.74 pJ | -0.00001 % | 148 of 148 | 212 of 212 (100 %) |

Leakage 2.3325 mW and switching power on the macro's output nets 17.5 pJ over the window (the `rdata` nets into the 64 flops) in both; neither is in E.

**Why (a) is low.** The SAIF gives `req_i` a static probability of 0.624937 and `we_i` 0.374938, and `clk_i` 4,000 rises. 4,000 x (0.624937 x 0.625062 x 3.53819 + 0.624937 x 0.374938 x 6.4677) = 11,590.27 pJ, (a)'s figure to 0.004 pJ: averaged mode takes P(req & !we) as P(req) x P(!we), 0.39 reads and 0.23 writes per cycle, where the run had 0.25 and 0.375. A SAIF holds no joint states, so averaged mode cannot do better; the gap's size and sign depend on the mix and on `we_i` while idle (low here) (PITFALLS PT7). Time-based mode evaluates the `when` conditions at each rising edge and charges exactly the counted energy.

**So:** a cluster run reports memory energy as counted accesses x the table (`RAMCOUNT`, or the cluster's own counters), or as time-based PT on the VCD; averaged PT's memory figure is not used (X49).

## Step 7

(b) is within 2 %. (a) differs by 12.5 %, and nothing in the reports points to a fix: the `when` conditions match the netlist's pin names (time-based mode on the same `.db` is exact, and DC's 0.5 / 0.5 check in step 5 equals the expected value), and the gap is averaged mode's independence of pin probabilities, reproduced to 0.004 pJ. No fix tried; kept as a finding (PITFALLS PT7).
