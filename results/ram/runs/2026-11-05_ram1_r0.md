# Run note: RAM1 R0, OpenRAM probe on one TCDM bank (512 x 64, 1RW, byte mask), FreePDK45, 5 Nov 2026

R0 probed OpenRAM 1.2.48's analytical model on one macro, the shape of one snax_cluster TCDM bank: 512 words of 64 bits, one read-write port, byte write mask (`write_size = 8`), FreePDK45, TT, 1.1 V, 25 C. Run B (`netlist_only = True`) finished in 9:44; run A (with layout) was stopped by hand after 49 min, in supply routing, and wrote no outputs. Steps 6 and 7 (`lc_shell`, DC) ran on run B's `.lib`.

**Outcome: OpenRAM is dropped for RAM1.** Its analytical energy is one power value at 100 MHz written where Liberty expects an energy, the same for read, write and deselected; the area is 0 without layout; one macro takes about 10 minutes without layout and hours with it. Area and energy will come from CACTI 7 at 45 nm, with our own `.lib` and `.v` written from CACTI's numbers (R1). The `ram` pixi environment is removed again; `pixi.toml` and `pixi.lock` are back to main's.

## What the outputs hold (run B)

### The four corners

`process_corners = ["TT"]`, `supply_voltages = [1.1]`, `temperatures = [25]` gave four `.lib` files. `lib.py`'s `create_corners` always adds the tech's nominal corner (`nom_supply_voltage` 1.0 V on freepdk45), the min and max of the configured supplies and temperatures, and the FF and SS process corners at the nominal supply, unless the config sets `only_use_config_corners = True` (then only the product of the three lists) or gives `use_specified_corners`. Per corner:

| corner | clk0 internal power value | min period (ns) | min pulse width (ns) | clk0 to dout0 (ns) | leakage (mW) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TT 1.0 V 25 C | 4.756269 | 1.528 | 0.764 | 0.412 | 0.033506 |
| TT 1.1 V 25 C | 5.755085 | 1.389 | 0.6945 | 0.374 to 0.375 | 0.033506 |
| FF 1.0 V 25 C | 5.284743 | 1.375 | 0.6875 | 0.371 | 0.033506 |
| SS 1.0 V 25 C | 4.323881 | 1.68 | 0.84 | 0.453 | 0.033506 |

TT 1.1 V / TT 1.0 V = 1.2100 = 1.1²: the energy figure scales with V². Leakage is the same in every corner.

The rest of this section is the TT 1.1 V `.lib` unless said otherwise. Run A wrote no `.lib`, so there is no A-against-B difference to report; B's `.lib` is a full one except for its area.

### Library units and conditions

`time_unit` 1 ns, `capacitive_load_unit` 1 pF, `voltage_unit` 1 V, `current_unit` 1 mA, `resistance_unit` and `pulling_resistance_unit` 1 kohm, `leakage_power_unit` 1 mW; no internal power unit is declared, so it is derived: DC's `report_lib` prints `Dynamic Energy Unit : 1.000000pJ (derived from V,C units)`. `nom_process` 1.0, `nom_voltage` 1.1, `nom_temperature` 25; one operating condition `OC` (process 1.0, voltage 1.1, temperature 25), the default. `voltage_map` VDD 1.1, GND 0. Thresholds 50 %, slews 10 to 90 %. Nangate 45 nm is in ns, fF, nW and mA, so the two libraries differ in capacitance and leakage units.

### The cell

`cell (sram_512x64_wm8)`: a `memory()` group (`type : ram`, `address_width : 9`, `word_width : 64`), `interface_timing : true`, `dont_use`, `map_only`, `dont_touch`, `pg_pin` vdd and gnd. **`area : 0`**: `lib.py` writes `sram.width * sram.height`, which is 0 without layout; run A, the one that would have had it, did not finish. The datasheet also says `Area (µm²) 0`. **Leakage** `cell_leakage_power : 0.033506` mW = 33.5 µW: 1 nW per bitcell (`spice["bitcell_leakage"]`, 32,768 bitcells = 32.8 µW) plus 1 nW per inverter and flop; a constant, not a function of V or T.

### The internal_power groups

Four, all on pin clk0, no `related_pin`, each with a scalar `rise_power` and `fall_power` (no table, no index):

| `when` | what it represents | rise_power | fall_power |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!csb0 & !web0` | write (full or masked) | 5.755085 | 5.755085 |
| `csb0 & !web0` | deselected, web0 low | 5.755085 | 5.755085 |
| `!csb0 & web0` | read | 5.755085 | 5.755085 |
| `csb0 & web0` | deselected, web0 high (idle clock) | 5.755085 | 5.755085 |

There is no group for a masked write: wmask0 has no power group and no `when` has a wmask term, so a write with one byte enabled costs what a write with eight does. There is one value for everything.

**How DC reads it (Liberty).** `internal_power` values are energies in capacitive_load_unit · voltage_unit² = 1 pF · (1 V)² = 1 pJ. On an input pin with no related pin, `rise_power` is charged on each rising transition of the pin and `fall_power` on each falling one. Every clk0 edge matches exactly one of the four `when`s, so DC and PT charge 5.755 pJ on each edge, 11.51 pJ per clock cycle, also when the macro is deselected. Step 7's DC run shows it: the macro's internal power at the 10 ns clock and default activity is 1.151 mW = 11.51 pJ × 100 MHz (`report_power -cell`: `u_sram` 1.151e+03 µW internal, 0.3047 µW switching, 33.51 µW leakage). OpenRAM meant rise_power and fall_power as writing a 1 and writing a 0 (`lib.py` writes `write1_power` and `write0_power` there), not as clock edges.

**What the number is (OpenRAM's model).** `analytical_delay = True` selects `model_name = "elmore"` (the default). `elmore.analytical_power` calls `sram.analytical_power(corner, load)`, which sums over every module instance `calc_dynamic_power` = C_eff[fF] · V · (V · swing) · f[MHz], divided by process and temperature delay factors, with f = `spice["default_event_frequency"]` = 100 MHz for every gate. fF · V² · MHz is nW; elmore divides by 1e6 and gets mW. Bitcells contribute leakage only (dynamic "FIXME" = 0), sense amps 0, bitlines C · V² · `rbl_delay_percentage` · f per column. `get_lib_values` then puts that one value into every `*_power` measure: read, write, deselected, for a 1 and for a 0. So 5.755085 is a power in mW at 100 MHz with every gate of the macro switching, not an energy per access, and Liberty reads it as pJ per edge.

**Energy per access.** Read as OpenRAM meant it, E = P / f = 5.755085 mW / 100 MHz = 57.55 pJ per access (read or write alike), 57.55 / 64 = 0.899 pJ = 899 fJ per bit. Read as DC does, 11.51 pJ per cycle, 179.8 fJ per bit (per cycle, accessed or not).

| | per 64-bit word | per bit | / D154 |
|---|---|---|---|
| snax-forge D154's placeholder (4 · e_load per bit, nangate45) | 4.40 pJ | 68.68 fJ | 1 |
| OpenRAM, P / f (its intent) | 57.55 pJ | 899 fJ | 13.1 |
| OpenRAM, as DC charges it (rise + fall per cycle) | 11.51 pJ | 179.8 fJ | 2.62 |

Neither OpenRAM figure is an energy per access of a 512 x 64 array: the first assumes every gate switches on every event (all 128 rows' decoder gates, all flops at probability 0.5) and leaves out the bitcells and sense amps; the second is the same number with its unit reinterpreted.

### Timing

- clk0 to dout0: `timing_type : falling_edge`, `timing_sense : non_unate`; `cell_rise` and `cell_fall` 0.374 to 0.375 ns over CELL_TABLE (index_1 `input_net_transition` 0.00125, 0.005, 0.04 ns; index_2 `total_output_net_capacitance` 0.052, 0.209, 0.836 fF); `rise_transition` and `fall_transition` 0.001 ns. The read data comes from the falling edge of clk0.
- Inputs (din0, addr0, wmask0, csb0, web0): `setup_rising` 0.009 ns and `hold_rising` 0.001 ns to clk0, constant over CONSTRAINT_TABLE (both indices 0.00125, 0.005, 0.04 ns).
- clk0: `min_pulse_width` 0.6945 ns, `minimum_period` 1.389 ns = 2 · 1.85 · 0.3754 (elmore's max delay times a period margin of 1.85, both half-cycles).

### The Verilog model

Port list: `clk0, csb0, web0, wmask0, addr0, din0, dout0`, plus `vdd, gnd` under `` `ifdef USE_POWER_PINS ``. Parameters `NUM_WMASKS = 8`, `DATA_WIDTH = 64`, `ADDR_WIDTH = 9`, `RAM_DEPTH = 1 << ADDR_WIDTH`, `DELAY = 3` ("FIXME: This delay is arbitrary"), `VERBOSE = 1` (a `$display` per read and write), `T_HOLD = 1` ("Value is arbitrary"). No `` `timescale `` (the simulator's default applies; DELAY and T_HOLD are in its unit).

- posedge clk0: csb0, web0, wmask0, addr0 and din0 are registered (blocking assignments); then `#(T_HOLD) dout0 = 64'bx`.
- negedge clk0, `!csb0_reg && !web0_reg`: the write, byte by byte under wmask0_reg.
- negedge clk0, `!csb0_reg && web0_reg`: the read, `dout0 <= #(DELAY) mem[addr0_reg]`.

So dout0 is valid from DELAY after the falling edge to T_HOLD after the next rising edge, and X otherwise, whether or not the next cycle reads. A register sampling dout0 at the next rising edge gets the data in zero-delay simulation (it samples before T_HOLD); a design that expects a held read value (as `tc_sram_impl` gives) does not get one. The `.lib` agrees on the edges (inputs at the rising edge, dout0 from the falling edge).

### The datasheet

`sram_512x64_wm8.html`: "Area (µm²) 0"; operating conditions VDD 1.0 to 1.1 V, 25 C, "Operating Frequency (F) 654 MHz" (1 / 1.528 ns, the TT 1.0 V corner); "Timing Data, Using analytical model: results may not be precise": setup 0.009, hold 0.001, dout0 cell rise 0.371 to 0.454 ns and fall 0.412 to 0.413 ns, transitions 0.001 ns (min and max over the corners); "Power Data": Read rising and falling 2.3781 mW, Write rising and falling 2.3781 mW, csb0 leakage 0.033506 mW; the four corners; deliverables naming `.db` files it never wrote. Against the `.lib`:

- The headline figures are the **first** corner's (TT 1.0 V), not the 1.1 V one asked for; the TT 1.1 V values (Read and Write 2.8775 mW, min period 1.389 ns) are only in an HTML comment.
- The power is **half** the `.lib`'s: 2.3781 = 4.756269 / 2, because `lib.py:846` takes `np.mean(read1_power + read0_power) / 2`, where `+` concatenates two lists, so it halves the mean.
- The labels are swapped: "Read" is printed for `!csb0 & clk0 & !web0`, which is the write condition, and "Write" for `!csb0 & !clk0 & web0`.
- "Git commit id: 3c1bd9f6..." is this repo's HEAD: OpenRAM records the git commit of the working directory, not its own.
- Leakage and timing match the `.lib` where they are the same corner.

## DC links the macro (step 7)

The wrapper `ram_probe_top` has `tc_sram_impl`'s ports for one bank, maps them onto the macro and registers `rdata_o` once (async active-low reset):

```systemverilog
module ram_probe_top (
  input  logic        clk_i, rst_ni, req_i, we_i,
  input  logic [8:0]  addr_i,
  input  logic [7:0]  be_i,
  input  logic [63:0] wdata_i,
  output logic [63:0] rdata_o
);
  logic [63:0] dout;
  sram_512x64_wm8 u_sram (
    .clk0 (clk_i), .csb0 (~req_i), .web0 (~we_i), .wmask0 (be_i),
    .addr0 (addr_i), .din0 (wdata_i), .dout0 (dout)
  );
  always_ff @(posedge clk_i or negedge rst_ni)
    if (!rst_ni) rdata_o <= '0; else rdata_o <= dout;
endmodule
```

The probe Tcl (not `syn/syn.py`), X1 to X4's settings on nangate45 (ns and fF, scale 1), `compile`:

```tcl
set_app_var target_library $nangate
set_app_var link_library   [list * $nangate $macro]
set_app_var auto_wire_load_selection false
read_db [list $nangate $macro]
remove_attribute [get_libs NangateOpenCellLibrary] default_wire_load
analyze -format sverilog ram_probe_top.sv
elaborate ram_probe_top
link
remove_wire_load_model [current_design]
set_max_transition 0.25 [current_design]
set_max_fanout 32 [current_design]
create_clock -name clk -period 10 [get_ports clk_i]
set_clock_uncertainty 0.1  -setup [get_clocks clk]
set_clock_uncertainty 0.02 -hold  [get_clocks clk]
set data_inputs [remove_from_collection [all_inputs] [get_ports clk_i]]
set_driving_cell -lib_cell INV_X4 $data_inputs
set_load 25.0 [all_outputs]
set_input_delay  -max 0.01 -clock clk $data_inputs
set_output_delay -max 0.01 -clock clk [all_outputs]
set_ideal_network [get_ports rst_ni]
compile
report_timing -to [get_pins {u_sram/addr0* u_sram/din0* u_sram/wmask0* u_sram/csb0 u_sram/web0}]
report_timing -from [get_pins u_sram/dout0*]
report_power -cell -only [get_cells u_sram]
```

Run on run B's `.db` in `ram/runs/probe/dc/`: rc 0, no error; warnings 84 × UID-401 (driving-cell design rules on the ports) and 2 × DDB-24 (design file overwritten); `report_constraint -all_violators`: no violated constraints.

- **One cell.** `report_reference`: `sram_512x64_wm8` from `sram_512x64_wm8_TT_1p1V_25C_lib`, count 1, unit area 0, attributes `b, d` (black box, dont_touch); `report_area`: 1 macro/black box, area 0, total cell area 392.616 µm² (64 DFFR_X1 340.48, 64 CLKBUF_X1 51.07, 2 INV_X1 1.06). The macro carries the `.lib`'s area, which here is 0.
- **Units.** No message about the two libraries' units. DC converts the macro's values into its own: the pin capacitance reads 0.2091 (fF) on `get_pins u_sram/clk0`, leakage 33.506 µW, internal energy in pJ. DC2's danger is SDC values read in a library's units; the constraints are in the target library's (nangate45's) ns and fF, so it does not apply.
- **The 64 buffers.** dout0's `max_capacitance` is 0.8364 fF; a DFFR_X1's D pin is 1.128 fF, so each dout0 bit driving its flop violates it. DC's design-rule fixing (`Beginning Design Rule Fixing (max_capacitance)`) puts a CLKBUF_X1 (input 0.780 fF) on each of the 64 bits. Any real load on dout0 will need a buffer while that limit stands.
- **Timing uses the macro's arcs.** Out of dout0: clock clk fall edge 5.00, `u_sram/clk0` 5.00 f, `u_sram/dout0[63]` +0.38 = 5.38 f (the falling_edge arc), CLKBUF_X1 +0.02, `rdata_o_reg[63]/D` 5.40; required 9.86 (10 - 0.10 uncertainty - 0.04 setup); slack 4.46 ns: a half-cycle path. Into the macro: `req_i` → INV_X1 → `u_sram/csb0` at 0.02 ns, endpoint `u_sram` as a rising-edge flop, library setup 0.01 ns, slack 9.88 ns. The min-delay (hold) paths into the macro are unconstrained, because the input delays are `-max` only, as in `syn/scripts/constraints.sdc`.
- **Power (an early check, X25).** Memory group internal 1151 µW, switching 0.30 µW, leakage 33,506 nW; clock network 41.6 µW; registers 20.6 µW; total dynamic 1.210 mW; the macro is 94.8 % of the total, all of it the 11.51 pJ per cycle above.

## Odd things

- `use_conda` defaults to True and installs a conda toolchain from inside the compiler run (above).
- `FREEPDK45` is not used by 1.2.48's freepdk45; the models are bundled.
- Four `.lib` corners for one asked for (`only_use_config_corners`).
- The netlist build ("Submodules") takes 555 to 560 s for a 32 kbit array, single-threaded, before any layout.
- SPICE stimulus and measurement files (`functional_*.sp`, `delay_*.sp`, 1.3 MB) are written to the output folder although nothing is simulated; the netlist is written three times (`.sp`, `sram.sp`, `trimmed.sp`).
- The power figure is a power in mW written as a Liberty energy (pJ), the same in all four `when`s, charged on both clock edges, and in the datasheet halved and under swapped labels.
- The datasheet reports the first corner (TT 1.0 V) and this repo's git commit.
- `max_capacitance` 0.84 fF on dout0 and `max_transition` 0.04 ns on addr0 and wmask0: far below any real cell's pins and slews (the 0.04 ns limit did not fire here: the inputs come straight from INV_X4-driven ports).
- The `.v`'s dout0 goes X T_HOLD after every rising edge.
- In the DC probe a `report_power -cell [get_cells u_sram]` gives `Error: extra positional option` (CMD-012); it needs `-only`.

## Why OpenRAM was dropped

- **Energy model.** The analytical (elmore) model gives one dynamic power per macro, every gate switching at a fixed 100 MHz, with no bitcell or sense-amp dynamic energy, written into Liberty's energy fields: 57.55 pJ per access as OpenRAM means it (899 fJ per bit, 13.1 × D154's placeholder), 11.51 pJ per cycle as DC reads it (2.62 ×), the same for read, write, masked write and deselected. There is no read/write split and no masked-write energy to put into HWCOST. A better figure needs SPICE characterization (ngspice or Xyce), which RAM1 excludes.
- **Run times.** About 10 minutes per 512 x 64 macro without layout (single-threaded netlist build), and with layout about 2.3 h (extrapolated from run A's supply routing: 16 of 120 pins at about one a minute, after 17 minutes of netlist and signal routing). Generating on demand is not cheap.
- **Area only with layout.** `area` is `width * height` of the layout: 0 with `netlist_only`, so area costs the hours above.
- **The four corners.** Unasked-for corners are written unless `only_use_config_corners = True` or `use_specified_corners` is set, and the datasheet reports the tech's nominal corner (1.0 V).
- **freepdk45 is broken in 1.2.50** (yanked, OpenRAM issue #336), so the package is pinned to a January 2024 release, 1.2.48.

## For R1 (CACTI 7 and our own .lib and .v)

What steps 6 and 7 say our own `.lib` must get right:

- **Units.** Pick them on purpose and say them: ns, fF, nW or µW leakage, so the macro matches nangate45's; or pF and mW as OpenRAM did, which DC converts. Declare the energy in the unit the `internal_power` values are written in: Liberty derives it from voltage and capacitance units (pF · V² = pJ; fF · V² = fJ).
- **Energy.** `internal_power` on clk0 with one `when` per operation: read `!csb0 & web0`, write `!csb0 & !web0` (and a masked write if CACTI or a rule gives one), deselected `csb0`. Put the per-access energy on one edge only (rise_power E, fall_power 0), or DC and PT charge it twice per cycle; give deselected its own value (0 or a clock-only figure).
- **Area.** CACTI's area in µm² in `area`, so `report_reference` and `report_area` carry it as the macro's area.
- **Pin limits.** `max_capacitance` on the outputs large enough for a flop and some wire (OpenRAM's 0.84 fF put 64 buffers in front of the read register); no 0.04 ns `max_transition` on the inputs; input capacitances from CACTI or a stated guess.
- **Timing.** Read data from the rising edge (as `tc_sram_impl`) or the falling edge (as OpenRAM); setup and hold to the rising edge; CACTI's access time as the clk-to-dout delay.
- **Library hygiene.** A `char_config` group with `internal_power_calculation` (LBDB-366), and `is_memory_cell` in place of the deprecated `memory()` groups (LIBG-280); `dont_touch` and `dont_use` as OpenRAM had them.
- **The `.v`.** A `` `timescale ``; ports as the `.lib`; a held read value (not X after every rising edge) if it is to stand in for `tc_sram_impl`.

The wrapper and Tcl above are reusable as they are, with the macro's `.db` swapped. The lc_shell two-liner turns a hand-written `.lib` into the `.db`.

PITFALLS candidates (not added in R0; to decide with R1): OpenRAM's analytical power is mW at 100 MHz in a pJ field, charged on both edges; `use_conda = True` by default; the unasked-for corners. Only one of these touches a tool RAM1 keeps: Liberty internal_power on a clock pin with rise and fall both set is charged twice per cycle.
