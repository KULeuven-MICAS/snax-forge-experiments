## The DC link check

Design-Compiler licences: 10 of 10 free. A throw-away probe in `tmp/` (deleted at the end, not committed): `probe_top` with one instance of each macro, every input pin driven by a `DFF_X1` and every `rdata_o` bit into a `DFF_X1` (787 flops), Nangate 45 nm typical plus the three `.db` from their zips (`arc.unpacked`), wire load off as in `dc_syn.tcl`, `analyze -format sverilog`, `elaborate`, 10 ns clock, `compile`, `link`, `check_design`, `report_timing -from u_m<i>/clk_i -through u_m<i>/rdata_o*`, `report_power`, `report_area`; DC Y-2026.03-SP2, `sh_continue_on_error false`, 10 s.

- `link`: the three macro libraries next to NangateOpenCellLibrary, "No messages were issued during linking"; no unmapped cell; `check_design` prints nothing. The log has no `Error` and no `Warning`.
- The `rdata_o` arc, clk_i to `rdata_o`, as DC reports it: 1.0179 ns (512 × 64), 0.8709 ns (128 × 256), 0.7599 ns (128 × 39), each the macro's `t_access_ns`. With the `DFF_X1` setup of 0.0415 ns the slack is 8.9405, 9.0875 and 9.1985 ns (MET).
- Leakage, `report_power -cell`: `u_m0` 140.4320 nW, `u_m1` 134.6910 nW, `u_m2` 25.3707 nW, each the `cell_leakage_power`; the `black_box` group's 300.4937 nW is their sum. The flops leak 62.26 µW. The dynamic numbers are DC's default toggle rates (PWR-414, PWR-415, PWR-428 in the report) and mean nothing here.
- `report_area`: 3 macros / black boxes, 35 052.75 µm² (13 502.9 + 19 262.8 + 2 287.05), flops 3 558.81 µm², total cell area 38 611.57 µm².

## Failures on the way

- `ram table -j 32` left 35 empty `tmp/cacti_d*_w*.*` folders (`arc check: 35 left over`). Each point was in `cacti.zip`; the folders had no files. `arc.workdir` deletes with `shutil.rmtree(..., ignore_errors=True)`, so a final `rmdir` that fails on NFS leaves an empty folder silently, most likely while a `.nfs` stub of a just-closed CACTI file is still there; at R1's `-j 2` it did not show. Removed with `rmdir`; `arc check` then: nothing left over.
- Probe, first try: DC's Verilog reader (`read_verilog`) rejected the generated `top.v`, and DC went on without a design. `analyze -format sverilog` reads the same file without a message; the probe then set `sh_continue_on_error false`.
