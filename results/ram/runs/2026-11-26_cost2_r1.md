# COST2 R1, 26 Nov 2026: the SRAM table at itrs-lstp

Claude in the chat, in its sandbox (2 cores, host g++, CACTI built by `pixi run ram-build` at `1ffd8df`), on `ry/cost2` from main ("TIERA4: SimdLanes scalar forms characterized (X99)"). The banks move from CACTI's itrs-hp cells to itrs-lstp, cells and peripherals both, and a bank's leakage becomes CACTI's subthreshold plus gate leakage (X47 · X100). The hp table is dropped. No macro is made here: R1b makes the lstp macros on the server.

## What changed

- `ram/ram.py`: `CELL = "itrs-lstp"` for `-Data array cell type` and `-Data array peripheral type`; `VDD_CACTI` 1.1, so `E_SCALE` is 1; `leak_uw` = `leak_sub_uw` + `leak_gate_uw` (CACTI's "Total leakage power of a bank" and "Total gate leakage power of a bank"), the two apart in `point`'s columns; `vdd_check` reads `-Vdd[1]` (lstp) and stops on any itrs-hp (`-Vdd[0]`) line; the `.lib` header names the cell type and the leakage as subthreshold plus gate; `GEN_VERSION` 2, so every macro is regenerated.
- `results/ram/nangate45/ram_table.csv`: rebuilt, same grid and columns, `leak_uw` now subthreshold plus gate.
- `results/snb/nangate45/dma_copy.json`: rebuilt by `pixi run dma-copy` on the new table (D184's bank price only; the measured groups are unchanged); `report/test_dma_copy.py` pins 322.41 nJ.
- `ram/README.md`, `docs/cost_tables.md` ("SRAM banks"), `docs/DECISIONS.md` (X47 · X100).

## The vdd check

`vdd check: 28 itrs-lstp (-Vdd[1]) lines, all 1.1 V, none itrs-hp; energies scaled by 1.0000`. The lstp blocks are the cell, the peripherals and the sleep transistor (power gating off); 8 lines of flavor 3 (lp-dram, 1.1 V) are the DRAM wordline device, as at hp.
