# Run note: CSYN1 R1, n2048 on the untiered HLS points, 10 Nov 2026

The HLS side of CSYN1 R1 (`results/forge/runs/2026-11-10_csyn1_r1.md` has the repos, licences and the forge side). X55 gives every untiered point an `n2048` task; this round adds it and runs it at W = 4 on nangate45 for Vitis opt, Bambu and XLS (four kernels each) and for relu dace (W = 1) and matadd dace (W = 4).

## What changed

- `sim/sources/hls.py`: `EXTRA_SIZES` by tier and kernel (`"*"` for every kernel of a tier): tier done `{"n": 2048}`; untiered relu and axpy `{"n": 2048}`, matadd `{"m": 32, "n": 64}`, rowsum `{"m": 16, "n": 128}`, each tagged `n2048` (`REF_TAG`) whatever its shape. `sizes_for` takes them (shared by `sim/sources/bambu.py` and `sim/sources/xls.py`, whose sizes come from the same function); a size whose element count is not a multiple of W is refused. Pins from each `params.json`'s config at those values (dslx-exercises' `cfg` for XLS); expected cycles from cosim's line (X19) and the Bambu and XLS lines (X44).
- `default_size` never picks `n2048`: rowsum has no `b64`, and its default (the last cosim size, m8_n64) would otherwise have become n2048 (see the forge note, Surprising).
- X56 for every source: the window is the measurement, `expected_cycles` and `expected_diff` columns beside it, failing only past 5 %.
- The dace cases of axpy and rowsum stay parked (their RAM, R3).
