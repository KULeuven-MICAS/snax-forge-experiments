## Odd things

- **opt at W = 32 keeps `lane<long,32>` as a module in the two-input kernels** (dot, vecadd, vecmul, axpy, matadd; not relu, vsum, rowsum). The top instantiates `<kernel>_lane_long_32_s` 64 times, each a 2048-bit right shift by a constant `i * 64`, and registers both 2048-bit beats (`a_reg`, `bb_reg`) and the 64 lane values. vecadd opt has 8363 register bits at W32 against 1143 at W16 and 2285 for vecadd dace W32; DC sequential area goes 8728 -> 65923 µm² (W16 -> W32), and total area 23774 -> 130572 (5.5x), while vecadd dace goes 24573 -> 46826 (1.9x). Cycles are unchanged (19, as dace). At W <= 16 the opt Verilog has no lane module.
- The same shows in Vitis's LUT estimate at W32: vecmul opt 275464 against vecmul dace 136619 (2.0x), where W8 has 34276 against 34379. DC narrows it to 673130 against 615379 µm², because the multipliers dominate.
- **unopt takes more cycles at every W > 1 than at W = 1**, as W4 and W8 already did: vecadd unopt n512 is 513 at W1, 2049 at W2, then falls to 1569 at W32. W2 is the slowest unopt point of every kernel.
- opt cycles are N/W plus a fixed 2 to 5 at every W (vecadd opt: 512 + 2, 256 + 2, ..., 16 + 3 = 19), so at W32 the overhead is a sixth of the run. dot opt grows from + 3 to + 5 (21 at W32), axpy opt is 20, relu opt 18.
- matadd equals vecadd and rowsum equals vsum at every new W, in cycles and DC area, as at W = 1, 4, 8.
- DC run time follows the multipliers: the four slowest runs are the 64-bit multiply points at W32; the slowest adder-only run is vecadd/opt_w32 at 387 s.
