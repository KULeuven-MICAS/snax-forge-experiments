## Odd things

- **The DaCe case is not at W = 1 only.** vecadd, vecmul and matadd have `dace_w1` to `dace_w32` in `rtl/` and all six are synthesized; dot, vsum, axpy, relu and rowsum have `dace_w1` only (dot, axpy and rowsum parked).
- **Energy at DC's default activity follows the logic in front of the multipliers, not the work done.** With no vectors, DC sets toggle rate 0.1 and static probability 0.5 on the inputs and propagates them. At equal area, vecmul `dace_w16` is 100.1 pJ per cycle and `opt_w16` 13.47 pJ on nangate45 (124,948 vs 124,575 µm²), 30.3 vs 5.82 pJ on nangate15; nearly all of the gap is `e_comb_pj` (88.2 vs 5.16 pJ on 45). The same holds at W = 1 between unopt and opt: vecmul 9.83 vs 1.33 pJ, dot 10.76 vs 2.51 pJ (nangate45). vecadd `dace_w16` is 13.93 vs `opt_w16` 7.07 pJ, mostly `e_reg_pj` (6.05 vs 0.40). `e_total_pj` compares points only as far as the default activity holds; the 45 / 15 energy ratio in the ratio table spreads from 2.05 to 3.58 for the same reason.
- **`fmax_mhz` is a lower bound, not the libraries' speed.** At a 10 ns clock DC keeps area-optimized structures: in vecadd `opt_w8` the worst path runs from `A_TDATA[320]` through a 64-bit adder, 4.54 ns on nangate45 and 0.58 ns on nangate15. In the ratio table the 45 / 15 fmax ratio is 0.144 to 0.161 except for relu (0.225 to 0.263), whose worst paths are short (0.96 to 1.69 ns on nangate45, 0.14 to 0.31 ns on nangate15, over all W).
- **Leakage per area is about 1.8x higher on nangate15:** median 0.0347 µW/µm² against 0.0193 on nangate45, so leakage falls by about 1.9x where area falls by 3.2 to 3.6x.
- **Area ratio follows the share of multipliers** (ratio table): 3.19 to 3.26 for dot, vecmul and axpy opt and vecmul dace, which are mostly multipliers; 3.15 at W = 1 rising to 3.43 to 3.47 at W = 32 for their unopt case, which keeps one multiplier while the registers grow with W; 3.44 to 3.60 for the adder-only kernels. A 64-bit multiplier alone is 6,925 / 2,203 = 3.14x; flops and adders shrink more.
- **Worst slack on nangate15 is axpy `opt_w16`** (9.067 ns), not `opt_w32` (9.095 ns) as on nangate45 (3.990 ns).
- vsum and rowsum `unopt_w2` differ by 0.012 % in area on nangate45 (identical on nangate15) with the same Verilog apart from names; DC's result depends a little on names and order, as seen in `2026-10-27_wsweep_r2.md`.
- nangate15 took 9 % longer in DC (14,293 against 13,071 s of DC time; slowest run axpy `opt_w32`, 826 against 632 s).
