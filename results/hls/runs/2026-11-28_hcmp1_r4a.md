# Run note: HCMP1 R4a, the probe's Vitis and Bambu points of vecadd, relu and dot, 28 Nov 2026

HCMP1's probe (X118) at one spec: forge's `i8` preset (int8 in; int32 products, sums and outputs), W = 8, 4096 elements a stream, full-range random data. hls-exercises R3 moved vecadd, relu and dot to it; this round builds and simulates their Vitis `opt` and Bambu points, removes the int64 and `dace` rows and folders of the three kernels, and rewrites the reports. Synthesis and power on these points, and forge's points, are R4b.

**Result:** all six points pass. Vitis `opt`: II = 1 on `BEAT`, 512 trips, cosim 514 cycles (`Pass`), timing met, portable. Bambu 2026.11: II = 1 on `BEAT` (BB2), 513 cycles first beat to last (`cycles_done` 514), stall runs PASS, portable. dot runs at II = 1 at int8 in Bambu, where it ran at II = 2 at int64 (W = 4 and 8, BAM3 R1).

## Step 2: Bambu (`bambu.py run --kernel vecadd relu dot -j32 --sim questa --sim-jobs 6`)

The first run failed to compile all three (`unknown type name 'vacc_t'`, `undeclared identifier 'acc_t'`). Bambu builds against its own header, hls-exercises `bench/include/bambu/lanes.h`, which R3 had not moved: it had no `acc_t`/`vacc_t` and fixed 64-bit lanes. It now has `acc_t` (from `-DACC_T`, else `data_t`), `vec_t = ac_int<8·sizeof(data_t)·W>`, `vacc_t = ac_int<8·sizeof(acc_t)·W>`, and lane accessors that take the lane width from the beat (WB / W bits). With `data_t == acc_t` (every int64 kernel) the beat layout is the old one: a C simulation of all eight Bambu kernels with the new header (g++ 8.5 with Bambu 2026.11's `ac_int.h`, every size and W in their `params.json`, 62 points) passes; their Bambu RTL was not rebuilt.
