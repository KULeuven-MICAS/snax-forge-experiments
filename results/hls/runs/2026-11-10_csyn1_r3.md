# Run note: CSYN1 R3, DaCe at n2048 with SRAM macros, and the split tables, 10 Nov 2026

E7's tables lacked three Vitis dace points, parked because DaCe's buffer is a RAM Nangate has no cells for: `done/dot/dace_w1` (`__tmp0`), `untiered/axpy/dace_w1` (`__tmp0`) and `untiered/rowsum/dace_w1` (`y_buf`). This round rebuilds the three at their n2048 size in hls-exercises, swaps Vitis's RAM for a CACTI macro where one exists (X62), synthesizes and measures them on nangate45, and adds the parts at n2048 to `compare_hls.md` (X63). The kickoff note the prompt cites, `claude/dace1-kickoff-2026-11-07.md`, is in none of the four repos or their histories; the round follows the prompt.

**Result:** all three rebuilt, co-simulated (`Pass`) and measured; every window equals cosim's count at n2048 (4101, 4101, 2137), every output matches the reference. dot and axpy's `__tmp0` (2048 x 64) are macros; rowsum's `y_buf` (16 x 64) is flops: CACTI refuses 16 words and the buffer has two ports. `compare` writes both tables with the three rows unparked and no refusal.

## Step 2: the macro swap (X62)

- `ram/vitis_ram.py`, used by `syn/syn.py` and `sim/pwr.py`: each `*_RAM_AUTO_1R1W` instance's `DataWidth`, `AddressRange` and `AddressWidth` from its parent's instantiation; only a one-port RAM (`address0`, `ce0`, `d0`, `we0`, `q0`, `reset`, `clk`); the macro from `ram.py`'s `make_macro` (`pixi run ram lib <depth> <width>`, no mask); a wrapper with Vitis's module name and ports around the macro cell, plain wiring (`ce0` to `req_i`, `we0` to `we_i`, `address0` to `addr_i`, `d0` to `wdata_i`, `q0` from `rdata_o`, `reset` unused), written into the DC run folder (kept in its zip, `ram/*.v`) and put in the run's own `filelist.f` in place of Vitis's file. DC links the macro's `.db` after the library check (`SYN_RAM_DBS`); the plan is in the run's `tech.json` (`resolved.ram`). A dry run only looks macros up.
- **Macros:** `sram_d2048_w64` for dot and axpy: CACTI 49,275.1 µm², read 9.50298 pJ, write 12.5817 pJ (at 1.1 V), access 0.370 ns. `16 x 64` (and 8 x 64, the old size): CACTI `ERROR: no valid data array organizations found` (PITFALLS C4).
- **Read-first.** Vitis's RAM reads first (on a write, `q0` takes the old word); the macro, as `tc_sram`, keeps the last read word on a write. In dot and axpy one pipelined loop only writes `__tmp0` (`VITIS_LOOP_62_1`, `VITIS_LOOP_19_1`: `we0`, no `q0`) and the next only reads it (`VITIS_LOOP_37_1`, `VITIS_LOOP_37_2`: `q0`, no `we0`), and the top's FSM starts the reading loop only after the writing loop ends (states 2 then 4 in dot, 2 then 5 in axpy): DaCe never uses `q0` after a write, so the behaviours differ nowhere it looks. The netlist simulations confirm it: every output beat matches.
- **rowsum** stays Vitis's RAM, built from flops with `syn.py --with-ram`: CACTI has no 16-word array, and the buffer has a second read port (`address1`, `ce1`, `q1`), which a one-port macro cannot take; `vitis_ram.plan` refuses it by its ports.

## Surprising

- **The RAM is most of a dace point:** 85 % of dot's and axpy's area and 60 % of their energy per task; DaCe keeps the 2048 products in memory between its two loops, and the window is about twice the beats (4101 cycles for 2048 elements).
- **CACTI's leakage of the 2048 x 64 macro, 8.5 mW,** is 49 times the rest of dot's leakage in DC (8,529 against 175 µW); it is in the `.lib` and in DC's and PT's black-box group, and not used (X13, X50).
- **rowsum dace's 16-word buffer from flops** (9,232 µm², 1,024 bits with their multiplexers) is half the point.
- **PT's averaged macro energy was within 0.03 %** of the counted one here, unlike RAM1's probe (PT7): the gap depends on the mix, and these loops separate reads from writes.
