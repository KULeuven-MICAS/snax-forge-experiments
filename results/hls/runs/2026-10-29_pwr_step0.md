## Simulation

**Testbench.** `ap_clk` 10 ns. `ap_rst_n` low for 5 cycles, then 3 idle cycles. The TB drives every input with a nonblocking assignment at the rising edge and samples there. `ap_start` rises and holds until an edge samples `ap_ready`; `loop_count` = 64 with `loop_count_ap_vld` high for the first `ap_start` cycle only. A and B are always valid and hold each beat until the handshake; `C_TREADY` = 1; each C beat is compared on the handshake, and X or Z in `C_TDATA` (or on `C_TVALID` after reset) fails. 200 idle cycles after `ap_done`, then `$finish`; a timeout of 10,000 cycles per run.

**Power-on state: `vopt +initreg+0`.** Vitis's RTL starts its unreset registers with an `initial` block (`b_fu_76`, the beat counter, has no reset branch; `#0 b_fu_76 = 32'd0`). The netlist has no such block, so its flops start at X. At the start edge the RTL clears the counter, but DC's logic for that clear also depends on the X compare `b_fu_76 == loop_count`, and the X stays: on nangate45 all 64 beats pass, then `C_TVALID` goes X and `ap_done` never rises (timeout); on nangate15 X appears after 6 beats (`sim/vsim_n15_forge_no_initreg.log`). `+initreg+0` sets every reg and UDP state to 0 at time 0, as the RTL's initial blocks do. With it both netlists pass.

**Result (nangate45).** Both stimuli PASS: 64 beats, 0 mismatches, 0 X/Z. Cycles 66, cosim 66 (`results/hls/hls_cycles.csv`, done/vecadd/opt, n512, W = 8): no offset. The definition that matches cosim: rising edges from the first edge that samples `ap_start` = 1 (edge 9, 95 ns) to the first edge that samples `ap_done` = 1 (edge 75, 755 ns). In the VCD, `C_TVALID` is sampled high at edges 11 to 74 (the 64 beats), and `ap_done` and `ap_ready` are high together in the cycle after the last beat.

**Windows.** The boundaries sit on falling edges, where nothing toggles, so a window holds exactly the transitions of the rising edges inside it. The TB's `$stop`: 90 ns (busy opens; the next edge is the first start edge), 750 ns (busy closes, idle opens; the next edge is the done edge), 2750 ns (idle closes). Busy is 660 ns (66 cycles), idle 2000 ns (200 cycles). `$stop` works from a do-file with `onbreak {resume}` (and `onfinish stop` so `quit -f` runs); `when` was not needed.

**+acc.** Without `+acc`, `power add /tb_vecadd_opt_w8/dut/*` finds nothing (`vsim-3561 No objects found matching 'dut'`) and the SAIF is empty. `+acc=n` works for `power add` and `vcd add`. Speed, 5000 back-to-back runs of the full stimulus with `+NOSTOP +RUNS=5000` (about 345,000 cycles): no `+acc` 29.9 s, `+acc=n` 25.3 s, `+acc=npr` 32.3 s; 500 runs: 4.15 s and 3.51 s. The differences are run-to-run noise: `+acc=n` costs nothing measurable on this flat netlist of 3572 nets.

**power add.** `power add /tb_vecadd_opt_w8/dut/*` (no `-r`): the netlist's own 3572 nets, ports included. `-r` adds one cell-internal net (`U1074/A1`, an input of `OAI33_X1` tied to `1'b0`); `-r -nocellnet` gives the same as `-r`, because the nangate45 models carry no `` `celldefine `` for vsim to know a cell by. `-internal` keeps 1991 nets (the internal ones, no ports).

**Run times.** vsim wall time for one run (load and 2750 ns): 1.1 to 1.3 s without power or VCD, 1.5 to 1.6 s with `power add`, two SAIF reports and the VCD. PT: 6.0 to 7.5 s per run, licence checkout and link included.

## Questa's SAIF against the VCD

The plan's do-file (power reset / power on at each boundary, power off, `power report -all -bsaif`) runs, but vsim 2022.4's SAIF is wrong in four ways (`sim/saif_compare.txt`, against vcd2saif of the same VCD):

1. **DURATION is the time since 0**, not the window: 750,000 and 2,750,000 ps for windows of 660,000 and 2,000,000. PT divides TC by it.
2. **Nets with no event in the window get T0 = T1 = TX = 0**: 246 of 3572 in busy (all 32 `loop_count` bits, 31 of `loop_count_preg`, `ap_rst_n`, ...), 3558 in idle. PT then has no static probability for them: idle leakage comes out 86.4 µW instead of 108.7.
3. **TC drops toggles with the zero-width glitches.** In a zero-delay simulation a net can change twice in one time step; vsim counts such pulses in IG (36,234 in busy) and takes real toggles out of TC with them. TC differs from the VCD's on 3326 of 3570 nets in busy, below it on 3266; the sum is 77,599 against 94,918 (-18%). Example `n1031` (`XNOR2_X1 U1456`): TC 0, IG 56; the VCD has 29 toggles.
4. **The first edge of a window is lost**: in idle, `ap_clk` has TC 399 (VCD 400) and T1 1,005,000 (1,000,000); the done edge's transitions (`ap_CS_fsm`, `ap_done`) are missing.

Without `power reset`, cumulative reports at each `$stop` have T0 + T1 + TX = DURATION on every net, which repairs 1 and 2, but TC is not monotonic (`n1031`: 1 at 90 ns, 0 at 750 ns, as IG grows), so no difference of reports repairs 3.

The VCD of the same run is right: PT's `read_vcd -zero_delay -strip_path tb_vecadd_opt_w8/dut -time {90 750}` and vcd2saif's SAIF read with `read_saif` agree within 0.1% on every run (rows `v` and `s` below). PT's `read_saif` prints nothing about the Questa SAIF's defects, and the annotation coverage is 100% for it too.

## For the design

- **Power-on state.** Gate-level simulation of Vitis's netlists needs `vopt +initreg+0` on both libraries: Vitis starts unreset registers (`b_fu_76`) in an `initial` block, the netlist starts them at X, and DC's logic keeps the X (an X-pessimism artefact, not a design bug). The TB's X check on `C_TVALID` and the timeout catch it. A seeded `+initreg` (random) run would show whether any point depends on the 0.
- **Visibility.** `vopt +acc=n` is required for `power add` and `vcd add`; it costs nothing measurable here. `power add <dut>/*` without `-r` is right for a flat netlist. For netlists with sub-designs (107 hierarchical references across the 116 netlists), `-r` is needed, and `-nocellnet` does not keep cell internals out of it: the nangate45 models have no `` `celldefine `` (nangate15's neither), so vsim cannot tell a cell; expect cell-internal nets such as tied constant pins (1 here), which PT will not map.
- **SAIF from Questa: do not use.** `power report -bsaif` after `power reset` / `power on` gives DURATION since time 0, T0 = T1 = 0 on quiet nets (most nets in an idle window), TC without the toggles that coincide with zero-delay glitches (-18% of all toggles in busy), and a lost first edge per window; it under-reports energy by 13% busy and 34% idle on this point, and PT reports 100% coverage on it without a warning. The VCD of the same run is right. Two routes that agree within 0.1%: PT `read_vcd -zero_delay -strip_path <tb>/<dut> -time {t0 t1}` (ns), or `vcd2saif -instance <tb>/<dut> -time t0 t1` (in the VCD's time unit, ps here, not ns) and `read_saif`. A VCD of this point is 0.75 MB for 275 cycles; for bigger points or longer runs, dump only the windows (`vcd on` / `vcd off` around them, or one VCD per window) or pipe (`read_vcd -pipe_exec`).
- **Window boundaries on falling edges.** `$stop` from the TB at the falling edge before the first start edge and before the done edge, with `onbreak {resume}` in the do-file; vcd2saif and `read_vcd` take the same times.
- **Wire load in PT.** PT uses the library's default wire-load model whatever `auto_wire_load_selection` and `remove_wire_load_model` say; `report_design` hides it, `report_wire_load` shows it. `set_load 0` and `set_resistance 0` on every net match DC's wire_load off. On nangate15 (no wire-load models) this is a no-op, but harmless.
- **P0 is not a calibration.** PT and DC differ by 2.4% (register group 35%) with default activity because their vectorless propagation differs on enable feedback; with identical annotated activity they agree to 0.00%. The uniform-activity pair (`pt_power.tcl uniform`, `dc_readback.tcl uniform`) is the setup check per technology.
- **Coverage is not a SAIF check.** Every run, the defective Questa SAIF included, shows 3566/3566 nets annotated. Check an activity file before PT: T0 + T1 + TX = DURATION on every net and DURATION = the window (`saif_compare.py` does both against a reference).
- **Groups.** `clock_network` and `register` split the flops' energy by whether Q toggles; compare e_clk + e_reg, not e_clk alone.
- **Idle costs a third of busy** (record only; idle is out of scope per X13). 4.04 pJ per idle cycle (no clock gating in Vitis's netlist), 0.35 of a busy cycle; energy per task depends on how long a block idles between runs.
- **Cycles.** The TB's count from the first edge sampling `ap_start` = 1 to the first edge sampling `ap_done` = 1 equals cosim's latency (66) with no offset.
