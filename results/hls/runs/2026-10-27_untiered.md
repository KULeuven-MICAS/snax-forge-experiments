# Run note: tier untiered, first run, 27 Oct 2026

First run of tier untiered (relu, matadd, rowsum, axpy at W = 1, 4, 8) at hls-exercises `5c867c1`, with the conventions of tier done: axis ports without register slices, run-time pins `ap_vld` pulsed with the start, one `rtl/` folder per hardware point. Vitis ran all 60 points at 3.33 ns, then DC synthesized every hardware point at 10 ns. Tools are as in `2026-10-27_done.md`, with `python3.11` throughout. Vectors and DaCe sources are the committed ones.

## Odd things

- **rowsum is vsum.** Its unopt/opt Verilog differs from vsum's only in the output port's name (`y` for `out_r`), and its DC area and cells equal vsum's. rowsum opt m8_n64 takes 514 cycles, as vsum opt n512: no stall at the row restarts.
- **rowsum dace is 46/50 cycles above M + M x N + M** (118 for 72, 578 for 528). Its top divides `loop_count / len` for DaCe's M with a 36-cycle sequential `udiv` before the loops start (43 FSM states in the top). Unlike vsum dace, its `len` pin is read.
- **axpy dace runs two loops one after the other**: x times alpha into `__tmp0` (2 BRAM_18K), then + y, so about 2N + 6 cycles (134, 1030), as dot dace.
- csynth clock estimates at 3.33 ns repeat tier done's: axpy 3.648 ns everywhere (the 64-bit multiply, as dot), rowsum opt 3.023 (as vsum opt), matadd dace W4/W8 2.528 (as vecadd dace W4/W8), all `missed`; rowsum dace 3.417, also missed. relu and matadd unopt/opt meet it.
- relu unopt W1 takes 2 cycles per element (129, 1025), while matadd unopt W1 takes 1 (129 for 128).
