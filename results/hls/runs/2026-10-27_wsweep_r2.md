## Odd things

- **The pragma renamed 61 folders that the plan expected to stay as they were.** Hardware is the same (cycles equal everywhere), but DC area moved on 6 of them by 1 to 23 µm² (at most 0.02 %): vecadd, vecmul and matadd unopt_w32, axpy unopt_w2, **unopt_w8** and unopt_w32. DC's result depends a little on net names and order; axpy unopt_w8 is a W = 8 point; its row had stood since tier untiered's first run (`2026-10-27_untiered.md`, 48092).
- **bench.py never clears rowsum's W = 32 folders.** `cmd_run` starts a folder afresh only when the run covers every size in params.json; the valid rule drops rowsum m4_n16 at W = 32, so `unopt_w32` and `opt_w32` are always compared with the last run's Verilog. Here unopt's `l`/`l_1` swap gave `rtl_same no: rowsum.v` and a side folder `rtl/untiered/rowsum/unopt_m8_n64_w32/` (58 folders). `bench.py collect` for rowsum cleared and placed them again (57 folders, `ref`); only `rtl`/`rtl_same` changed in the CSV. bench.py is not changed here.
- **The lane fix also cut one cycle** at W = 32 (19 -> 18 at 512 elements): the beat and lane registers sat in the pipeline. Area at W32 is now about 1.9x W16 for vecadd/matadd opt (46080 / 23774), and opt is now slightly below dace for vecadd (46080 < 46826) and vecmul (614725 < 615379).
- dot, vecmul and axpy opt W32 drop only 8 %: sequential area falls by about 33k µm² (vecmul 65923 -> 33237) and combinational by about 26k (4 %); the 32 64-bit multipliers keep the rest.
