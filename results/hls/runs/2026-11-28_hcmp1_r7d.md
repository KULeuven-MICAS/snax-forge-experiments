# Run note: HCMP1 R7d, tier A with the masked bank index, and why Bambu refuses matvec_col, 28 Nov 2026

R7c's `--print-dot` graphs showed Bambu splitting mvt's, bicg's and atax's loops at the bank (and buffer) access indexed by the beat-in-row counter, and gesummv's at the branch where the compiler had sunk beta's product. The chat changed the shared source (hls-exercises `7bfe746`): the bank and buffer indexed by `k & (R - 1)`; gesummv adding alpha · (A's part) + beta · (B's part) every beat, only the write in the branch. matvec_col is unchanged. Rule as before: no Bambu-only source; Bambu's rows stay as built.

**Result:** Vitis: 4 of 4 pass at II = 1 with R7c's cycles (514; atax 526); mvt's, bicg's and atax's Verilog is R7c's circuit (equal once Vitis's line-numbered and numbered names are masked); gesummv's multiplier changes. Bambu: 4 of 4 build and pass. gesummv's loop is now one block and Bambu tries it, but refuses it at II 1 and 2 as it does matvec_col: 1,024 cycles, down from 1,599. mvt, bicg and atax are unchanged (3,072, 2,048, 2,065 cycles): the mask keeps the index in range, but Bambu still lowers the access to a switch over 0 to 7 with a default that leaves the loop. For matvec_col and gesummv, Bambu's scheduler logs `resMII=1` and `recMII=1`: no resource and no loop-carried dependence (matvec_col's `acc`) bounds II above 1; it fails at II 1 and 2 without naming a constraint.

## Step 3: matvec_col, why Bambu refuses it

One rebuild at verbosity 4 (`-v4`, the highest; this release build has no `--debug` option), with `--print-dot`, in `build/diag_r7d/matvec_col/`; gesummv the same, for comparison. Both logs, under `HLS::ParametricListBased(DynamicMobility)`:

```
resMII=1
recMII=1
minII=1
maxII=2
Loop pipelining not possible for loop (BB2)
```

- **Not a resource:** resMII = 1, the resource-bound II.
- **Not a dependence through `acc`:** recMII = 1, the recurrence-bound II; matvec_col's sums (one beat-wide register) and gesummv's row sum do not force II above 1.
- **Not timing, as far as the log shows:** the loop's schedule without pipelining has a minimum slack of 7.25 ns at 10 ns (matvec_col; estimated 364 MHz). The pipelined attempt's timing is not logged.
- **What fails:** the modulo scheduler itself, at II 1 and 2 (`maxII=2` ends the search), and the log names no constraint. The loop is one block (`LF.dot`), its stream reads unconditional (A, p) and the q write predicated; without pipelining the reads sit in control step 2 and the products, the sums and the write in step 3. The "LOADs or STOREs with unaligned accesses" note in the memory allocation also appears for dot, which Bambu pipelines at II = 1, so it is not the cause. What is left in common to the two refused loops and absent from dot is not established here.

matvec_col's row stays as built in R7c (1,024 cycles).
