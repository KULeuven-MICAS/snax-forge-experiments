## The two forms

With $n = n_{rw} + n_{ro} + n_{wo}$ inputs and $b$ banks:

$$\text{factor:} \quad A = A_{table}(n, b)\thinspace\Big(1 - f_{ro}\frac{n_{ro}}{n} - f_{wo}\frac{n_{wo}}{n}\Big) \qquad \text{crosspoint:} \quad A = b\thinspace(c_{rw}\thinspace n_{rw} + c_{ro}\thinspace n_{ro} + c_{wo}\thinspace n_{wo}) + d\thinspace n + e\thinspace b + g$$

$f_{ro}$ and $f_{wo}$: at each of the 9 (inputs, banks) pairs, $1 - A(\text{all read-only}) / A(\text{all read-write})$ (and the same with all write-only), then the mean of the 9. The crosspoint coefficients: ordinary least squares (`numpy.linalg.lstsq`, unweighted squared error in µm²) over the 27 grid points, the two `i36_b32` points held out.

$f_{ro}$ rises slightly with the banks (0.389 at 16 to 0.407 at 64); the three read-only 64-bank points are also the ones with max-capacitance violations (X70's open item), so that rise may read slightly high. $f_{wo}$ does not move with the banks. Fitted per bank count alone (no $d$, $e$ terms), $c_{rw}$ is 161.3, 159.1, 160.0 and $c_{ro}$ 98.8, 96.4, 95.2 at 16, 32, 64 banks; the shared fit stays within ±2.2 % at every 64-bank point. The two forms agree: $1 - c_{ro}/c_{rw}$ = 0.403 ≈ $f_{ro}$, $1 - c_{wo}/c_{rw}$ = 0.412 ≈ $f_{wo}$.
