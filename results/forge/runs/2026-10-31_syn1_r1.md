# Run note: SYN1 R1, all 48 forge points on nangate45 and nangate15, 31 Oct 2026

The area sweep of snax-forge's accelerators: the 48 bundles of R0 (`2026-10-31_syn1_r0.md`), synthesized at 10 ns on both technologies with `syn.py --source forge`, after two additions to `syn.py`: an automatic licence retry (X17) and the `glue` share. Power is R2.

**Superseded for dot_5 and dot_6 (every W) by R1b** (`2026-10-31_syn1_r1b.md`): both problems below are fixed in snax-forge and those 12 points were resynthesized; the other 36 rows are R1's.

**Not every point ends ok.** On both technologies, 44 of 48 points are usable. dot_5 and dot_6 at W = 2 crash DC every time (`Error id=834758`, PITFALLS DC7), and dot_5 and dot_6 at W = 1 synthesize `ok` but are not dot products: snax-forge's RTL for them has no datapath and drives the output data with zero. Both need a decision before R2.

## Step 1: the licence retry

- `run_one` now looks for a licence failure in `logs/dc_shell.log` and `logs/dc_stdout.log` after each DC run; on one it prints `retry <point> on <tech>: licence error: <the line>`, empties the run folder and runs DC once more; `run` ends with `licence retries: <n> (<points>)`. After a second licence failure the row is `failed: licence (<the line>)`. It applies to both sources.
- **The match is narrower than "license".** Every good DC log contains it: the banner of every `dc_stdout.log` (`license agreement`, `license keys`, `license restrictions`, all 232 HLS runs) and the DesignWare table of every log (`Licensed DW Building Blocks`). And R0's nangate15 failure printed only `Fatal: Design Compiler is not enabled. (DCSH-1)`, with no "licens" in it. So `LICENCE_ERROR` matches `licensing error`, `Unable to obtain feature`, `is not enabled. (DCSH-1)`, or an `Error:`/`Fatal:` line naming a licence, case-insensitive, and prints the `licensing error` line first when there are several. It matched none of the 234 existing runs (232 HLS, R0's two forge runs) and both forms of R0's failure.
- Checked with a stub `DC_SHELL`: a stub that always prints R0's -92 failure gives a retry, then `failed: licence (Error: Design-Compiler Internal licensing error number -92: Feature removed during lmreread, or wrong SERVER line hostid.)`; a stub that fails on a licence once and then otherwise gives a retry, then `failed: no area report`; both count 1 retry.
- HLS unchanged: `syn run done/vecadd/opt_w8 --tech nangate45 --dry-run` is identical to R0's output, and `syn summary` to the one before R0. No HLS point was rerun.

**A fix found on the way.** A run with no area report was always labelled `failed: library mismatch`: syn.py looked for `dc_syn.tcl: library mismatch` anywhere in `dc_shell.log`, and DC echoes the script into that log, `puts` line included. It now matches the line only at a line's start (the echo is indented). Only failure labels change: a run with an area report never reaches that test, and no HLS log has the line at a line's start. nangate45's sweep ran with the old code, so its two crashes were printed as library mismatch at first; their committed rows come from the reruns below.

## Step 2: the glue share

Forge rows of `synth_dc.csv` have four more columns after `snax_forge_commit`: `csr_um2`, `ctrl_um2`, `datapath_um2` (the total, children included, of the `synth_dc_modules.csv` rows whose design is a key of `bundle.json`'s `modules`: `snax_forge_csr`, `snax_forge_ctrl`, `snax_forge_datapath`) and `glue_um2` = `area_um2` minus the three. Boundary optimization stays on. `run` prints a point whose glue is negative or above 1 % of the total, and, added after nangate45 showed the need, one with no row for a module (empty glue); neither fails the point. On R0's nangate15 probe it gives 0.2948 um2, R0's 0.295.

Glue over the sweep: 0.532 to 2.128 um2 on nangate45 (one to four `INV_X1` of 0.532), 0.147 to 0.442 um2 on nangate15, at most 0.077 % of a total (vecadd W = 1 on nangate15); none negative, none above 1 %.

pixi run syn run 'done/*/forge*' --source forge --tech nangate45 -j 4 --cores 4
