# Run note: SYN1 R3, power at n2048 on every forge point and on tier done's HLS points, 1 Nov 2026

R2 (`2026-10-31_syn1_r2.md`) built the forge power flow and probed two points. This round runs it at `n2048` (one task of N = 2048 elements) on all 48 forge points, and runs the HLS flow at the same size on the 61 tier-done HLS points (vecadd, vecmul, vsum, dot) on their existing netlists, so both sides have dynamic energy for the same task. No synthesis was rerun, `syn/runs/` is untouched, and the HLS rows already committed (64 beats, cosim sizes) are unchanged: the HLS CSVs only gained rows.

**Every point passes every check on both technologies.** 48 of 48 forge points and 61 of 61 HLS points `ok` per technology (dot `dace_w1` parked: RAM). Every output matched `sim/ref.py`; every window equals its expected cycles (forge: also the COUNTER read back); the SAIF, annotation and dump-scope checks pass. The two R2 probes reproduce R2's rows exactly.

**The setup check still fails on nangate15 for the forge netlists' register group** (-0.25 % to -1.08 %, tolerance 0.1 %), while the HLS reference point passes as in R1. Not changed here; R4 decides.

## Odd or open

- **HLS unopt at W >= 2 costs more per element as W grows** (nangate45 vecadd 19.6 pJ at W = 2 to 89.4 pJ at W = 32): its cycles are 2 to 4.5 per element (vecadd 8193 at W = 2 and 6273 at W = 32, cosim's line a = 8 to 98 per beat; vsum 4289 at W = 32), the lanes done one after another, so the busy window grows with the hardware. cosim has the same counts; it is the design, not the flow.
- **HLS vecadd unopt W = 1 is the cheapest vecadd point at W = 1** (nangate45 1.259 pJ per element; opt 1.962, dace 2.153, forge 2.229): 67 flop bits against opt's 134, and its register group 0.03 pJ per cycle against opt's 0.84; its data path has no 64-bit register.
- **forge dot 5 and dot 6 rise at W = 32** (nangate45 dot 5 12.449 to 12.566 pJ per element from W = 16; dot 6 12.894 to 13.098; nangate15 also dot 6 from W = 8 to 16): their cycles are T + W + 1 and T + W + 2 (SerialAccumulate after the MAC reads one lane per cycle at the end), 97 and 98 cycles for 64 beats at W = 32, so a third of the window is the tail. dot 2 to 4 keep falling with W.
- **forge's control costs the same per cycle at every W** (nangate45 vecadd and vecmul: csr 0.695, ctrl 0.760 pJ per cycle; dot about 0.94 and 1.45 to 1.70), so its share of e_run follows the cycles: vecadd W = 1 csr + ctrl 65 % of e_run (nangate45), W = 32 6 %; vsum W = 1 53 %. The datapath's e_run is flat in W where the work is (vecadd 1.58 nJ, vecmul 23.1 nJ on nangate45 at every W).
- `vecmul opt` W = 16 to 32 rises by 0.03 % (13.878 to 13.882 pJ per element): flat, within noise of the data.
- The setup check's forge register gap on nangate15 (Step 2) is open for R4.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md` X19 still describes the default stimulus (64 beats); `n2048` is an extra size like the cosim sizes, documented in `docs/power.md`. Whether X19 or a new X-number records it is for R4.
