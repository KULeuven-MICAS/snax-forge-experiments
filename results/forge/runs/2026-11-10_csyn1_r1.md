# Run note: CSYN1 R1, forge's untiered points, the forge source, a probe at W = 4, 10 Nov 2026

E7's untiered tier (relu, axpy, matadd, rowsum) had HLS rows but no forge rows and no n2048 task. This round makes the flow measure forge's six untiered recipes (`relu`, `matadd`, `axpy`, `axpy_baked`, `rowsum`, `rowsum_2`), adds n2048 to the HLS untiered points (`results/hls/runs/2026-11-10_csyn1_r1_hls.md`), and probes every recipe at W = 4 on nangate45 end to end (DC, Questa, PT). The sweep (R2) and the DaCe macros (R3) are not started. snax-forge was read only, through `forge/query.py` and `forge/bundles.py` (X14).

**Result:** all six forge points and all 14 HLS points of the probe are ok; on every one of the 20 the window equals the expected count exactly (and forge's COUNTER), every net is annotated and every output matches the reference. Three choices beyond X55 and X56: X57 (the int32 recipes at int64), X58 (a forge point's program from snax-forge's LOWER at its n2048 shape) and X59 (16 cores at most, licences left free).

## Step 1: forge points on today's snax-forge

- **Recipes in folders.** `forge/bundles.py` `find_recipe(name)` finds `recipes/**/<name>.json` under the snax-forge checkout, leaves out `recipes/custom/`, and refuses a missing or ambiguous name; `forge/query.py` imports the same function (one resolver). A point whose recipe fails gets `failed: recipe (...)`.
- **Per-point sets.** Each `forge/points.json` entry has an optional `sets` list, given to the sandbox after `W` and before the global `--set`, and tagging the work and design folder names in that order; `point.json` records the combined `sets`. The 48 tier done points got `sets: ["N=2048"]` (X58: the design point is the n2048 task's, so LOWER writes that task).
- **dtypes (X57).** Found when bundling: snax-forge's relu, matadd and axpy kernels are int32 (its D113), so `relu`, `matadd`, `axpy` and `axpy_baked` bundle int32 datapaths (4 lanes in 2 channels at W = 4, K1 one 32-bit CSR), against int64 everywhere else (PITFALLS E4). Asked Ryan; agreed: an entry's `dtypes` (every container int64) is written into snax-forge's recipe, in a copy in `tmp/forge_recipes/<work name>.json` given to the sandbox and deleted after the point. The work name ends `_int64`; `point.json` records `dtypes` and `recipe_file`. Checked first by hand on axpy: int64 ports, `K1_LO` and `K1_HI`, `dirty: false`.
- **30 untiered points**, `untiered/<kernel>/<case>_w<W>` at W = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16: case `forge` for relu, matadd, axpy, rowsum; `forge_baked` for axpy_baked; `forge2` for rowsum_2. Sets: relu and axpy `N=2048`; matadd `M=32`, `N=64`; rowsum and rowsum_2 `M=16`, `N=128`. 78 points in all.
- **Tier done still bundles to the same SystemVerilog.** dot_4 and vecadd at W = 4, rebundled on `e8bb93e` (with `N=2048`) into a scratch folder and compared with the existing `rtl/forge` zips (`f9764d5`): not byte-identical, because snax-forge renamed things since: the tag carries the design's name (`snax_forge_dot_4_shell_wrapper`, `snax_forge_dot_4_ctrl`, ...; it was `snax_forge_*`) and `ElementwiseLanes_*` is `SimdLanes_*` (PITFALLS E5). With those two renames masked (and the generator's header line), every SV file of both points is identical, and the file lists match. The scratch bundles were deleted; the 48 tier done zips in `rtl/forge/` are still `f9764d5`'s (R2 rebundles them).
- **The six W = 4 untiered bundles** (`pixi run forge-rtl 'untiered/*/*_w4' -j 6`): all made, 12 to 14 s each, `snax_forge_commit` `e8bb93e`, none dirty. No recipe failed at sandbox, design or gen-rtl.

## Step 2: the forge source

- **What changed.** `forge/query.py`: `program BUNDLE_DIR` (LOWER's task list for the bundle's design point: `lower/derive.py`'s `task_list`, checked by `check_values`; `lower/c.py`'s `job_writes` for JOBS and JOB_BEATS; each slot read from a scalar container with its CSRs, `gen/accelerator.py`'s `scalar_csrs`; per streamer task the element order, `lower/derive.py`'s `addresses` over its bounds and strides, mapped back through the container's L1 layout to row-major indices; the bound symbols); `point` returns the recipe's baked scalars; `ref` takes the design point's symbols and the point's dtypes. Both `point` and `cycles` read D157's nested `accelerator.json` (`flat`) and today's `gen.accelerator.units`; the old flat form still loads. `sim/sources/forge.py`: tb.json from `program` (below), the streamer file `<tag>_streamer_<accelerator>.hjson`. `sim/pwr.py`: the forge stimulus, the CSR writes with scalars, stream names, the X56 cycle rule and columns, `--cores`. `sim/ref.py`: rowsum takes `m` from forge's shape.
- **Sizes.** `SIZES` is gone: a forge point has one size, `n2048`, whose shape is the point's sets (from `point.json`); a bundle whose sets are not X55's shape for its tier and kernel (`sim/sources/hls.py` `extra_sizes`) is refused, as is a design point whose symbols differ from the sets.
- **JOBS and JOB_BEATS from LOWER** (X58), at W = 4:

| point | symbols | JOBS | JOB_BEATS | scalars | expected cycles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| relu | N 2048 | 512 | — | — | 512 |
| matadd | M 32, N 64 | 512 | — | — | 512 |
| axpy | N 2048 | 512 | — | alpha int64: K1_LO (2), K1_HI (3) | 512 |
| axpy_baked | N 2048 | 512 | — | none (alpha baked = 3) | 512 |
| rowsum | M 16, N 128 | 16 | acc 32 | — | 513 |
| rowsum_2 | M 16, N 128 | 4 | acc 128 | — | 513 |
| tier done dot_4 (rebundled) | N 2048 | 1 | mac 512 | — | — |
| tier done vecadd (rebundled) | N 2048 | 512 | — | — | — |

  Tier done's values are the old rule's (vecadd N / W jobs; dot one job of N / W beats).
- **Scalar CSRs.** A unit's read-write CSR must be a JOB_BEATS or a scalar the program writes, else the point is refused. axpy: alpha drawn as X19's data pin (rand64, seed 0, after x and y), written low word then high word before START; `pins.json` and the reference get it. axpy_baked: alpha = 3 from the recipe's `bake_scalar`, no CSR write. Both pass alpha to `sim/ref.py` and to `forge_ref_check`, whose cache key now holds the scalars (else axpy and axpy_baked, same kernel and inputs, would compare against each other's reference).
- **Stream order.** Every stream of every tier done point checked (dot_4, vecadd rebundled) and every elementwise untiered point is the identity, and so are rowsum's A and both rowsums' y. rowsum_2's A at M = 16, N = 128, W = 4: beat t holds rows 4⌊t/128⌋ to 4⌊t/128⌋ + 3 of column t mod 128, checked element by element over all 512 beats. The TB feeds and checks each stream in its streamer's order (`order` in tb.json, null for the identity).
- **In-place y.** axpy's y has an input and an output stream on one container; they were one TB stream name (`+y_HEX` twice). Now `y_in` and `y_out` (tb.json `name`); the input is the drawn y, the output is checked against αx + y on that original y (`sim/ref.py`'s `y_out`), and snax-forge's kernel body agrees (`y += alpha * x`, at int64).
- **Cycles (X56).** The window is the measurement (`cycles`); `expected_cycles` and `expected_diff` = expected − window for every source, and for forge `counter` and `counter_diff`; a run fails only when either is more than 5 % of the window away. Bambu's and XLS's lines (`cycle_line`) are still required to be integer and exact at the tool's own sizes; that is the line's check, not the window's.

## Surprising

- **int32 kernels** (step 1, E4, X57): without the check, four of the six forge recipes would have been compared at int32 against int64 HLS, with no error.
- **axpy against axpy_baked:** the run-time alpha costs 8.1 × the area (29,924 against 3,705 µm²) and 6.8 × the energy per cycle: four 64 × 64 multipliers against a multiply by 3; csr also grows by the two K1 registers (1374.7 against 849.9 µm²).
- **rowsum_2 against rowsum:** the same 513 cycles, but rowsum_2's datapath is 2.0 × rowsum's and its energy per cycle 1.9 ×: W lane accumulators against one adder tree.
- **rowsum's default size:** `hls.default_size` took the last size in tb.json when there is no b64; with n2048 appended, rowsum's default (m8_n64) became n2048, and the first HLS run of rowsum W = 4 (Vitis, Bambu, XLS) wrote its n2048 reports at the top of `power_reports/.../rowsum/*_w4.zip` and its modules into `power_pt_modules.csv`. Caught in the diff; `default_size` now never picks `n2048`, the three zips and three modules CSVs were restored from git and the three points rerun (the same energies to the digit). Only `tb.json` differs in those zips (it lists n2048).
- **DC-Expert at `--cores 16`:** six DC runs held 8 DC-Expert licences (above).
