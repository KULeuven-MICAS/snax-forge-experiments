# Run note: SYN1 R1b, dot_5 and dot_6 resynthesized from snax-forge's fixes, 31 Oct 2026

R1 (`2026-10-31_syn1_r1.md`) left dot_5 and dot_6 at W = 1 and W = 2 unusable on both technologies. Both causes are fixed on snax-forge's branch `ry/span1-read` (KULeuven-MICAS/snax-forge#46, KULeuven-MICAS/snax-gvsoc#41, open): the group rule now raises `read` at every span, so SerialAccumulate at lanes 1 updates and firtool keeps the datapath; and SerialAccumulate's update is written `acc := Mux(first && lane === 0, 0, acc) + x`, which avoids the RTL shape DC Y-2026.03-SP2 crashes on (PITFALLS DC7). This round rebuilt all 48 bundles from the branch, found the points whose SV changed, and resynthesized exactly those 12 on both technologies.

**All 48 forge points are now usable on both technologies.** Every rerun ends `ok` with timing met, and dot_5 and dot_6 at W = 1 and 2 have their datapath.

**The rows of the 12 points carry the branch's commit** (`snax_forge_commit` `2741b462f7ee8947ae7be4d61c5ba81db053ba65`) until the PRs merge; then a short step rebundles from main, checks the SV is identical and sets the commit to main's. The other 36 rows are R1's (`520a566`).
