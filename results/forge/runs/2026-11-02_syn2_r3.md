# Run note: SYN2 R3, the before-and-after report, 2 Nov 2026

R1 and R2 (`2026-11-02_syn2_r1.md`, `2026-11-02_syn2_r2.md`) reran SYN1's 48 forge points on snax-forge's CTRL1 (`f9764d5`). This round writes the report: `report/analysis_syn2.py` (`pixi run syn2`) and `results/analysis/syn2/syn2_before_after.md`, records X27, and adds the README, PITFALLS and notes lines.
