- **dot 2's ctrl loses 48.6 % of its area but 14.6 % of its energy** (nangate45, W = 8); dot 3 loses 67.6 % and 43.7 %. The 32 × 32 multiplier D153 removes had JOBS and the units' T at its inputs, constant during a run, so it cost area and little switching; dot 3's two removed counters switched every cycle.
- **The datapath's energy moves by at most 0.22 % (nangate45)** with its SV unchanged: R1's DC noise in its netlist (up to 0.13 % of its area), not a change of activity; the stimulus and cycles are identical.
- **csr's energy grows less than its area on nangate45** (W = 8: +1.8 % to +4.8 % against +3.4 % to +7.9 %) and more on nangate15 (+7.1 % to +9.4 % against +4.3 % to +6.7 %); both small in nJ (at most 0.009 nJ at W = 8).
