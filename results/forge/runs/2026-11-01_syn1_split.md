# Run note: SYN1 control/datapath split, 1 Nov 2026

The R5 analysis note (`results/analysis/syn1/syn1_analysis.md`) could split forge by its wrapper's instances (csr, ctrl, dp) but HLS only by register names, since Vitis writes control flat beside the datapath. This round puts every cell of every n2048 point, HLS and forge, both technologies, on the control or datapath side by one rule and adds section 9 to the note. Nothing was resynthesized or resimulated; nothing under `syn/runs/`, `sim/runs/`, `results/hls/` or `results/forge/<tech>/` changed.

**The rule:** datapath is the forward cone of the data input ports (Vitis `*_TDATA`, forge `stream2acc_*_data_i`), through logic and through every flop it reaches until nothing new is reached; control is every other cell. On forge it reproduces the hierarchy: no csr or ctrl cell is in the cone, every dp flop is, and the dp cells outside it are logic behind ctrl's enables and lane select inside dp's blocks (at most 0.18 % of the area and 0.113 % of e_dyn).

## What ran

- `sim/scripts/pt_cells.tcl`: reads `pt_power.tcl`, drops its final `exit`, evaluates it unchanged (the same library, netlist, wire-load setting, constraints and SAIF), then marks the data cone with `all_fanout -flat -only_cells -trace_arcs all` from the data ports and from the outputs of every flop reached, until no new flop is reached, and writes every leaf cell's area, internal, switching and leakage power and cone to `cells.tsv`.
- `report/flop_split.py pt` (`pixi run split pt -j 12`): one PrimePower run per point in `sim/split_runs/<side>/<tech>/<point>/`, with the SAIF the power run kept (`sim/runs/.../n2048_s0/busy.saif`) read in place; a run is ok only if its Power Group table equals the committed run's `pt/power.rpt`.
- `report/flop_split.py collect` (`pixi run split collect`): `results/analysis/syn1/split_<tech>.csv` (per point: kind flop or comb, cone, unit; cells, area, dynamic energy per cycle) and `split_checks_<tech>.csv`.
- `report/analysis_syn1.py` (`pixi run analysis`): section 9, `fig8_control_split.png`, one summary line and one open question.

## Checks

- 218 of 218 runs ok: every Power Group table equals the committed run's.
- 218 of 218 points pass collect's checks: the cells' area equals `synth_dc.csv`'s area_um2 within 0.0019 µm²; their energy per cycle equals `power_pt.csv`'s e_dyn_pj within 0.0001 % and the flops' its e_flop_pj within 0.0003 %; the flop count equals flop_bits; every HLS flop is a register of the point's Verilog by name, and no register has more flops than bits.
- The per-cell sums close exactly on the two points probed first (`done/dot/opt_w4`, `done/dot/forge3_w4`, nangate45): the flops' internal power equals the clock_network plus register groups' internal power, their switching the register group's, the logic's the combinational group's.

## Found on the way

- **A parser bug in R5's register inventory.** `hls_reg_entries` (was `hls_registers`) took the names assigned on the clock edge with `name <=`, missing assignments to a bit range (`N_reg_64[30 : 4] <= ...`), so registers of vecadd and vecmul unopt and dace were left out of section 3's "RTL" bit sums; collect failed on `done/vecadd/dace_w16` until it was fixed. Section 3's table changes on 8 rows (e.g. `vecadd/unopt_w32` RTL 6450 → 8498 against DC's 6455 flops) and its caption now states the Verilog's sum minus DC's count, 0 to 2043 bits: DC removes the constant bits. No other number in sections 1 to 8 changed.
- **Names against the rule.** Section 3's loop-state class holds the accumulator (data) and the counters (control) together; Vitis's registered loop indices and increments (`l_reg_*`, `add_ln*_reg_*`, `N_reg_64`) carry data-pipeline names and are control. Section 9 tabulates both.
