# Run note: CTRL2 R5, forge's Part A points on snax-forge's leaner control unit (D168), 11 Nov 2026

snax-forge's CTRL2 (merged, D168) keeps the settings only in `<tag>_csr`, which holds a write to a busy group's START or settings until the cycle after its run ends; the control unit reads them live with no copy, and the busy-cycle CSR, renamed BUSY_COUNTER, sits in a new child of the csr, `snax_forge_<design>_peripherals`. This round renames what reads the CSR, makes `report/compare.py`'s csr part leave the peripherals out (X65), rebundles, resynthesizes and powers CSYN1 Part A's 70 forge points at W = 1 to 16 on nangate45, rewrites the X64 pages with the new numbers and keeps the old ones only in `results/compare/ctrl2_before_after.md`.

**Result:** 70 of 70 points ok in synthesis and power. Every window equals BUSY_COUNTER read back, snax-forge's expected count and Part A's window exactly. The ctrl's flops equal snax-forge's `estimate/bits.py` count at all 70 points (elementwise 66 to 34, a fold 131 to 67, dot 2 196 to 132, dot 4 164 to 100). The datapath moved by at most 0.05 %. The ctrl's area fell 28 to 41 %, its energy per cycle 33 to 49 %, and the reported csr 22 to 36 % (the 279 µm² peripherals leave it). On X63's basis an elementwise unit barely moves, because BUSY_COUNTER now counts in forge's total without csr; a fold gains up to 11.9 % in area and 8.5 % in energy. 9 verdicts against the HLS cases turn in forge's favour, none against it. `arc check`: nothing left over.
