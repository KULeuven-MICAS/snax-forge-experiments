## Model

The table snax-forge reads is `results/char/<tech>/char.json` (`report/char_export.py`, `pixi run char-export`; X37). Registers are bits: a flop that loads costs e_load per cycle, one that holds e_hold; a block keeps its comb part per fire (its busy window's e_comb; LinkBuffer's stream, per load); ctrl is its flops x e_hold x a counter factor. Per unit and task, with C_acc the task's cycles:

$$E_{\mathit{unit}} = n_{\mathit{fire}}\thinspace\big(e_{\mathit{comb}} + n_{\mathit{bits}}\thinspace(e_{\mathit{load}} - e_{\mathit{hold}})\big) + C_{\mathit{acc}}\thinspace n_{\mathit{bits}}\thinspace e_{\mathit{hold}}, \qquad E_{\mathit{ctrl}} = f_{\mathit{ctrl}}\thinspace n_{\mathit{bits}}^{\mathit{ctrl}}\thinspace C_{\mathit{acc}}\thinspace e_{\mathit{hold}}$$

### The constants

`flop.area_um2` is the mean of `seq_area_um2 / seq_cells` over the 21 registered points (flops and clock gates): nangate45 5.296378 µm² (nangate45 5.2914 to 5.3191).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nangate45 | `flop.e_load_fj` | 17.8153 | 17 | 17.3557 | 17.9869 | 0.1595 | 3.54 % |
| nangate45 | `flop.e_hold_fj` | 0.0655 | 14 | 0.0024 | 0.0754 | 0.0253 | 111.49 % |
| nangate45 | `ctrl.counter_factor` | 83.20 | 48 | 43.2954 | 148.2543 | 37.4544 | 126.16 % |

e_load is the mean busy e_bit of the points whose flops all load every cycle (Mac, TreeAccumulate and SerialAccumulate busy, LinkBuffer depth 1 stream); e_hold the mean idle e_bit (as above); the counter factor, per tier-done forge point ctrl/ctrl's e_dyn per cycle (`power_pt_modules.csv`) over its sequential cells x e_hold, the mean over the 48 points with a power row rounded to two decimals (the 48 int64 tier-done points, forge/points.json's without a `types=` set). `synth_dc_modules.csv` has no per-module `seq_cells`: ctrl's cells are its `seq_um2` / `flop.area_um2`, rounded. The factor spreads widely: a gated control unit's energy follows its counters' activity more than its cell count.

### Per block point

e_fire = e_comb + n_bits (e_load - e_hold), pJ per fire, and the hold term n_bits e_hold, pJ per cycle, from char.json at each measured point. n_bits is the block's register bits from its params (lanes x depth x the output's width; SimdLanes none; FifoPacker its held slots and counter, of which (r - 1) / r of a slot and the counter load per push, r = d_out; FifoSlicer its word, counter and full flag, of which 64 / r of the word and the counter load per pop, r = d_in, X94); `seq_cells` adds the block's clock gates, one per register enable (`pixi run char-export` prints each). A lanes value char.json does not hold takes the entry's `lanes_rule`: `copy`, W / W0 x the point at W0 (SimdLanes, Mac, LinkBuffer, whose W = 8 and 32 points are the copy's checks above); `interpolate`, linear in lanes (the int64 TreeAccumulate and SerialAccumulate); `slope`, a narrow fold's point at W0 = 8 plus its slope per lane (the type axis, above); a LinkBuffer at a narrower dtype is the int64 one x w / 64.
