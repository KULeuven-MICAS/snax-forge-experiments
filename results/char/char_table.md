Generated by `report/char_table.py` (`pixi run char-table`) from `results/char/<tech>/char_synth.csv` and `char_power.csv`; the known limits also read the files they cite. Do not edit by hand.

- **Commits:** snax-forge `e8611d51d5653e4c6e246184831136ec363727f5` (every row); snax-forge-experiments `ae0c6b7fe57095b7b0fb0f728a510ac8a58ac103` (the commit that wrote the power CSVs).
- **Settings:** clock 10 ns; synthesis as SYN1 and SYN2 (X1 to X4, X22; the block as top, X30), clock-gated (X79 · X87 · X88; a clock gate is a sequential cell, X83); power rand64 seed 0, 128 cycles per window (SerialAccumulate's busy: 128 beats of W cycles), inputs at the rising edge, a lead-in slot before each window, the VCD cut per window (X31, X32).
- **Units:** area µm²; energy pJ per cycle of the window (e_comb, e_flop = clock + register + sequential, e_dyn = the two); e_bit and e_hold fJ per sequential cell per cycle.
- **Windows:** busy (a fire every cycle) and idle (the enable at 0, every other input held) for ElementwiseLanes, Mac, TreeAccumulate and SerialAccumulate; stream for LinkBuffer (a load every cycle at full occupancy), a link that is not loading costing its flops x e_hold (X33).

## Model

The table snax-forge reads is `results/char/<tech>/char.json` (`report/char_export.py`, `pixi run char-export`; X37). Registers are bits: a flop that loads costs e_load per cycle, one that holds e_hold; a block keeps its comb part per fire (its busy window's e_comb; LinkBuffer's stream, per load); ctrl is its flops x e_hold x a counter factor. Per unit and task, with C_acc the task's cycles:

$$E_{\mathit{unit}} = n_{\mathit{fire}}\thinspace\big(e_{\mathit{comb}} + n_{\mathit{bits}}\thinspace(e_{\mathit{load}} - e_{\mathit{hold}})\big) + C_{\mathit{acc}}\thinspace n_{\mathit{bits}}\thinspace e_{\mathit{hold}}, \qquad E_{\mathit{ctrl}} = f_{\mathit{ctrl}}\thinspace n_{\mathit{bits}}^{\mathit{ctrl}}\thinspace C_{\mathit{acc}}\thinspace e_{\mathit{hold}}$$

### The constants

`flop.area_um2` is the mean of `seq_area_um2 / seq_cells` over the 21 registered points (flops and clock gates): nangate45 5.296378 µm² (nangate45 5.2914 to 5.3191).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nangate45 | `flop.e_load_fj` | 17.8153 | 17 | 17.3557 | 17.9869 | 0.1595 | 3.54 % |
| nangate45 | `flop.e_hold_fj` | 0.0655 | 14 | 0.0024 | 0.0754 | 0.0253 | 111.49 % |
| nangate45 | `ctrl.counter_factor` | 82.87 | 41 | 49.2494 | 146.8615 | 36.5755 | 117.79 % |

e_load is the mean busy e_bit of the points whose flops all load every cycle (Mac, TreeAccumulate and SerialAccumulate busy, LinkBuffer depth 1 stream); e_hold the mean idle e_bit (as above); the counter factor, per tier-done forge point ctrl/ctrl's e_dyn per cycle (`power_pt_modules.csv`) over its sequential cells x e_hold, the mean over the 41 points with a power row rounded to two decimals (tier done's 48 but the 7 W = 32 bundles made before snax-forge D168, which have none). `synth_dc_modules.csv` has no per-module `seq_cells`: ctrl's cells are its `seq_um2` / `flop.area_um2`, rounded. The factor spreads widely: a gated control unit's energy follows its counters' activity more than its cell count.
