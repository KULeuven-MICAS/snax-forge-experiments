## What R3 wrote

- **`report/char_table.py`** (`pixi run char-table`, a task in `pixi.toml`): reads `results/char/<tech>/char_synth.csv` and `char_power.csv` of both technologies and `char/points.json`, checks that every point is there on both, every row ok, one snax-forge commit and R2's windows, and writes:
  - **`results/char/char_table.md`**: the commits and settings, e_hold per technology with its spread, one section per block (every point, both technologies side by side: lanes, depth, op, area, comb, seq, flops, then per window e_comb, e_flop, e_dyn) with a short "how it scales" line computed from the rows, and the known limits. The limits read only the files they cite: SYN2's `synth_dc_modules.csv` (TreeAccumulate inside dot 4), R1's `char_power.csv` at the pushed commit `03b7a83` through `git show` (duty50), R2's run note's diagnostic table, and SYN2's `power_pt.csv` and `power_pt_modules.csv` (the dot 4 to 6 Mac).
  - **`results/char/<tech>/char_draft.json`**: `"status": "draft"`, `tech`, `clock_ns`, `units`, `source` (both commits, the stimulus and windows), `e_hold_fj`, and per point `point`, `datapath`, `params` (exactly `char/points.json`'s, so `blocks.json`'s names and values; checked equal for all 27), `area` (`total`, `comb`, `seq`), `flops`, `fmax_mhz` and `energy` per window (`busy` and `idle`, or `stream`: `comb`, `flop`).
- **`docs/DECISIONS.md`**: X28 to X36 and their rows in the index (every index anchor checked against GitHub's slug of its heading).
- **`results/char/README.md`**, **`README.md`**'s CHAR1 row and run block: the files, the table and the draft, how to rebuild (`char-rtl`, `syn --source char`, `pwr --source char`, `char-table`).

## What the table says

As `results/char/char_table.md` computes it:

- **e_hold**: 7.1476 fJ per flop per cycle on nangate45 and 1.8911 fJ on nangate15 (14 registered points, range 0.17 % and 0.06 %).
- **Lane copies** (X(W) / (W X(1)) at W = 8 and 32): area 1.000 for ElementwiseLanes, Mac 0.984 to 1.002; busy 1.005 to 1.027 (add, mul, Mac); Mac's idle 0.999 to 1.000.
- **TreeAccumulate**: busy e_comb grows about linearly with W (0.744 to 11.747 pJ on nangate45 from W = 1 to 32), its 64 flops' e_flop and idle flat. **SerialAccumulate**: busy e_comb grows slowly (0.907 to 1.377 pJ from W = 2 to 32).
- **LinkBuffer** at W = 1, D = 2 to 16 (D = 1 apart, no read mux): e_dyn = 0.3101 D + 1.2800 pJ on nangate45, 0.1299 D + 0.4730 pJ on nangate15; d1 at W = 8 and 32 is 1.012 to 1.017 of W x d1_w1.
- **Known limits**: the free `first` (TreeAccumulate alone is 363 to 380 µm² above dot 4's on nangate45, 105 to 109 µm² on nangate15); an isolated fire (+25.4 % Mac, +29.7 % TreeAccumulate, R1's duty50); nangate45's LinkBuffer slope (0.710 e_dyn, 0.564 e_flop of 64 e_hold; the diagnostic changed e_flop by -2.3 %); and **the Mac inside dot 5 and 6 at W = 32**: it fires 64 of 97 and 98 cycles there (SYN2's `power_pt.csv`), and (64 E_busy + 33 or 34 E_idle) / cycles gives 271.607 and 268.985 pJ on nangate45 against 261.339 and 258.460 inside (1.039, 1.041), where busy alone is 1.546 and 1.564; nangate15 1.029 and 1.029 (busy alone 1.537, 1.552). The same mix gives 1.017 to 1.041 for dot 4 to 6 at every W: busy and idle reproduce the Mac in its accelerators within 4 %.
