## The model

Registers are bits: a flop that loads costs e_load per cycle, one that holds e_hold; a block keeps only its comb part per fire; ctrl is its flops x e_hold x a counter factor. Per unit and task, with C_acc the task's cycles:

$$E_{\mathit{unit}} = n_{\mathit{fire}}\thinspace\big(e_{\mathit{comb}} + n_{\mathit{bits}}\thinspace(e_{\mathit{load}} - e_{\mathit{hold}})\big) + C_{\mathit{acc}}\thinspace n_{\mathit{bits}}\thinspace e_{\mathit{hold}}, \qquad E_{\mathit{ctrl}} = f_{\mathit{ctrl}}\thinspace n_{\mathit{bits}}^{\mathit{ctrl}}\thinspace C_{\mathit{acc}}\thinspace e_{\mathit{hold}}$$

## Odd

- **The reference values use the measured point at the design's W, not the copy rule.** With every e_comb from the lanes rule (W x the lanes-1 point for ElementwiseLanes and Mac) the kernel ratio is 0.991 to 1.032 (vecmul nangate45 W = 8 0.995, 23.13 nJ; dot 5 1.026). The export uses a point char.json holds as it is and the rule only for a lanes value it does not hold (X37); `char_table.md` shows both ratios.
- **The sum units inside dot 4 and 5 cost far more than their firings.** Per unit the Mac is at 1.027 to 1.038 of SYN2's, but the sum at 0.06 to 0.19 (TreeAccumulate, one fire) and 0.41 to 0.78 (SerialAccumulate, W fires). Inside the accelerator the sum's inputs are the Mac's outputs, which change every cycle the Mac fires, so its comb works every cycle: SYN2's sum costs 2.85 pJ per cycle in dot 4 at W = 8 on nangate45 against its busy e_comb 3.25, and 1.12 in dot 5 against 1.12 (`power_pt_modules.csv` against char.json). The Mac's overshoot (+0.89 nJ in dot 4 at W = 8) and the sum's shortfall (-0.61 nJ) partly cancel. The model prices a unit's comb only when it fires.
- **ctrl's flops are not in SYN2's modules CSV as a count.** `synth_dc_modules.csv` has area per cell, not `seq_cells`; ctrl's flops are its `seq_um2` / `flop.area_um2`, an integer at all 48 points on both technologies (66 for vecmul, 131 for vsum, 164 for dot 4, 167 and 169 for dot 5 at W = 8 and 32). csr's seq_um2 is not a multiple of the flop area on nangate45 at 15 points (plausibly a second flop type, not checked); csr is outside the model.
- **The counter factor is computed from SYN2 and checked on SYN2.** The 8 checked points are among its 48, so ctrl's ratio (0.915 to 1.043) is not an independent check; vecmul's ctrl is the lowest on both technologies.
- **A block's n_bits is not in char.json.** It follows from the params (lanes x depth x the output's width; ElementwiseLanes none) and equals `seq_cells` at every point; snax-forge computes it.
- **SerialAccumulate has no lanes-1 point** (X29), so its interpolation starts at W = 2.
