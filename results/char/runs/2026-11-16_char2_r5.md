# CHAR2 R5: char.json by type pair (2026-11-16)

R5 turns R2's and R4's 81 points into the table snax-forge reads, with the rule over lanes Ryan chose after R3 and R4 (X89). No server run: `char-export` and `char-table` on the committed CSVs.

## What changed

- `report/char_export.py`: one entry per block and params, so one per type pair; the flop constants (area, e_load, e_hold) from the int64 points only, so they and the control factor are CHAR1's (5.296378 µm², 17.8153 fJ, 0.0655 fJ, 82.87); a narrow TreeAccumulate or SerialAccumulate gets the `slope` rule, the int64 cost per lane between W = 8 and 32 times w_in / 64; `copy` takes the pair's point at its smallest lanes; a LinkBuffer at a narrower dtype reads the int64 entry times w / 64; FifoPacker (not in `blocks.json`) has its params checked here and its `flops` and `load_bits` in its point; SimdLanes' null constants (`k`, `k1`, `k2`) are accepted. The points that check a rule are left out (`char_table.check_only`).
- `report/char_table.py`: SimdLanes for ElementwiseLanes, the in and out columns, FifoPacker's section, and "The type axis": what SimdLanes computes per pair, the Mac and the folds per pair, each rule against the points that check it, and the narrow registers' load energy.
- `report/test_char_export.py`: the constants from the int64 points, the check points left out, copy from a pair's W0, the slope, a narrow LinkBuffer by width, the packer's bits.
- X89 added, X29 and X37 amended; `results/char/README.md`.

## Found

- The forge comparison (vecmul, vsum, dot 4, dot 5 at W = 8) is unchanged: the int64 points equal CHAR1's.
- The rules against their check points (measured / rule): SimdLanes copy at 8/32, W = 8: area 1.000, energy 1.107 (add) and 1.063 (mul); TreeAccumulate slope at 8/32, W = 32: area 1.010, energy 1.018; SerialAccumulate: area 1.052, energy 1.322; LinkBuffer width at D = 4: area 1.278 (int8, on 38 µm²) and 1.030 (int32), energy 1.094 and 1.015.
