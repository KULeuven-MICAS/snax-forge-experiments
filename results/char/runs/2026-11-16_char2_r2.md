## Odd things

- **`lanes/mul_w1_i8_i16` is larger than `mul_w1_i8_i32` and `i8_i64`** (483.85 against 293.93 and 305.63 um2; busy e_comb 0.748 against 0.646 and 0.851 pJ), though each computes the same full 8 × 8 = 16-bit product. DC keeps each ALU a hierarchical cell (`ElementwiseAlu_w16_mul`, `_w32_mul`), whose operands are sign-extended int8: the 16-bit ALU costs 484 there and 585 on full int16 operands, the 32-bit one 294 on int8 and 1124 on int16. So DC exploits the copied sign bits far more in the 32-bit multiplier than in the 16-bit one; the RTL is as D175 says. A fit of the comb part on width should treat i8/i16 mul as an outlier or take the smaller of the two implementations (R3).
- **Narrow adds cost little over their input width**: `add_w1_i8_i16` to `i8_i64` 34.8 to 44.2 um2, `i16_i16` to `i16_i64` 65.4 to 78.5: the sum of two sign-extended inputs needs an adder of w_in + 1 bits and the sign copied above.
- **Lane copies hold at narrow types**: `lanes/add_w8_i8_i32` and `mul_w8_i8_i32` are 8 × their lanes-1 points in area (304.30, 2351.44 um2).
- **`char/checks.py --power`'s e_hold** now averages 23 registered points, the narrow ones included (0.0743 fJ, range 197 %): each block's idle is one clock gate's 0.0049 pJ, so e_bit falls with the bits behind the gate (X33, X83). The LinkBuffer depth fit's ratio against 64 e_hold is far above 0.7 (gated, X82: reported, not checked).
- `char/checks.py`'s RTL table against the local forge bundles (`rtl/forge/`, made at snax-forge `3c7ea00`): LinkBuffer, Mac and TreeAccumulate (vsum, dot 2 and 3) are byte for byte the module inside; SimdLanes differs only by the unused `clock` and `reset` it keeps alone (firtool drops them inside); SerialAccumulate and dot 4's TreeAccumulate differ where the accelerator ties a pin to a constant (CONTRACTS, `Emit --block`).
