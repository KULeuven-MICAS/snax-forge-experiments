# TIERA2 B6x: the Mac's bank of rows measured (2026-11-23)

snax-forge's B6b gave the accumulating `Mac` a bank of R rows (D191). Its estimate prices a bank as the char entry's comb, measured without rows, plus the catalogue's flops, `lanes * width(outType) * max(accumulate, regOut) * rows`. That leaves out the bank's read muxes and row enables. This round measures 9 points and proposes a form for the rest (X97); neither `char.json` nor snax-forge's estimate changes (the chat decides how the estimate takes X97).

## What changed

- `char/points.json`: 9 points that name `rows`, int8 in, int32 out, accumulating, unseeded, `regOut` false: `mac/acc_w4_i8_i32` (rows 1, the base at W = 4) and `mac/bank_r<R>_w<W>_i8_i32` at R = 2, 4, 8, 16 and W = 4, 8. The base at W = 8 is CHAR2's `mac/acc_w8_i8_i32` (snax-forge `0416557`), whose SV is byte for byte the one snax-forge `a0e5a1e` emits for it.
- `char/blocks.py`: a point may leave out an `int` the catalogue fills from `unit.params`. Emit then builds and lists it at its min, so char-rtl compares `emitted.json` against the point's params with the min filled in (`as_emitted`, snax-forge's open item 161, its `a0e5a1e`). The CHAR points without `rows` would otherwise fail a rerun.
- `sim/sources/char.py`, `sim/tb/char_bank_seq.sv`, `sim/pwr.py`: the TB kind `bank`, for a Mac of R ≥ 2 rows, with pins `fire`, `first`, `row` and `drain` (⌈log₂ R⌉ bits each). `busy`: R lead-in slots, the restart of each row in turn, then a fire every cycle with `row` = t mod R and `drain` = t − 1 mod R, as the control unit drives them, `first` 0. `idle`: `fire` 0, everything held at busy's last.
- `sim/ref.py`: the Mac's R rows, written at `row` and read at `drain`. `syn/syn.py`: a bank's `form` is `acc_r<R>`. `char/checks.py`: a Mac's bits times its rows; `--bank` prints this note's tables.
- `report/char_table.py`: `bank_study` and `table_points` leave the 9 points out of `char.json` and `char_table.md`; `char_export.py` and `test_char_export.py` read the points through it. Without that, a W = 8 bank would enter `char.json`'s Mac entries, and the block's two snax-forge commits would fail `load`.
- X97; X29 names it; PITFALLS DC20; `power.md`, "Char"; `results/char/README.md`.

- **Flops.** DC's sequential cells are the catalogue's W · 32 · R plus R clock gates, one per row, at every point; idle costs R × 4.90 fJ a cycle, the gates' share alone.
- **Two read muxes.** Presto infers two MUX_OPs of R inputs and W · 32 bits at every bank point: `drain`'s for `out`, and `row`'s for the add into the row (`s(row) + p`).
- **Area.** The rest, beyond the extra flops priced at 5.296 µm², is 59.8 µm² × W · R (about 1.87 µm² a bank flop) within 5.8 % on the 7 points DC maps to AOI22 and-or logic. One constant per lane per row fits; per lane per extra row (W (R − 1)) misses by 40 %, and per lane times log₂ R by 43 %.
- **Energy.** A fire's extra over the base is W · (0.343 log₂ R + 0.316) pJ within 14 % on the same 7 points. Most of it is comb: e_comb grows W · (0.167 log₂ R + 0.581) pJ, within 13 %. e_flop changes little, W · (0.048 R − 0.149) pJ: one row loads a fire, the others hold behind their gates.
- **The MUX2 point.** At R = 16, W = 8 (16 × 256 bits a mux) compile_ultra maps both muxes to 7,680 MUX2 cells (14,300 µm²). At `bank_r16_w4` (16 × 128) and `bank_r8_w8` (8 × 256) there is no MUX2. Against the forms this point's area rest is 1.94× and its energy extra 2.77× (PITFALLS DC20). Where between 2,048 and 4,096 bits a mux the mapping changes is not measured.
- **Today's estimate against DC.** A bank's area at 0.76 to 0.87 (0.63 at the MUX2 point) is the lower bound D191 says. Its energy is not: it loads all W · 32 · R bits each fire, where one row loads, so it is 1.05 to 3.37 times the measured.
