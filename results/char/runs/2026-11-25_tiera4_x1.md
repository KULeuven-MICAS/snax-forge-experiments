# TIERA4 X1: SimdLanes' scalar forms measured (2026-11-25)

TIERA4 builds gesummv (y = α A x + β B x) in snax-forge. Its literal form scales each matrix by a CSR scalar (`a * k`, one stream), its reordered forms end in one axpby (`k1 * a + k2 * b`, both scalars from CSRs). `char.json` held no SimdLanes with a scalar, so snax-forge's estimate priced all of these at 0 (`char.missing`), as it did `axpy_v1` (`k1 * a + b`), `axpy_v1_1` (k1 baked to 3) and `relu_v1` (`max(a, 0)`). This round measures the seven SimdLanes scalar forms snax-forge looks up, at W = 1, and puts each in `char.json` as its own entry, `copy` over lanes (X99). Their params are snax-forge's lookup keys at `ry/tiera4` `8d9128c` (the estimate carries a unit's CSR flags as gen-rtl fills them); `import-costs` and the totals are snax-forge's next step.

## What changed

- `char/points.json`: seven SimdLanes points at the end, lanes 1, every param as the lookup keys it: `lanes/mul_w1_i64_kcsr` (inTypes int64, op and out int64, mul, 1 stream, kCsr), `lanes/mul_w1_i8_i32_kcsr` (inTypes int8, op and out int32, mul, 1 stream, kCsr), `lanes/axpby_w1_i64_k1csr_k2csr` and `lanes/axpby_w1_i32_k1csr_k2csr` (axpby, 2 streams, k1Csr and k2Csr), `lanes/axpby_w1_i32_k1csr` (k1Csr), `lanes/axpby_w1_i32_k1_3` (k1 3), `lanes/max_w1_i32_k0` (max, 1 stream, k 0); every other k null and Csr flag false.
- `sim/sources/char.py`: `port_bits` gives a one-stream SimdLanes port a alone; `scalar_bits` the pins a SimdLanes reads from its CSRs (`io_k`, `io_k1`, `io_k2` at opType's width); `build` requires them in the netlist as inputs of that width (and no other port), checks W lanes of each data input the params name, and writes them as tb.json's `scalars` (only where there are some, so every earlier tb.json is unchanged).
- `sim/tb/char_lanes_seq.sv`: a parameter `NK` (default 0) and an output `scal`, one 64-bit slot a scalar pin; `+K_HEX` and `+K_N` give one line a window, applied at the window's lead-in slot and held through it. With NK = 0 `scal` is left open (vopt's TFMPC warning, not an error) and nothing is read.
- `sim/pwr.py`: `tb_top_char` passes `NK` and wires each scalar pin to its slot's low bits, only where there are scalars (the tops of earlier points are generated byte for byte as before); `char_stimulus` draws, after the data streams from the same generator, one full-range rand64 a scalar pin a window, its low bits, holds it from the window's lead-in, gives each slot's scalars to the reference and keeps the scalar pins out of the toggle check (the data ports keep it); the values go into `k.hex`, the run's output and tb.json's `scalar_values` (in `power_reports/<point>_cg.zip`).
- `sim/ref.py`: `char_ref` takes a SimdLanes of one stream (a alone) with k in b's place, from the slot's pin or baked; `max` and `min` compare by opType's signedness; `axpby` is `k1 a + k2 b`, `k1 a + b` without k2, at opType's bits; a cast to opType before the op and the result to outType, as the plain ops.
- `syn/syn.py`: `char_extra`'s `form` names a SimdLanes' scalars (`kcsr`, `k1csr_k2csr`, `k1csr`, `k1=3`, `k=0`; empty for a plain op).
- `report/char_table.py`: `in_widths` read `ws[1]` of a one-stream point (an IndexError), now a single width; `scalar`, `scalar_label` and `op_label` show a form's scalars in the op column (`mul (k csr)`, `axpby (k1 = 3)`); `block_rows` puts the scalar forms after every plain row (each existing row keeps its place and label); the scalar forms are a commit group of their own (`SCALAR`), as the mixed pairs; a section "Scalar forms" sets each beside the plain SimdLanes at its pair and W.
- `report/char_export.py`: the scalar forms' commit as `source.snax_forge_scalar` (`ct.source_key`); the entries needed no change, an entry being keyed by its params.
- Tests: `sim/test_char_ref.py` (new, in `pixi run test`: the spot checks, a pin's value per slot, the one-stream ports and scalar pins, the stimulus's data as without scalars and a value a window from the lead-in on); `report/test_char_export.py`'s `test_scalar_forms_own_entries`.
- X99; X29 names it; `results/char/README.md`; `docs/power.md`, "Char"; PITFALLS Q17; `char.json`, `char_table.md`.

Before the run, every existing point's reference was compared with HEAD's `char_ref` over its own schedule and rand64 beats (98 points, equal at every sample), and nine existing points' tb.json and generated top (SimdLanes plain and mixed, Mac, TreeAccumulate, SerialAccumulate, LinkBuffer) were built with HEAD's `sim/` and with the new one from their netlists: byte for byte the same.

## Found

The SV tops name their scalars: `SimdLanes_i64_i64_i64_n1_mul_kr`, `SimdLanes_i8_i32_i32_n1_mul_kr` (each with `ElementwiseAlu_w<op>_mul`), `SimdLanes_i64xi64_i64_i64_n1_axpby_k1r_k2r`, `SimdLanes_i32xi32_i32_i32_n1_axpby_k1r_k2r`, `SimdLanes_i32xi32_i32_i32_n1_axpby_k1r`, `SimdLanes_i32xi32_i32_i32_n1_axpby_k13` (`io_a_0 * 32'h3 + io_b_0`) and `SimdLanes_i32_i32_i32_n1_max_k0` (with `ElementwiseAlu_w32_s_max`). The scalar pins are as wide as opType: `io_k` 64 bits on the int64 scale and 32 on the int8 one (whose `io_a_0` is 8), `io_k1` and `io_k2` 64 and 32 on the axpbys; the one-stream forms have no `io_b`, the baked forms no scalar pin.

None has a sequential cell (`char/checks.py`: 0 registers, 0 clock gates, as expected; "Not as expected: none"): a CSR scalar is a pin, the register outside the datapath. What the scalar and the second multiplier add:

- **A scale by a CSR k is the plain multiplier, k held.** The one-stream mul is the same `ElementwiseAlu` with `io_k` where `io_b` was, and DC builds the same netlist: the int64 scale's area is `lanes/mul_w1`'s to the last digit (6,912.542 µm²), the int8 one's is X98's int8 × int32 mul's (833.112 µm²), the multiplier it is (a at int8 times k at int32), 2.83 times the plain int8 × int8 → int32 mul at its pair. A fire costs 0.79 (int64) and 0.80 (int8, against the mixed mul's 1.4259 pJ) of the two-stream multiplier: k does not toggle.
- **axpby with both scalars from CSRs is two multipliers and an add.** Beyond the add, its comb is 2.04 (int64) and 2.03 (int32) plain muls at the pair, within 2 % of 2 muls + 1 add (14,094.8 and 3,793.2 µm²); its energy 1.44 and 1.34 muls (16.87 pJ against 23.21 for two toggling muls and an add at int64; 4.42 against 6.40 at int32), both multipliers' scalar held.
- **k1 from its CSR alone** (`k1 * a + b`): 0.98 muls of comb and 0.71 of energy beyond the add, 1,924.8 µm² against a mul and an add's 1,963.3.
- **k1 baked to 3** is `a * 3 + b`, a shift-add: 129.5 µm² beyond the add (0.97 of an int32 add, 0.07 of a mul) and 0.19 pJ a fire.
- **relu's max with 0** is a select on a's sign bit: 33.5 µm² and 0.20 pJ, a quarter of an int32 add in area and half in energy.
- **One held value a window.** A held scalar's energy depends on its value (its ones select partial products): these are one rand64 value a window at seed 0, as every char point is one seed's (X19).

## char.json

`char-export`'s diff is the seven new entries, at the end of the SimdLanes list after X98's, each at its exact params (k, k1 or k2 baked or null, the Csr flags, `streams`, `inTypes` of one or two types), `copy` over lanes, a single W = 1 point, and in `source` the added `snax_forge_scalar` (`8d9128c…`) and the restamped `snax_forge_experiments`; every other entry and constant is byte for byte the committed one (flop area 5.296378 µm², e_load 17.8153 fJ, e_hold 0.0655 fJ, counter factor 83.20; `bank` unchanged). The entries are `mul (k csr)` int64 `{"lanes": 1, "comb_um2": 6912.542033, "e_comb_pj": 8.891865}`, int8 → int32 `{833.112003, 1.142523}`, `axpby (k1 csr, k2 csr)` int64 `{14343.252077, 16.87358}`, int32 `{3840.50802, 4.417724}`, `axpby (k1 csr)` `{1924.776009, 2.509546}`, `axpby (k1 = 3)` `{263.074002, 0.5689094}`, `max (k = 0)` `{33.516, 0.2010275}` (comb µm², e_comb pJ). snax-forge's `pair_params` at `8d9128c` gives each of these params back unchanged (one input width each; opType at most outType), and its `_lookup` drops the null values as the entry's key does, so the lookup finds them once snax-forge imports this table.

`source.snax_forge_experiments` names the last commit on the char CSVs, so the rows are committed first and `char.json` and `char_table.md` are exported again in a second commit, as in TIERA3 X1.
