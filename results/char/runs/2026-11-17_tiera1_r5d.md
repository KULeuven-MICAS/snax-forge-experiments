# TIERA1 R5d: the input slicer characterized and priced (2026-11-17)

R5 put a `FifoSlicer` (snax-forge D180) on every input narrower than its word; HWCOST named it in `char.missing` and the dynamic energy left it out. This round measures it as CHAR2 R4 measured the packer (X94) and prices it in snax-forge (D181). Block points only: no cluster synthesis of the sliced designs (snax-forge open item 142).

## What changed

- `char/points.json`: six points, `slice/d<d_in>_i<w>_n<outLanes>`, `words` 1: int32 with 1 lane (d_in 2), int16 with 1 and 2 (4, 2), int8 with 1, 2 and 4 (8, 4, 2). RTL from snax-forge `c5c7d3b` (`tiera1-r5e`), a later commit than the other 81 points' `0416557`; the catalogue blocks' RTL is the same at both.
- `sim/sources/char.py`, `sim/tb/char_slice_seq.sv`, `sim/pwr.py`: the TB kind `slice`. After the start (`clear`): `busy`, `enable` high, the input always valid, the output always ready, so a pop every cycle and a take every d_in cycles, a new word offered in the slot after each take; `idle`, `enable` low, the output not ready, the inputs held. `out_bits` is compared through `out_valid`: the data register has no reset, so it is X until the first take.
- `sim/ref.py`: the slicer's reference from its docstring, with `enable` and `clear` (`clear` wins over a take).
- `syn/syn.py`: `char_extra` takes the slicer's `outLanes` as `lanes`. The DC batch ran before that fix, so its six rows were written with `lanes` empty and set afterwards in `char_synth.csv` and `summary.csv`; no other row changed.
- `report/char_export.py`: FifoSlicer's params checked, `lanes_rule` `none`, its point's `flops` = 64 + ⌈log₂ d_in⌉ + 1 and `load_bits` = 64 / d_in + ⌈log₂ d_in⌉; `source.snax_forge_FifoSlicer` names its commit. `report/char_table.py`: a FifoSlicer section, one snax-forge commit per block (was one for every row). `char/checks.py`: the slicer's bits. `report/test_char_export.py`: the slicer's bits and entries.
- X94; X29 names it; `power.md`, "Char"; `results/char/README.md`.

## Found

Every row is `ok`: 0 mismatches and 0 X/Z at every sample against the reference, 128 pops in busy and none in idle. The points at one d_in are one netlist with the same energy whatever the type and lanes: a pop is 64 / d_in bits of the held word, the lanes only wiring. - **Bits.** DC's registers (`clock_gating.rpt`) equal 64 + ⌈log₂ d_in⌉ + 1 at every point: d_in 2, 64 gated (the word) and 2 ungated (the counter, the full flag) behind 1 gate, 67 sequential cells; d_in 4 and 8, the word and the counter gated, the full flag ungated, behind 2 gates, 69 and 70.
- **Energy.** A pop costs less the more pops share a word (0.73 to 0.36 pJ); a word costs 1.46 to 2.89 pJ through the slicer against 1.42 to 3.79 pJ through the packer. The model prices a pop at 1.11 to 1.30 of busy: the word's bits load at 11.2 to 14.4 fJ a loading bit in busy's `e_flop`, against e_load's 17.8 fJ (the accumulators', X37), most visible at d_in 2, where the word loads every other cycle. The packer's ratio is 0.90 to 1.13.
- **Area.** The model's area (comb + bits × 5.296 µm²) is 1.10 to 1.12 of DC's: the slicer's sequential area per cell is 4.53 µm² (its 64 data flops have no reset), below the mean cell the model uses.
- **Hold.** Idle costs 0.017 to 0.019 pJ a cycle (the clock gates, the ungated full flag, at d_in 2 the counter), about 4× n_bits × e_hold (0.0043 pJ), and at most 5 % of a pop.

## In snax-forge (D181)
