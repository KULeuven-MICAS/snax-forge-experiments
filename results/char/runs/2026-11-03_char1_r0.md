## Odd things

- **ElementwiseLanes alone is not byte-identical to its module inside, but only by two ports.** Alone its top keeps `input clock, reset` (unused: it has no flop); inside an accelerator firtool drops the unused ports of a private module. Its ALU files and the logic are identical, and the area is equal to 0.00 um2 on vecadd (both technologies). snax-forge's C§17 says the block alone is the module an accelerator holds "byte for byte, but where the accelerator ties a pin to a constant"; that sentence also needs "and a block without flops keeps its unused `clock` and `reset`". Not changed here (nothing goes back into snax-forge in this task).
- **SerialAccumulate alone is not above dot 5's and 6's on nangate45.** Expected above, for the same reason as TreeAccumulate against dot 4 (`first` tied in dot 5 and 6). On nangate45 the difference is −32.7 to +2.9 um2 (−3.05 % to +0.32 %), below at W = 4 and 8 against dot 5 and at W = 8 against dot 6; on nangate15 alone is above at every W, +16.2 um2 (+6.84 %) at W = 2 and +0.4 to +0.6 um2 (about +0.1 %) at W = 4 to 32. The RTL difference is small: SerialAccumulate's `first` only enters `first && lane == 0`, the one-bit clear of the adder's acc input, so tying it saves about one gate, not TreeAccumulate's 64-bit choice between beat and acc + beat. What is left is within DC's name and order noise for a few-hundred-um2 block (PITFALLS DC3), and dot 5 and dot 6 differ by up to 33.8 um2 from each other for the same identical RTL (`serial/sum_w4`: 1064.53 against 1030.75 on nangate45).
- **SerialAccumulate does have SYN2 counterparts at every width**: dot 5 and dot 6 hold `SerialAccumulate_i64_i64_n<W>` at W = 2 to 32 (the prompt listed its widths among the points without one). Only LinkBuffer at depth 2 to 16 has none.
- **Mac at W = 32 on nangate15 is 1.6 % below 32 copies of W = 1** (ratio 0.9843; 1.0024 at W = 8; 1.0003 and 1.0005 on nangate45). DC maps the 32 multipliers slightly better than one; the block is also 33.8 um2 (0.04 %) below the same module in dot 4 to 6. Every other lane series is within 0.1 % of linear.
- **ElementwiseLanes mul in dot 2 and dot 3 is 0.53 to 17 um2 below vecmul's on nangate45** (0.01 %), with the same RTL; alone matches vecmul to 0.00. DC noise (DC3).
- Seq area per flop is one constant per technology: 5.3200 um2 on nangate45 (`DFFR_X1`) and 1.2780 um2 on nangate15 (`DFFRNQ_X1`), every flop with its asynchronous reset pin used: in `tree/add_w8` on both technologies one `INV_X1` on `reset` drives the 64 active-low `RN` pins (an ideal net, so no buffer tree), counted in comb.
