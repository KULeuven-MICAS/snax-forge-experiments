# E2E1 A5b: the int8 packer at 2 and 4 lanes in (2026-11-28)

E2E1's narrow outputs (the MLP's int8 $a_l$; the image pipeline's uint8 Y, X, V, T) leave a unit through a `FifoPacker` when W lanes fill less than a 64-bit word: at int8, W = 1, 2 and 4, a packer of `inLanes` W and d_out = 8 / W. `char.json` held the packer at one lane in only (`pack/d2_i32`, `pack/d4_i16`, `pack/d8_i8`, X89), and snax-forge's lookup keys a packer exactly by (`inLanes`, `laneType`, `words`), so every E2E1 design at W = 2 or 4 priced its packer's comb part 0 (`char.missing`). This round measures int8 at 2 and 4 lanes in and rebuilds all five packers at snax-forge main, so FifoPacker has one commit of its own (`source.snax_forge_FifoPacker`, as X94's slicer); the three old points are the check. X89 is amended; no new X-number.

## What changed

- `char/points.json`: `pack/d4_i8` (`inLanes` 2, int8, `words` 1) and `pack/d2_i8` (`inLanes` 4), after `pack/d8_i8`. The names keep X89's `pack/d<d_out>_i<w>`; d_out · lanes in · w = 64 makes each unique.
- `results/char/nangate45/`: the five packers' rows in `char_synth.csv`, `char_synth_modules.csv`, `char_power.csv`, their `reports/` and `power_reports/` zips; `char.json`.
- `report/char_table.py`: FifoPacker's "How it scales" adds a line on the int8 points against their same-bit twins (area and idle energy compared, busy e_dyn side by side); `results/char/char_table.md` regenerated.
- X89 amended in place (FifoPacker one point per type and lanes in, int8 at 1, 2 and 4, made at a commit of its own); `results/char/README.md`: the model's packer bullet, `char.json`'s `source` keys, the points' naming paragraph (the packers a group of their own at `3b1e6af`, the narrow count 51 without them; 120 points in all).

### DC, gated

All five `ok`, 0 errors, slack 9.40 to 9.45 ns. `syn.py` prints no `flops` line for char points (that check is the streamer's and the interconnect's), so DC's `clock_gating.rpt` was read: registers 59, 50, 33, 50, 33, each the formula's; clock gates 8, 4, 1, 4, 1 (at d_out 2 the 1-bit counter ungated), so `seq_cells` 67, 54, 34, 54, 34.

The three old rows equal their committed rows in every number (area, comb, seq, buf/inv area, cells, seq_cells, the four slacks, DC's power and energy columns, leakage); only `date` and `snax_forge_commit` changed, and in the report zips only the report headers' dates. The two new points are identical to their same-bit twins in every DC number: `pack/d4_i8` to `pack/d4_i16`, `pack/d2_i8` to `pack/d2_i32`. A packer of n lanes of w bits holds the same bits as one lane of n · w, and DC builds the same netlist.

### Power

All 10 windows `ok`: 128 cycles each, 128 fires in busy and 0 in idle. The three old points' six rows equal their committed rows exactly (cycles, fires, every energy column; 0 % off); only `snax_forge_commit` changed.

The new points' idle energy equals their twins' (0.0196 and 0.0120 pJ a cycle, the clock gates'); their busy energy is a little above (`pack/d4_i8` 0.6354 against `pack/d4_i16` 0.6335 pJ, +0.3 %; `pack/d2_i8` 0.7408 against `pack/d2_i32` 0.7098 pJ, +4.4 %, of which e_comb 0.5226 against 0.4952). The hardware is the same; the stimulus is not: `rand64` draws one stream per input port (`in_bits_0` .. `in_bits_<n-1>`), so four 8-bit ports carry other data than one 32-bit port.
