# TIERA3 X1: the mixed-type Mac and multiplier measured (2026-11-24)

TIERA3 builds atax (y = Aᵀ(A x)) at int8 through `atax_types`: t = A x is int32, so half 2 multiplies A at int8 by t at int32 into int32. snax-forge's cost lookup (D176, `pair_params`) prices a block at its widest input, so an int8 × int32 Mac is priced as int32 × int32 (at W = 8, 17,197 µm² and 25.67 pJ of comb a fire, against int8 × int8's 3,416 µm² and 4.13 pJ), and the same for a SimdLanes mul with inputs int8 and int32 (atax_v1_2's half 2). This round measures both blocks at their real pair and puts each in `char.json` as its own entry (X98). snax-forge's side (`import-costs`, `pair_params` trying the exact pair first, D176 amended) is the next round's.

## What changed

- `char/points.json`: two points at the end, `mac/acc_w8_i8xi32_i32` (Mac, inTypes int8 and int32, op and out int32, lanes 8, accumulating, `regOut` and `seed` false) and `lanes/mul_w1_i8xi32_i32` (SimdLanes mul, 2 streams, the same types, lanes 1, k, k1, k2 none, the Csr flags false).
- `report/char_table.py`: `pair` stays the widest input and the output (the slope's w_in, the in-against-out comparisons); `in_widths`, `pair_key` and `pair_label` key a pair by both input widths where a and b differ, so a mixed pair sorts after int8's pairs, never with int32 × int32, and shows as `i8xi32`. `load` checks one snax-forge commit per block with the mixed pairs as a group of their own (`group`), `block_commits` names that group in the header. A section "Mixed input pairs" sets each mixed point beside its block at a × a and b × b.
- `report/char_export.py`: the mixed pairs' snax-forge commit as `source.snax_forge_mixed`, not as `snax_forge_<block>` (which would read as the commit of every Mac entry). The entries themselves needed no change: an entry was already keyed by its params, inTypes in full.
- `syn/syn.py`: `char_extra`'s `in_type` is `int8xint32` where a and b differ in width, the widest input otherwise (every earlier row unchanged). The DC run had loaded the old `char_extra`, so its two rows said `int32`; `in_type` of those two rows in `char_synth.csv` and `syn/runs/char/nangate45/summary.csv` was set to `int8xint32` by a one-off script (that field only). The power rows were written with the new code.
- `report/test_char_export.py`: `test_mixed_pairs_own_entries`, each mixed pair one entry at its exact inTypes beside the int32 × int32 one, `pair` equal and `pair_key` apart, its sort place, and `source.snax_forge_mixed`.
- X98; X29 names it; `results/char/README.md`; `char.json`, `char_table.md`.

The testbench and `sim/ref.py` needed no change (step 3): `sim/sources/char.py` takes each data port's width from the netlist and checks it against its own type (`port_bits`: a 8, b 32, out 32), `sim/pwr.py`'s `char_stimulus` keeps each port's own low bits of its rand64 stream and checks that every bit of every port toggles in busy, and `char_ref` casts a from `inTypes[0]` and b from `inTypes[1]` to the op type. A spot check (−128 × 3, 127 × −2³¹) gave the wrapped int32 for both blocks before the run; the run's 0 mismatches at every sample against the netlist are the check.

## Found

The SV tops are `Mac_i8xi32_i32_i32_n8_acc` and `SimdLanes_i8xi32_i32_i32_n1_mul` (with `ElementwiseAlu_w32_mul`), `io_a_*` 8 bits and `io_b_*` 32. SimdLanes sign-extends a into a 32-bit multiplier. DC: both `ok`, slack 6.73 and 7.17 ns. The Mac's 257 sequential cells are its W · 32 = 256 register bits and one clock gate, as its two neighbours; SimdLanes has none (`char/checks.py`: not as expected, none). Power: every row `ok`, every sample compared with 0 mismatches and 0 X/Z, 128 fires in busy and none in idle.

- **Between, as expected.** The Mac's comb is 7,690 µm² and 11.06 pJ a fire, inside the 3.4k–17k µm² and 4–26 pJ the brief expected, about a third of the way from int8 × int8 (0.31 in comb area, 0.32 in e_comb): 0.45 of the comb area and 0.43 of the e_comb it is priced at today.
- **The SimdLanes mul the same,** 833 µm² and 1.43 pJ, 0.35 and 0.33 of the way.
- **The register loads like int32's.** The Mac's e_flop is 4.61 pJ, int32 × int32's 4.61 rather than int8 × int8's 3.04: its 8 × 32 products fill the 32-bit sums, where int8 × int8's leave the upper bits copying the sign. Its busy e_bit (17.95 fJ) counts with the in = out points in `char_table.md`'s narrow-register paragraph, by its widest input, which moves that paragraph's range from 16.5–17.9 to 16.5–18.0 fJ. Idle is the one clock gate's share, 4.90 fJ a cycle, as the other two.
- **Today's price against the measured.** Priced as int32 × int32 at W = 8 the Mac's comb is 2.24 times the measured area and 2.32 times the e_comb; char.json's model for the new entry (e_comb + 256 bits × (e_load − e_hold)) gives 15.600 pJ a fire against 15.669 measured.

## char.json

`char-export`'s diff is the two new entries, one at the end of the SimdLanes list and one at the end of the Mac list, at their exact inTypes, and in `source` the added `snax_forge_mixed` (`90e9fba…`) and the restamped `snax_forge_experiments`; every other entry and constant is byte for byte the committed one (flop area 5.296378 µm², e_load 17.8153 fJ, e_hold 0.0655 fJ, counter factor 83.20; `bank` unchanged). The SimdLanes entry is `{"lanes": 1, "comb_um2": 833.112003, "e_comb_pj": 1.425924}`, the Mac's `{"lanes": 8, "comb_um2": 7690.060041, "e_comb_pj": 11.0557}`, both `copy` over lanes as their block. snax-forge's lookup today maps an int8 × int32 query to int32 × int32 before it looks, so it does not yet find these entries; it does not mistake them for the int32 × int32 ones either (their inTypes differ).

`source.snax_forge_experiments` names the last commit on the char CSVs, so the rows are committed first and `char.json` and `char_table.md` are exported again in a second commit, as in TIERA2 B6x.
