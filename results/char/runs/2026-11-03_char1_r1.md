**All 21 rows of the six points are `ok` on nangate45**: every output matches `sim/ref.py` at every falling edge (0 mismatches, 0 X/Z), no output changes in any idle window, every busy data bit toggles, X9's SAIF checks hold on every window (DURATION = cycles x 10 ns, `clock` TC = 2 x cycles, T0 + T1 + TX = DURATION on every net), PT annotates 100 % of the nets and no net keeps a wire capacitance or resistance (X25, PITFALLS PT1). ## The windows

Per kind (`sim/sources/char.py`, the sequencers): 5 cycles of asynchronous active-high reset, 4 settle slots with every input low, then per window one lead-in slot of the window's own kind and the window (128 cycles; serial's busy and busy_t1 are 128 beats of W = 8 cycles, its duty50 128 beats of 16 cycles):

| kind | busy | busy_t1 | idle | duty50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lanes | new a, b every cycle | - | inputs held | new data every other cycle |
| fold | fire and new data every cycle, first = 0 (the lead-in is the restart) | first on every fire | fire = 0, data and first held | fire every other cycle, first on the first |
| serial | read every cycle, lane 0 .. 7, a new beat at lane 0, first at lane 0 of the first beat | first at lane 0 of every beat | read = 0, lane and data held | read every other cycle |
| link | load and new data every cycle, wslot round the ring, rslot the slot written the cycle before | - | load = 0, held | load every other cycle |

A slot's inputs are applied at its rising edge (nonblocking, after the flops take the slot before), its outputs sampled at its falling edge, and the next rising edge takes it. A window runs from a quarter cycle before its first slot's rising edge to a quarter cycle before the rising edge after its last slot, so it holds its slots' 128 input changes, 128 captures of its own kind (the lead-in's and its own but the last, which falls on the next lead-in's edge, outside every window) and 256 clock edges, and no event at either end. The stimulus is X19's rand64 (seed 0, one stream per data input port in tb.json order).

- **Run 1** (the prompt's flow as written: inputs at the falling edge, windows back to back, one shared VCD, `vcd2saif -time t0 t1` per window). Idle was not quiet: ElementwiseLanes 0.028 pJ, Mac 4.27 pJ (+19 % over the expected 3.6).
- **Run 2: the VCD cut per window, and fold's idle holds `first`.** Two causes of run 1's idle:
  - **vcd2saif counts a phantom toggle.** A net whose last change before a window's t0 was a zero-width pulse (two or more changes in one time step that end where they began, which the zero-delay simulation makes at the time step where inputs change) gets one toggle in the window, however long before t0 the pulse was. On ElementwiseLanes' idle window 519 of 3586 nets have TC 1 while the VCD has no change at all between t0 and t1; every one of the 519, and no other net, is such a pulse (checked net by net; moving t0 later or t1 earlier does not remove it; duty50 carries the same 519 from the same pulses in busy). Fix: `sim/pwr.py` (`vcd_windows`) cuts the VCD into one file per window in one pass: the header, every variable's value at the last time step before t0 as plain value changes, the window's events, and a bare time step at t1 (vcd2saif ends a file's window at its last time step and starts a `$dumpvars` block's late: both tried, both cost 2.5 ns of DURATION). On the cut files TC equals the VCD's net toggles on every net of every window, and T0, T1, TX equal the shared VCD's SAIF on every net; DURATION and the clock's TC are exact. Each cut is deleted after its vcd2saif, the shared VCD after the cut (X18).
  - **Mac's idle:** 15808 nets toggled once or twice at idle's start: busy_t1 ends with first = 1, the first idle slot drove first = 0, and DC's adder input (`first ? 0 : s`) switched to s, so the 64-bit adders evaluated s + p once (about 78 pJ in one cycle). Fix: idle holds `first` (the prompt's fold idle is fire = 0, inputs held).
- **Final: inputs at the rising edge, one lead-in slot per window.** Run 2's busy TreeAccumulate was 5.963 pJ against 4.387 inside vsum on the same rand64 stream. Driving the inputs at the falling edge (run 1 and 2) made the inputs and the accumulator reach the adder half a cycle apart, two settled transitions per cycle in a zero-delay simulation where SYN1 and SYN2's TBs (and a block's neighbours inside an accelerator) change both at the same rising edge, one transition. Driven at the rising edge the same block gives 4.367 pJ (a scratch run of run 2's TB with only that change) and 4.380 pJ in the final batch: 0.998 of vsum's. A rising edge then carries the previous slot's capture and the next slot's input change, so back-to-back windows would give idle the last busy capture; the lead-in slot keeps each window to its own kind. Busy fell 21 % on Mac, 27 % on TreeAccumulate, 13 % on SerialAccumulate; ElementwiseLanes (no state) and LinkBuffer (out is a register read) moved by under 0.4 %.

## Odd things

- **`link/d4_w1`: duty50 (1.872 pJ) is below idle (1.879 pJ), and busy's flop group (1.375 pJ) below idle's (1.879 pJ).** Every one of its 256 flops has less internal power in busy than in idle (4.3 to 6.0e-7 W against 7.32 to 7.36e-7 W, a PT per-cell report on the kept SAIFs), including the three slots of four that a cycle does not write; switching is small. Its flops are `DFFS_X1` with Q unconnected (DC's sequential output inversion), and nangate45's flop internal power is conditioned on both outputs (`when : "!CK & RN & !Q & QN"`, ...). LinkBuffer d1 W = 8 and the folds, written every cycle, do not show it. Not resolved; it matters for R2's depth sweep (d2 to d16 write 1 slot in D).
- **PT's time-based mode does not agree with the averaged mode on flops with an output pin open.** A diagnostic on run 1's activity (VCDs regenerated in the scratchpad, PT `time_based` with `read_vcd -time` per window, `pt_power.tcl` otherwise unchanged): idle clock energy per flop is the same in both modes where every flop uses Q and QN (TreeAccumulate: 0.4573 against 0.4573 pJ), but where the flops have one output open, time-based gives about half: LinkBuffer d1 W = 8 (QN open) 1.895 against 3.659 pJ, d4 W = 1 (Q open) 1.008 against 1.879, SerialAccumulate (62 of 64 QN open) 0.207 against 0.458; Mac (504 of 512 both used) 3.610 against 3.660. The averaged mode gives about 7.15 fJ per flop whatever is connected, as it should if the clock pin's energy does not depend on whether QN drives a net, which points at time-based's handling of the open pin; the flow stays averaged (X7, X8). Both modes rest on the same library conditions on Q and QN, so this and the d4 point are one question for R2.
- **The phantom toggle may also touch SYN1 and SYN2's runs.** Their single window per run is cut from a shared VCD with `vcd2saif -time` after reset and the setup writes, so a net whose last change before the window was a zero-width pulse would carry one extra toggle over a window of 64 or more cycles; not checked here.
- ElementwiseLanes idle is exactly 0 (no flop, nothing toggles); its busy matches vecadd's module to 0.4 %.

## Decisions R1 made that need an X-number (R3)

1. **Windows, idle measured** (against X13's busy window only): per block, fixed windows of 128 cycles (busy, busy_t1 for folds and serial, idle, duty50) after 4 settle slots, each after one lead-in slot of its own kind outside every window; idle and duty50 are measured, for E_task = n_fire (E_busy) + n_idle (E_idle) and the duty check.
2. **128 cycles** per window (serial: 128 beats) against X19's 64 beats.
3. **The four testbench kinds** (lanes, fold, serial, link) and their pin patterns, including idle holding every input but the enable.
4. **The duty50 check** as a diagnostic, and what it found: an isolated fire costs more than a back-to-back one where the state feeds the logic.
5. **Inputs at the rising edge** (the convention SYN1 and SYN2's TBs and the accelerators have), outputs sampled at the falling edge.
6. **The VCD cut per window** in place of `vcd2saif -time` on the shared VCD, for the phantom toggle (a PITFALLS entry too).
7. **char_power.csv's columns** (the prompt's), reports per window, and `e_bit_pj` from `char_synth.csv`'s `seq_cells`.
