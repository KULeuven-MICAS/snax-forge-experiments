## What changed

- **The windows** (`sim/sources/char.py` `schedule()`, the four sequencers): lanes, fold and serial have `busy` and `idle` only, each after one lead-in slot (fold's busy lead-in is the restart; fold's idle holds every input, `first` included at busy's 0); LinkBuffer has `stream` only: a lead-in of D loads round the ring (the fill), then 128 cycles with a load of new data every cycle into slot t mod D and `rslot` the oldest slot, the same one, so `out` at cycle t is the beat loaded at t - D. `busy_t1` and `duty50` are gone from the schedules and sequencers. Everything else is R1's: inputs at the rising edge, outputs sampled at falling edges, the per-window VCD cut, rand64 seed 0.
- **`sim/ref.py`**: `char_ref` is driven by the schedule's pins, so it follows the stream pattern as is; `sim/pwr.py` checks it against the FIFO (every stream slot's expected output is the beat loaded D slots before) before simulating.
- **`sim/pwr.py`**: the idle gate applies where a point has an idle window; the toggle gate covers busy or stream. The hls and forge sources are unchanged in behaviour: tb.json, the generated `tb.sv` and every compile command of 121 hls and 4 forge points are byte-identical to the pre-R1 commit `be8b878`'s, checked after this round's last change.
- **`char/checks.py --power`**: R2's checks (below); R1's version is at `char1-r1`.
- **`results/char/<tech>/char_power.csv`**: R2's 47 rows only (R1's six points are in git at `char1-r1` and in R1's note); `power_reports/<point>/<window>/` for R2's windows.

## Decisions R2 made that need an X-number (R3)

1. **The model's windows**: busy and idle for ElementwiseLanes, Mac, TreeAccumulate and SerialAccumulate; stream only for LinkBuffer, a link not loading costing about its flops x e_hold; `busy_t1` and `duty50` dropped (R1's duty50 finding, an isolated fire costing 25 to 30 % more for the accumulators, a known limit of the model).
2. **The stream window**: a D-slot fill as its lead-in, then a load every cycle with `rslot` the oldest slot.
3. **e_hold**: the mean idle e_bit of the registered blocks, one number per technology.
4. **The depth sweep's expected slope** 64 e_hold and the 0.7 threshold for the diagnostic, and the open question it found on nangate45.
5. From R1 still open: the window and timing conventions (lead-in slot, inputs at the rising edge, the VCD cut, 128 cycles), now settled for both technologies.
