## The W-shape check

X(32, pair) measured against the int64 shape applied to the pair's W = 8 point, X(8, pair) × X(32, int64) / X(8, int64), from the CSVs; Ryan's bar is within 15 %.

| point | quantity | X(8, pair) | X(32, int64) | X(8, int64) | predicted | measured | measured / predicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tree/add_w32_i8_i32 | comb area um2 | 401.6600 | 8619.1981 | 2209.6620 | 1566.7496 | 1214.2900 | 0.775 |
| tree/add_w32_i8_i32 | busy e_comb pJ | 0.3926 | 11.3867 | 2.8944 | 1.5445 | 1.4799 | 0.958 |
| serial/sum_w32_i8_i32 | comb area um2 | 243.3900 | 2235.7301 | 821.9400 | 662.0366 | 441.8260 | 0.667 |
| serial/sum_w32_i8_i32 | busy e_comb pJ | 0.2940 | 1.2964 | 1.0340 | 0.3687 | 0.4320 | 1.172 |

Only TreeAccumulate's busy e_comb is within the bar. What the W series show (comb area, um2):

| block | type | W = 8 | W = 32 | per lane added (24 lanes) | per lane at int64 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TreeAccumulate | int8/int32 | 401.66 | 1214.29 | 33.86 | 267.06 | 0.127 |
| SerialAccumulate | int8/int32 | 243.39 | 441.83 | 8.27 | 58.91 | 0.140 |

A lane costs about its input's share of an int64 lane (8 / 64 = 0.125): the tree's added adders sit at 9 to 13 bits, the serial's lane mux at 8; the rest (the accumulator's adder and register at the output's 32 bits, the final widening) does not grow with W and is a larger share at int8/int32 W = 8 than at int64. Scaling the whole W = 8 point by the int64 W-shape therefore overstates the area at W = 32. An affine form per pair, a fixed part at the output width and a per-lane part at the input width, would follow these two points; that is R5's choice. The energy does not split as cleanly: the serial's busy e_comb grows by 0.138 pJ from W = 8 to 32 at int8/int32 against 0.262 at int64 (0.53, not 0.125), as the lane select's switching and the 32-bit adder's are not apart.
