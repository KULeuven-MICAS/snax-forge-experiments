# E2E1 A5: SimdLanes' requant, axpbypcz and threshold, and the halo link (2026-11-27)

E2E1 builds an int8 MLP and a gray-blur-threshold image pipeline in snax-forge. Phase A built their blocks on `ry/e2e1-blk`: three new options of `SimdLanes` (requant, axpbypcz, threshold) and the halo link `HaloLink` (snax-forge D206). `char.json` had no entry for any of them, so every E2E1 design raised `char.missing`. This round measures the five forms the workloads use at W = 1, gated, checks each at W = 8 against the copy rule, measures the halo link at W = 1, 8, 32, and exports the forms (X116). A threshold's k is held inside the data's range (X117). It adds no design points.

### The threshold's k (X117)

The first power run held X99's scalar, one full-width int16 value a window drawn after the data: `0xb804` (busy) and `0x4e04` (idle) at W = 1, `0xb1c6` and `0x3fd9` at W = 8. For a uint8 a, `a - k + 1` is then 18,429 or more, or −16,089 or less, so every lane's output was constant (255 through busy). A full-width k is outside 1 to 256 in 99.6 % of draws. Drawn after the data, the two points also held different values, and the W = 8 energy came out at 0.62 of its copy rule (0.2862 against 0.4613 pJ). X117 holds k uniform over 1 to 255 from its own generator (`default_rng([0, 1])`): both points hold 134 in busy and 227 in idle, the output takes 0 and 255, and W = 8 lands at 0.917. The threshold's DC rows did not change; its W = 1 energy a fire went from 0.0577 to 0.1210 pJ.

Its comb area is 0.532 µm² a flop at every W: one `INV_X1` on each flop's reset (`DFFR_X1`'s RN is active low, the block's reset active high), as LinkBuffer at depth 1 has (34.048 µm² for 64 flops). Its stream e_comb is 0: the taps are wires and the load a clock gate. The choice (X116): the halo link gets no `char.json` entry. snax-forge's lookup returns comb 0, energy 0, found `wires` for a `HaloLink`, and its flops are priced from the catalogue as every block's; the reset inverters are left out as they are a flop's, not the link's.
