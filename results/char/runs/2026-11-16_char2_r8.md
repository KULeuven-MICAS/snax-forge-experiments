- The narrow energy is high where a block's output feeds another unit (dot 3's mul and sum 1.38 to 1.42, dot 4 and dot 5's Mac 1.36 at i8, vsum 1.25 to 1.44) and close where it leaves the accelerator (vecmul 0.94 at i8, 0.99 at i16): a block alone drives its output pins, whose sign-copied upper bits toggle with the data, more than an internal net.
- The packer: the model prices one push a result, 0.0009 to 0.0014 nJ; inside a design it costs 0.003 to 0.051 nJ, as it sees the unit before it change every cycle (as a sum fed by another unit); at most 3.7 % of a narrow kernel (dot 3 at i8, W = 8).
- `cluster-breakdown`, `cluster-energy` and `cluster-sweep` no longer reproduce their committed files: they read the forge points' ungated `alone` rows, which CG5 removed, so a rerun drops the `alone` section and `cluster-sweep` stops on it.
