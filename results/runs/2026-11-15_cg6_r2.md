Readings:

- vecadd and matadd, and vsum and rowsum but for Vitis's dace case, are the same designs (equal areas, ungated and gated); their $r_E$ differ only where their tasks do.
- Vitis's rowsum dace builds its 16-word buffer from flops (`--with-ram`), the one place where gating replaces write-enable muxes on a whole array: area 0.839, energy 0.530. The two macro points barely move in area (the macro is not gated: 0.997 and 0.999).
- Bambu's area falls most (median 0.83), its energy least of the three moves (median 0.99, relu W = 1 at 1.010).
- XLS has 32 registers in every design (33 sequential cells gated: the 32 and one ICG, `clock_gating.rpt`), the rest combinational, so gating has little to act on: area within −7 to +1 %, energy −4 to +6 %, the ICG costing more than it saves on the smallest designs (relu W = 1, +5.7 %).
