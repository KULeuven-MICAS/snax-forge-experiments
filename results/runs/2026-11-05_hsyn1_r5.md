Notes on the spec:

- Bambu's streams are `<arg>_TDATA/_TVALID/_TREADY` (`A_TDATA`, `out_TDATA`, `y_out_TDATA`), not `p_<arg>_...`: the `p_` prefix is on the arguments inside Bambu's top, not on its ports. `bambu.py` takes `<arg>_` and falls back to `p_<arg>_`.
- XLS's channels are named after the DSLX args (dot: `a`, `b`, `res`); `xls.py` maps them by position to hls-exercises' `axis` (`A`, `B`, `out`), which `sim/ref.py` and the plusargs use. Mapping by the `EXP_` names in dslx-exercises' params.json would fail on axpy (`y_out` is `EXP_y`).
- The random start: Questa 2022.4 splits it between `vopt +initreg=<spec>` (no value: prepare for randomization) and `vsim +initreg+<seed>` (vopt -help, vsim -help all). Spec `ru`: 4-state variables (nangate45's flops are behavioral `output reg Q, QN`) and sequential UDPs (nangate15's flops). No spec would also randomize the TB's 2-state flags and counters (PITFALLS Q9). The runs are tagged `_r<N>` and write nothing to `results/`.

## Checks before the runs

- `tb.json`: `done/dot/bambu_w8`, top `p_Z3dotPK6ac_intILi512ELb0EES2_Pxjj`, streams A, B (8 lanes), out (1 lane), pins `loop_count`, `len` (32 bits), every port covered (`idle_port` covered, not read); sizes n64 (8), n512 (64), b64 (64), n2048 (256). `done/dot/xls_w16`, top `dot`, cfg 64 bits: `loop_count` [63:32], `len` [31:0], channels `_a`, `_b` (16 lanes) as A, B and `_res` (1 lane) as out; sizes n64 (4), n512 (32), b64 (64), n2048 (128). axpy's 96-bit packing checked by hand (`loop_count` 4, `alpha` -1: `4ffffffffffffffff`, `loop_count` in [95:64] as dslx-exercises' `tb/axpy_tb.sv`).
- Expected cycles: Bambu dot W = 8, `cycles_done` = 2 x loop_count + 2 (n64 18, n512 130; b64 130, n2048 514); XLS dot W = 16, `cycles` = loop_count, so the window loop_count + 1 (n64 5, n512 33, b64 65, n2048 129).
- PITFALLS Q7 (the generated tops compiled into a scratch folder, the handshake outputs examined at 2, 12 and 52 ns, reset released at 45 ns): with `+initreg+0` neither tool raises an output TVALID in reset; XLS holds every `_TREADY` low in reset and `_cfg_TREADY` low until cfg is valid; Bambu holds `idle_port` at 1. With the random start, Bambu on nangate15 drives `out_TVALID` = 1 before the first clock edge (a flop's random value), cleared at the first edge. The sinks count beats only once `rst_n` is 1, so neither case counts.
- The random start took: the outputs fed by unreset registers start random (Bambu `out_TDATA` `10c0e48981e32dca` on nangate45, `65f0903c8a65833a` on nangate15; XLS `_res_TDATA` random before the first edge, then 0 from its reset), against 0 with `+initreg+0`. With `+initreg+0`, XLS on nangate45 shows `_res_TDATA` = `0000200000000000` before the first edge and 0 after: nangate45's flops have separate Q and QN regs, both 0 (most likely; PITFALLS Q8). Harmless: every TB clocks 5 cycles of reset before dumping.
