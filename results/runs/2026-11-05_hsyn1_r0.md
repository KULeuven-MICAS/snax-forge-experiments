# Run note: HSYN1 R0, Vitis opt W = 8 at 3.33 ns after BAM3's source change, 5 Nov 2026

hls-exercises 542fa32 (BAM3) declared the output beat `= 0` in the five elementwise kernel sources that Vitis's opt case shares with Bambu. This round reruns Vitis on the committed opt_w8 points (from e1427cc, 27 Oct, before the change) at the old 3.33 ns clock, to check that the change leaves the Verilog as it was before R2 rebuilds the points at 10 ns. dace uses its own sources and is not checked.

**Four of the five are identical apart from `CORE_GENERATION_INFO`; relu's differs only by one renamed wire.** csim, cosim, cycles and II are unchanged for all five.
