# Run note: ARC1 R1, outputs zipped one per point, 6 Nov 2026

Ryan's home was over its file-count quota (R0: 1,676,657 of 1,600,000 soft, folders count; R0b cleared the caches outside the repos to 1,347,610). This round makes the repo keep every point's bulky outputs as one zip, with every flow reading from and writing to the zips through `arc/arc.py` (X51, `CLAUDE.md` "Files are zipped"), and converts what was on disk. Run on the MICAS server (micas-lapserv1, 32 cores).

**The repo went from 149,561 to 37,003 inodes (du), its outputs from 116,062 to about 2,500; quota 1,347,610 to 1,237,646. 6,132 points converted, none kept back. Every committed number comes back from the zips:** the 424 synth rows and 8,962 module rows recomputed from the DC zips, the 1,124 ok power rows recomputed from the PT reports in the power zips, and the 424 committed report zips byte for byte what a rerun writes; the reruns of step 4 give every committed CSV cell back but dates, and `power_pt.csv`, `power_pt_modules.csv`, `char_power.csv` and `ram_table.csv` byte for byte. Three findings, none from the zips (PITFALLS DC11, E2, LIB7): the forge and char synth CSVs predate X40's columns, a forge power rerun needs snax-forge at the bundle's commit, and lc_shell cannot write a `.db` back to Liberty.

**Finding (DC11).** `results/forge/<tech>/synth_dc.csv` (SYN2) and `results/char/<tech>/char_synth.csv` (CHAR1) were committed before X40's `slack_<class>_ns` columns (HSYN1); any `syn` rerun of one of their points rewrites the whole file with the four columns, empty on the rows not rerun. Not from the zips: `syn.py` at `4cb1f87a` writes them too.

**Finding (E2).** `pwr run --source forge` asks snax-forge (`forge/query.py point`) to load the design point the bundle names; snax-forge from DRY1-2 (#52, on `main` and on the DRY1-3 branch alike) indexes its BRM library by family and stops with `no BRM 'elementwise_add' in the library (['accumulate', 'mac', 'simd'])`. With snax-forge at the bundles' commit `f9764d5` (same pixi lock) the point runs and gives the committed row byte for byte.

**Finding (LIB7).** `write_lib -format lib` after `read_db` stops with `Error: Invalid output format 'lib'. (UIL-23)`; my first comparison of the two `.db` diffed two missing files and printed "equal". `report_lib` compares them (173 lines each, the `Date` line apart).
