## Leakage in the tables (X101)

| Table | Points | Leakage per area (µW/µm²) |
|---|---:|---|
| char blocks | 105 | 0.016–0.026, median 0.019 |
| `streamer.csv` | 168 | 0.0180–0.0190 |
| `xbar.csv` | 27 | 0.0242–0.0291 |

A flop leaks 0.083648 µW: the mean over the 21 int64 points with sequential cells of (`leak_reg_uw` + `leak_clk_uw` + `leak_seq_uw`) / `seq_cells`, 0.0816 to 0.0845 (range 3.5 %), as `flop.area_um2` is the mean of `seq_area_um2` / `seq_cells`. A block's leakage, $P_{comb} + n_{bits} P_{flop}$ with $P_{comb}$ by the entry's lanes rule, against DC's `leak_power_uw` over the 87 block points that are not stream blocks or check points: median −0.09 %, the largest −10.2 % (`link/d4_w1_i8`, its int8 LinkBuffer read from the int64 entry scaled by width), then −5.9 % (`serial/sum_w32_i8_i32`); every other point within 2 %.

At 10 ns, 1 µW of leakage is 0.01 pJ a cycle.

| `dma` per burst, less its floor (pJ) | 72.32 | 76.13 |

`e_burst_pj` = 106.40 pJ, the mean. With D184's accesses at lstp (8 bank reads and writes, 78.71 pJ, and the crossbar's 3.29 pJ), a burst costs about 188 pJ. D184's bank price itself moved with R1's table: 322.41 nJ for the copy, from 327.87 at hp.

- The leakage is PrimePower's per instance in the window (the groups add up to the top's 89,757 µW to 1e-6), but the I-cache macros', taken from the lstp table (2 × 0.1347 + 2 × 0.0254 µW) in place of the reports' hp 5,134.4 µW.
- The fixed parts leak 6.27 mW, 62.7 pJ a cycle at 10 ns, as estimated in the plan from area × 0.018 µW/µm² (62); the idle floor is 23.6 pJ a cycle (24 in the plan). Together about 86 pJ a cycle.
- Their area, 390,555 µm², equals `breakdown.json`'s `not_costed_um2` for the point.
- The TCDM bank logic has no floor (2.1 pJ a cycle idle, 16.0 in this run window): it moves with L1 traffic.
