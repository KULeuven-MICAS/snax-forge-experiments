# Run note: CG5 R2, forge's and xbar_mix's gated rows read against the ungated ones, made the only rows, and the block tables exported from them, 16 Nov 2026

R1 (`2026-11-16_cg5_r1.md`) added clock-gated rows beside the ungated ones for the 78 forge accelerators (synthesis; power for 71) and the 27 `xbar_mix` grid points. This round, in the chat, reads total area and total energy gated over ungated, removes the ungated rows and zips of forge, `xbar_mix` and the char blocks (X79 · X87 · X88), and exports the block tables from the gated rows only: `char.json` (X37, X83 · X85) and `xbar_kinds.json` (X71). No tool ran; everything here reads committed files.

**Gating saves little on forge's accelerators: median $r_A$ 0.97 over 78 points and median $r_E$ 0.97 over 71, the most on the reductions (vsum and rowsum, $r_E$ 0.77 to 0.90) and the least where a multiplier sets the cost (vecmul 0.99 to 1.00; axpy and dot 2 to 5 at W >= 8, 0.98 to 1.00). Every source is gated; no ungated row is left.**

- Over all points, $r_A$ runs from 0.82 (rowsum forge2, W = 16) to 1.00, median 0.97 (78 points); $r_E$ from 0.77 (vsum, W = 1 and 2) to 1.00, median 0.97 (71 points).
- Both ratios rise toward 1 with W wherever the datapath grows with W: the control, csr and sum registers gating saves on are fixed, and the arithmetic, which gating does not touch, grows. rowsum forge2's area is the exception, its saving growing with W (0.88 to 0.82).
- The reductions save the most energy (vsum and rowsum, 0.77 to 0.90), the multiplier kernels the least (vecmul 0.99 to 1.00).
- As in CG6, inside a busy task window gating moves area and energy by a few percent; the large savings are where registers hold (CG3's idle cluster windows).

The control factor is X37's mean of `ctrl/ctrl`'s energy per cycle over its cells times e_hold, now on the gated rows: e_hold 0.0655 fJ per sequential cell (CG2's gated blocks), cells `seq_um2` / 5.296378 µm² rounded (a clock gate is a sequential cell, X83), over the tier-done points with a gated power row. snax-forge reads only $f_{ctrl}\thinspace e_{hold}$: 5.43 fJ per cell per cycle, against 7.58 fJ per flop ungated (1.06 x 7.1476). The factor spreads 3 to 1 between points: a gated control unit's energy follows its counters' activity more than its cell count, so the per-design ratio of control in `char_table.md` runs 0.57 (vecmul) to 1.62 (dot 5) at W = 8, where ungated it ran 0.93 to 1.03. Control stays a small share of a kernel (0.4 % of vecmul's energy, 8 % of vsum's), so the kernel lands within 0.996 to 1.035.
