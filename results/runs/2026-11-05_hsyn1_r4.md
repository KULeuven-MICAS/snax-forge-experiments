# Run note: HSYN1 R4, the synthesis probe, one point per HLS tool on both technologies, 5 Nov 2026

DC on three points, one per HLS tool, on nangate45 and nangate15 at the 10 ns ASIC clock, to check R3's code (the bambu and xls sources, the port guard, the path-class slacks, Bambu's split) on real netlists before the full tiers: `done/dot/xls_w16` (XLS's deepest one-stage path in scope), `done/dot/bambu_w8` (Bambu's II = 2 point) and `done/dot/opt_w8` (Vitis at a 10 ns HLS clock, R2). Synthesis only; power comes in R5.

**All six runs ok, every check passes, nothing to fix in `syn/`.** Timing met everywhere; the four class slacks are filled (every class has a path) and the smallest equals `slack_ns` in all six; Bambu's split sums to the total; the flop counts match the RTL (Bambu after DC's removal of 96 unloaded bits). Two new PITFALLS entries, DC8 and DC9.

**Path classes.** The smallest of the four equals `slack_ns` to the report's digits in all six (nangate15's `slack_ns` has one digit more: 9.101714 against 9.10171). No worst path starts at a reset port, so leaving the reset out of the classes changed nothing here. XLS's in2out path (`_a_TDATA` → `_res_TDATA`, the one-stage datapath) has 3.43 ns slack on nangate45, but its worst path is reg2reg (`____state_0` → `____state_3`, the sum's count into the accumulator, 2.38 ns). Vitis at 10 ns is limited by in2reg: with no operand registers, the eight 64-bit multipliers and the adder tree sit between `B_TDATA` and the partial-sum registers.

**Bambu's split.** DC kept the instances `PBI_Z3dotPK6ac_intILi512ELb0EES2_Pxjj_i0/Controller_i` (design `controller_Z3dotPK6ac_intILi512ELb0EES2_Pxjj`) and `PBI_Z3dotPK6ac_intILi512ELb0EES2_Pxjj_i0/Datapath_i` (`datapath_Z3dotPK6ac_intILi512ELb0EES2_Pxjj`), one of each. ctrl + datapath + glue = area: 81.3960 + 66287.7321 + 5.3200 = 66374.4481 (nangate45), 21.8235 + 20662.9113 + 1.2780 = 20686.0128 (nangate15), equal to `area_um2` at 4 decimals. The glue is one flop: `PBI_..._i0/done_delayed_REG` (`register_AR`, 5.32 um2 and 1.278 um2), the only cell outside the two instances. The controller is 0.12 % of the area; the datapath holds the stream modules (8.8 um2 each), the multipliers and the beat registers.

**Flops.**

- XLS: 160 = dslx-exercises `notes/xls.md`'s proc state for dot (`left` 32, `len` 32, `k` 32, `acc` 64).
- Bambu: the `register_*` instances in `rtl/done/dot/bambu_w8/dot.v` sum to 1347 bits (`register_SE` 1346: two 512-bit beat registers `reg_4`, `reg_6`, two 64-bit `reg_9`, `reg_10`, six of 32 bits, two of 1; `register_AR` 1). With the controller's 4-bit `present_state`, the three stream modules' `started` flags and the two `SIMPLEJOIN_FU`s (3 bits each), the RTL has 1360 flop bits. DC removed 96 (OPT-1207, unique lines): `reg_0`, `reg_3`, `reg_5`, 32 bits each. `reg_3` and `reg_5` drive the address input of the A and B AXI-stream read modules, which a stream ignores; `reg_0` only feeds its own increment and select. 1360 - 96 = 1264, on both technologies (PITFALLS DC9).
- Vitis: 328 on both technologies, against 1868 at 3.33 ns (`git show e8c4e91:results/hls/<tech>/synth_dc.csv`, both technologies). The flop bits of the Verilog (every `<=` target's declared width) are 1868 for the 3.33 ns `dot.v` (hls-exercises `cbc2541`) and 328 for the 10 ns one, so DC keeps every bit. The 1540 that went: the sixteen 64-bit operand registers (`temp_*`, 1024 bits), the eight 64-bit products (`mul_ln21_*_reg`, 512) and 4 pipeline bits (`ap_enable_reg_pp0_iter2`, `iter3`, `icmp_ln22`, `or_ln22` at iter 2). That is R2's pipeline change for `done/dot/opt_w8`: `BEAT`'s iteration latency 4 → 2, cycles 12 → 10 at n64 (`2026-11-05_hsyn1_r2.md`). The 10 ns design keeps the two 64-bit partial sums (`add_ln21_2`, `add_ln21_3`), `acc` (64), the counters and the latched pins (128) and 8 control bits. Area at 10 ns: 60079 against 69955 um2 at 3.33 ns on nangate45.

**check_design** (`logs/check_design.log`, before compile; the same counts on both technologies):

- Vitis `opt_w8`: no warnings.
- XLS `xls_w16`: one, `output port '_a_TREADY' is connected directly to output port '_b_TREADY'` (LINT-31): the two input streams share one ready. The underscore-led port names give no warning.
- Bambu `bambu_w8`: 4851, all from types Vitis does not have: LINT-28 unconnected ports (2126), LINT-29 feedthrough (1223), LINT-31 shorted outputs (626), LINT-52 constant outputs (620), LINT-32 submodule pins tied to 0 or 1 (231), LINT-33 a net on several pins of one cell (24), LINT-2 an unloaded net (1, the write module's `null_out_signal` output). They come from Bambu's generic library modules (unused bit slices of parameterized ports, constant selector inputs) and are not errors. None names `idle_port`.

In `dc_shell.log`, warning types beyond Vitis's (UID-401, one per input with a driving cell; VER-281, VER-318, VER-61, VO-4): Bambu has VER-708 (5, initializers ignored: PITFALLS DC8), VER-944 (12, unnamed generate blocks in `panda_libtech.v`) and TIM-134 (1 net with fanout over 1000; the forge points with more than 1000 flops have it too and those under do not, so most likely the ideal clock net, not confirmed). XLS has no type that Vitis lacks.

## PITFALLS

- DC8 (new): Bambu's declaration initializers (`reg x = 0`, `present_state = S_9`) are ignored by DC (VER-708); the beat registers have no reset, so gate-level simulation needs `+initreg+0` as for Vitis (Q1).
- DC9 (new): a Bambu point's `seq_cells` is below its register instances' bits because DC removes unloaded registers (OPT-1207): compare after subtracting them.
