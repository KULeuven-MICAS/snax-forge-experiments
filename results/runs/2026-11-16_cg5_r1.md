## Power: two passes

- **First pass (16 Nov 00:18, superseded).** snax-forge was on `ry/pack1a` `ac08369`, where PACK1a R2 (`84455a2`) made the axpy and matadd kernels compute at `ACC = int32`. `forge/query.py ref` runs the checkout's kernel body, so `pwr.py`'s reference check failed at all 15 untiered axpy (`forge_w*`, `forge_baked_w*`) and matadd points: `sim/ref.py differs from snax-forge's _kernel_axpy.axpy: {'y': False}` (matadd: `{'C': False}`). 56 were ok, 22 failed (those 15 and the 7 below). Its tb.json failure rows had empty `clk_gate` and `size`, so they read as 7 extra ungated rows (78 to 85). Nothing of it was committed: the two power CSVs were restored to HEAD and the second pass rewrote the `_cg` runs and report zips. PITFALLS E8.
- **The fix** (`917cfa7c`, `pwr.py: a tb.json failure row keyed like every other row`): `_do_point`'s tb.json failure row gets `clock_ns`, `clk_gate` and, where every run of the invocation names the same one, its size and seed, so it keys as `write_results` keys a run (point, clk_gate, size, seed). The chat asked for clock_ns, clk_gate and size; the seed was added as well, since with an empty seed the row would still sit under a key of its own and a later ok run would not replace it. Test: `syn/test_clk_gate.py` `test_pwr_tb_failure_row_keyed_like_a_run`.
- **Second pass (16 Nov 08:40, the rows committed).** snax-forge on main `169ca26`: 71 ok, 7 failed as expected.
