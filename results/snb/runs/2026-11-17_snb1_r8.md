## Sizing R10

From the pilot, one core, the netlist's 855 171 `$var`:

- VCD: 707 MB for the simulation's 71 244 testbench cycles to the check mark, about 9.9 kB per cycle. The other configs' netlists are larger (87 and 104 MB against 70 MB), so expect more per cycle there, scaled by their nets.
- Part 1 time per cycle: the run with the VCD 45.5 s (0.64 ms per cycle, against 30 s with no VCD), the cut 83 s (1.2 ms per cycle, 117 s per GB), vcd2saif 0.12 ms per cycle of a window; plus about 95 s of setup and netlist build per run.
- PT: 74 s for the 70 327 cycles of the three windows, 1.05 ms per cycle; but its time goes with the windows, not the cycles: about 27 s of link and setup, then 14 to 19 s per window (a SAIF is about 115 MB for 51 cycles as for 69 695).
- Disk: the VCD and the SAIFs, 1.4 GB at the peak, gone at the end. The longest int64 run (`snitch1_cg_matvec_int64`, 270 612 cycles) would write a VCD of about 2.7 GB.
