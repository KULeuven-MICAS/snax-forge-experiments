# Run note: HCMP1 R8a, the power flow for tier A's programs and the HLS streams entries, 28 Nov 2026

R8a makes the power flow drive what R8b measures at X118's spec (forge's `i8`, W = 8, full-range data, `cycles_ports`): forge's tier A forms as their programs (several jobs, groups or accelerators, chains, forks, ROWS, int32 scalars), the HLS ports that follow hls-exercises' `params.json` `streams` entries, and XLS's cfg widths from the kernel's types (X120).

**Result:** the one-task points' `tb.json` are byte for byte as committed (13 points of R4b, forge, Vitis, Bambu and XLS, and 3 int64 points). Both check points pass end to end: XLS `a/mvt/xls_w8` and forge's mvt_v6 (`a/mvt/forge6nm_w8_i8_m64_n64`) give every output equal to snax-forge's kernel; XLS's `cycles_ports` is 512, the HLS testbench's 512; mvt_v6's is 515, its BUSY_COUNTER 515, equal to snax-forge's `group_counter` and to the cluster RTL's 515 (snax-forge STATUS.md), SNAX-GVSoC's 519 at this spec. The dry check builds `tb.json`, stimulus and reference for all 24 HLS points and 15 of 17 forge forms; matvec_col's two forge forms have no bundle, refused by snax-forge's design check at int8. Three multi-job programs ran on their bundles' RTL with the new sequencer: every output exact, every job's BUSY_COUNTER its `group_counter`.

## Step 1: forge's multi-engine forms

- **`forge/query.py program`** now walks snax-forge's task list as the C backend's `_Program.run` does (default build): per accelerator task a job (`job_writes` with the group's chains: `JOBS_<SINK>`, JOB_BEATS, ROWS, CARRY; the scalar slots with their CSR defines; `group_counter` with the forks, as `gen/rtl.py`'s `vectors` calls it), the ops in program order (write, scalar, start, wait where `placed_waits` puts one on a SNAX component, a BUSY_COUNTER read for each task a wait ends, the DMA tasks' places), each streamer task's stream with its job (its container from the addresses it visits: gesummv_v2_1_1's one matvec group reads A, then B), and the DMA tasks with an L1 copy's containers. It refuses a program whose accelerator CSR accesses differ from the bundle's `main.c` (define and value, in order): every bundle queried this round passes (20: the 15 dry-check forms, mvt_v6 and R4b's four). A set sharing its movers (D169) is refused. `point` lists a fork's every port. New `run`: snax-forge's executor (`dfg/execute.py`) on the design point's graph, every container's final contents, transients included.
- **`sim/sources/forge.py`** builds as before a point of one accelerator task of one group writing only JOBS and JOB_BEATS (`simple`), now with int32 scalars (one CSR word; axpy's alpha at i8), and every other point as its program (`build_program`): `control.groups`, one stream per wrapper port (a fork one port) carrying its segments in program order (`x1_in` / `x1_out` for an in/out pair, `A_vmul_x1` / `A_mac_x2` for one container on two ports), `scalars` by job, `sizes.<tag>.program` and `left_out` (the DMA tasks). A segment reads the container's initial contents where no earlier job or copy wrote its elements, the graph's final ones where one did; a read between two writes of an element, or a write written again later, is refused. The shape is the point's sets that are the kernel's symbols (mvt_v6's `R=8` is a recipe param).
- **The TB.** `sim/tb/csr_prog_seq.sv` runs the program: writes held by a busy group as the hardware holds them, START per job, a wait until the group's done pulses equal its starts, the BUSY_COUNTER reads where the program has them (`TB: counter <job> <value>`). The window opens at the first START and closes at the last run's end; `cycles_ports` over every stream as before. `sim/pwr.py` checks each job's counter against its `group_counter` and the window against the program's expected cycles (each job's `group_counter` in program order, a wait ending its group's jobs), both within 5 %.
- **The data.** Inputs drawn full-range at their dtype from seed 0, container by container in stream order; snax-forge's executor gives every other container; its outputs must equal the kernel's (`ref_check`: the executor and `_kernel_<k>.<k>`, equal on every form).
- **The DMA left out.** mvt_v4_1_1's and atax_v1_2's `transpose_A_T` (an L1 copy between the jobs) is not run: the stream reading A_T carries the graph's A_T (the transpose node's output), the copy's cycles and energy are §C's. The L2 loads and stores are left out as for every forge point.
- **The one-group points' tb.json.** `pwr tbjson --source forge --clk-gate` against the `tb.json` committed in the power zips: `done/dot/forge3_w8_i8_n4096`, `done/dot/forge3nm_w8_i8_n4096`, `done/vecadd/forge_w8_i8_n4096`, `untiered/relu/forge_w8_i8_n4096`: identical.
- **`forge/bundles.py`**: a point's `w_param` names a recipe's width param when it is not `W` (mvt_v4_1_1 and mvt_v5 take `W_x1`; `--set W=8` was refused by the sandbox).

## Step 2: the HLS streams

- **The element.** At a narrow point a stream's dtype is snax-forge's dtype of the array the port carries (its `streams` entry's `array`, else its name; an output `<a>_out` that is no container carries `<a>`: axpy's `y_out` is `y`). This amends X119's rule (inputs at `dtype`, outputs at `acc_dtype`), which gives int8 for axpy's `y` and mvt's `x1_in` and `x2_in`, int32 in the RTL (256, 32 and 256 bits); `narrow_ref` checks each stream's array.
- **`streams` entries.** A port with one gets `follows` (array, beats, index) and `tb.json` the size's `dims`; rand64 draws each array once, in the order the input ports first carry it, and gives lane l of beat b element `index` of its array, the outputs from snax-forge's kernel at the point's types and symbols (M, N). A port without an entry carries its array in turn. No `b64` for a kernel with entries. Scalar pins (Vitis, Bambu) are drawn at their dtype (int32 at i8) and given to the reference; `hls.ports_of` reads an ANSI header (XLS's Verilog, snax-forge's SV).
- **The level 1 and dot points' tb.json.** Against the committed ones: Vitis `done/vecadd/opt_w8`, `untiered/relu/opt_w8`, `done/dot/opt_w8`; Bambu and XLS the same three; int64 `untiered/rowsum/bambu_w8`, `untiered/matadd/xls_w8`, `untiered/rowsum/xls_w4`: all identical. The mvt stimulus was checked against NumPy (`x1 + A y1`, `x2 + Aᵀ y2` at int32) on both paths.

## Step 3: XLS's cfg widths

`CFG_BITS` keeps loop_count and len at 32 bits; a scalar field takes its forge container's dtype (int32 at i8, 64 bits at an int64 point, as before), drawn at it; the sum is checked against `_cfg_TDATA`'s width: axpy {loop_count, alpha} 32 + 32 = 64, gesummv {loop_count, len, alpha, beta} 4 × 32 = 128.
